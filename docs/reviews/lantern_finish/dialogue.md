# A Lantern of His Own — dialogue

Captured through the production game from the shipped PR3 endpoint.

## Finish Invitation

**Liam:** Can we go and finish it now?

**Caelan:** Your lantern?

**Liam:** Mum hasn't seen the frame yet. I want the paper on when I show her.

**Tomas:** It's all waiting at home.

**Mother Serene:** Then I shall let you get back to it.

**Liam:** Will you still be here later?

**Mother Serene:** For a while. If you've more to ask, you know where to find me.

**Liam:** I want to show you the stairs when they're done.

**Mother Serene:** I'd like that.

**Caelan:** Thank you again.

**Mother Serene:** You're welcome. Enjoy the rest of your afternoon.

**Tomas:** Come on, then. Your father will be wondering how we got on.

**Liam:** I'll tell him. I remember what she said.

**Caelan:** All right. We'll walk back together.

## Home Return

**Liam’s father:** Back again. Was Serene able to give you some time?

**Caelan:** She was. Thank you for keeping everything here.

**Liam’s father:** It's just where you left it.

**Liam:** Daisy didn't get the paper?

**Liam’s father:** No. Your drawing's still beside it, too.

**Liam:** Good. I want this piece.

**Tomas:** The one with the bigger steps?

**Liam:** Yes. There's room for the window on that one.

**Liam’s father:** Then make yourselves comfortable. Shall we clear a little space for the work?

**Caelan:** We can keep the practice pieces together at this end.

**Liam:** Don't throw the first one away.

**Caelan:** I won't.

**Liam’s father:** You can keep all of them. We only need room for the piece you're using.

**Tomas:** And the frame.

**Liam:** I know. I haven't forgotten how big it is.

**Caelan:** Let's look at the drawing again.

## Purpose

**Liam:** Caelan?

**Caelan:** Yes?

**Liam:** Elira carries one for her husband.

**Caelan:** Yes.

**Liam:** What about someone who hasn't got anybody there?

**Caelan:** At the gathering?

**Liam:** Nobody to carry theirs.

**Tomas:** Were you thinking of someone?

**Liam:** I don't know anyone like that. But there might be.

**Caelan:** Were you thinking of carrying yours for them?

**Liam:** Could I?

**Caelan:** If that's what you'd like.

**Liam:** Even if I don't know their name?

**Caelan:** Yes.

**Liam:** (after a moment) I think I would.

**Tomas:** All right.

**Liam:** It can still be a tower?

**Caelan:** Of course.

**Liam:** Good. I want to keep the window.

## Final Panel

**Tomas:** These are the steps you tried before.

**Liam:** Those ones fit. The little ones went too far round.

**Caelan:** Would you like me to hold this corner?

**Liam:** Yes. Don't move it yet.

**Caelan:** Tell me when.

**Liam’s father:** Which of these sheets is the final piece?

**Liam:** This one. I'm keeping that one to look at.

**Liam’s father:** Then keep the two apart while you work. I'll help with the fastening afterward.

**Tomas:** Where do you want the first step?

**Liam:** Here. Then it turns before the window.

**Tomas:** You could mark the turn first.

**Liam:** I want to start at the bottom.

**Tomas:** All right. I'll hold this side.

**Liam:** Not on the line.

**Tomas:** Here?

**Liam:** Yes. That's better.

**Caelan:** Is my corner still where you want it?

**Liam:** It is. Wait until I've checked the top.

**Liam’s father:** Take your time. The frame isn't going anywhere.

**Liam:** All right. I know where I want it now.

## Dry Fit

**Liam’s father:** Before we fasten it, we'll try the paper around the frame.

**Liam:** With the window facing out?

**Liam’s father:** Yes. You show me which side you want at the front.

**Liam:** This one. That's where the stairs come round.

**Caelan:** Would you like me to steady the frame?

**Liam:** Yes, please.

**Tomas:** I can keep the loose edge from curling.

**Liam’s father:** Good. Liam, hold the paper lightly while we line it up.

**Liam:** Will it cover the point?

**Liam’s father:** The point stays. We keep the opening at the top clear, just as Mira showed you.

**Liam:** I remember.

**Caelan:** Ready at this side.

**Liam’s father:** Then let's fit it and see where the edges meet.

**Liam:** Don't fasten it until I've looked at the window.

**Liam’s father:** We won't.

## Fasten Covering

**Liam:** I can see the window.

**Tomas:** And the turn in the stairs.

**Liam’s father:** The edges meet here. Are you happy with where they are?

**Liam:** Can this one go a little further?

**Liam’s father:** A little. Keep the bottom level.

**Caelan:** Show me where you'd like it held.

**Liam:** Here. Until Dad fastens that part.

**Caelan:** All right.

**Liam’s father:** I'll do the small fastening. You can hold the edge for me.

**Liam:** Like this?

**Liam’s father:** Yes. Keep your fingers there.

**Tomas:** What about the other side?

**Liam’s father:** We'll go around it a little at a time. No need to pull it all tight at once.

**Liam:** I don't want the window bent.

**Liam’s father:** Then we'll keep checking it.

**Caelan:** We're ready, Liam.

**Liam:** All right. Let's do it.

## Finished Lantern

**Liam:** It's got all the sides on.

**Liam’s father:** It has. Let me check the joins and the handle before you pick it up.

**Tomas:** It looks like your tower.

**Liam:** That step bends.

**Tomas:** A little.

**Liam:** I could do it again.

**Caelan:** Would you like to?

**Liam:** (looking at it) No. I like the window.

**Liam’s father:** The covering is secure. The opening's clear, and Mira's handle is holding well.

**Liam:** Can I turn it?

**Liam’s father:** Gently. Give yourself room, like you did with the frame.

**Tomas:** I'll stand back.

**Liam:** Mum will see it straight away, won't she?

**Caelan:** She saw the drawing. You can show her how you made it fit.

**Liam:** I want to tell her about the knot, too.

**Liam’s father:** You can tell her all of it. She's still at the school.

**Liam:** I'm carrying it myself.

**Caelan:** We know. We're coming with you.

**Liam’s father:** Ready? Lift it carefully and see how it feels.

## School Welcome

**Tessa:** Did you finish it?

**Liam:** Yes. It's got the window now.

**Tessa:** It really is a tower.

**Liam:** I said it was going to be.

**Liam’s mother:** Liam? There you are.

**Liam:** Mum, look.

**Liam’s mother:** Just a moment, love. Tessa, leave that piece flat while it dries. Thank you.

**Tessa:** All right.

**Liam’s mother:** Now, let me see. You've been busy.

**Liam:** Mira helped with the frame. Then we finished it at home.

**Caelan:** His father helped us with the covering.

**Liam’s mother:** Thank you for bringing him back. Is he going to let me have a proper look?

**Liam:** Yes. But I'm holding it.

**Liam’s mother:** Of course you are. Come where I can see the front.

## Show Mother

**Liam’s mother:** Show me the stairs.

**Liam:** I had to use bigger ones. Otherwise there wasn't room for the window.

**Liam’s mother:** I see. They go around this side?

**Liam:** Yes. That one's a bit bent.

**Liam’s mother:** May I look closer?

**Liam:** Gently.

**Liam’s mother:** Of course. And this is the corner you tied?

**Liam:** Mira checked it afterward.

**Liam’s mother:** Then you've had good company while you made it.

**Tomas:** He did quite a lot himself.

**Liam:** Caelan held the paper where I wanted it.

**Caelan:** He told us where.

**Liam’s mother:** (smiling) I'm glad you worked it out together.

**Liam:** Do you like the point?

**Liam’s mother:** I do. It looks like the tower you showed me.

**Liam:** We changed the roof a bit. There has to be room for the heat.

**Liam’s mother:** Then I'm glad you listened to Mira.

**Liam’s mother:** And who will you carry it for?

**Liam:** Someone who hasn't got one.

**Liam’s mother:** Someone without anybody there to carry a light for them?

**Liam:** Yes. Is that all right?

**Liam’s mother:** Yes, love. We'll make room for it beside ours.

**Liam:** I'll keep hold of it.

**Liam’s mother:** Will you walk with me tomorrow?

**Liam:** I want to carry it myself.

**Liam’s mother:** You can.

## Homeward

**Liam’s mother:** I've still got some things to put away here.

**Liam:** Can I leave it with you?

**Liam’s mother:** There's rather a lot of paper about. I'd like yours kept safely at home until tomorrow.

**Liam:** Dad said he would look after it.

**Caelan:** We can walk him back and help him put it away.

**Liam’s mother:** Thank you. I'll be home when we're finished here.

**Tomas:** We won't keep him out this time.

**Liam:** We've already shown you.

**Liam’s mother:** You have. And I'm glad you came.

**Liam:** Will you remember which side has the window?

**Liam’s mother:** I think I'll remember. You can show me again tomorrow.

**Liam:** All right. Come on, then.

**Caelan:** Together, Liam.

**Liam:** I know.

## Put Away

**Liam’s father:** Did she get to see it?

**Liam:** Yes. She liked the point.

**Liam’s father:** I thought she might.

**Tomas:** He showed her how the stairs fit, too.

**Liam:** And the corner I tied.

**Liam’s father:** Good. Now let's find it a safe place for the night.

**Liam:** On the table?

**Liam’s father:** Yes. I'll keep this space clear until tomorrow.

**Caelan:** The drawing and practice pieces can stay together at the other side.

**Liam:** Daisy won't reach it there?

**Liam’s father:** It's well back from the edge. I'll mind it.

**Liam:** All right. Which way should the window face?

**Liam’s father:** Where you can see it when you come in.

**Liam:** This way, then. I'll set it down.

## Farewell

**Liam:** There. You can see the window.

**Liam’s father:** Yes. It's safe there.

**Liam:** Don't put anything on it.

**Liam’s father:** I won't.

**Caelan:** You can look at it again before you leave tomorrow.

**Liam:** I will. I want to see Elira's as well.

**Tomas:** She said to come and find her.

**Liam:** And Serene hasn't seen this one yet.

**Caelan:** You'll have time to show them.

**Liam’s father:** Thank you for giving him the afternoon, both of you.

**Tomas:** I enjoyed it.

**Caelan:** So did I.

**Liam:** Thank you for helping with the paper.

**Caelan:** You're welcome, Liam.

**Tomas:** We'll see you tomorrow.

**Liam:** At the lights.

**Caelan:** At the lights.

**Liam’s father:** Go and get on with your preparations. We'll see you there.
