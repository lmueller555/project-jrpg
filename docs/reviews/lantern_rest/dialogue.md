# Questions After the Noise — dialogue

Captured through the production game from the shipped PR2 endpoint.

## Break Invitation

**Liam:** Caelan?

**Caelan:** Yes?

**Liam:** Can we go home for a little while?

**Tomas:** Tired?

**Liam:** My feet are. And I'm hungry.

**Caelan:** Of course. We've been walking quite a bit.

**Tomas:** Your frame's still at Mira's.

**Liam:** I haven't forgotten it.

**Tomas:** I didn't think you had.

**Caelan:** We can collect it on the way. If the handle's ready, you can take it home.

**Liam:** And the drawing.

**Caelan:** And the drawing.

**Liam:** I want to try the stairs before Mum sees it.

**Tomas:** We can look at them after you've had something to eat.

**Liam:** All right. Come on.

**Caelan:** There's no hurry. We'll walk.

## Collect Frame

**Mira:** Back already? How did you get on?

**Liam:** We saw Elira's lantern. She's had it a long time.

**Mira:** She looks after it. Yours is ready to take home, if that's where you're going.

**Liam:** Did the handle stay where we put it?

**Mira:** It did. I've secured it and checked the joins.

**Liam:** Can I carry it this time?

**Mira:** Try it. Keep it clear of the ground, and give yourself room when you turn.

**Tomas:** I'll mind the other side.

**Caelan:** Would you like me to take the drawing?

**Liam:** Don't fold it.

**Caelan:** I can keep it flat in my satchel.

**Liam:** All right. Is this the paper for the sides?

**Mira:** Yes. Try your design on a spare piece first. Leave the covering loose until you're happy with it.

**Liam:** I want the stairs to go round here.

**Mira:** Then lay the paper beside your drawing and see how much room you have.

**Tomas:** The drawing's got quite a few steps.

**Liam:** I know how many.

**Mira:** You can change your mind when you try them. That's what the spare piece is for.

**Caelan:** I'll take the loose sheets with the drawing.

**Liam:** Please don't put anything on top.

**Caelan:** I won't.

**Mira:** Ready, Liam? You can lift it now.

## Home Welcome

**Liam’s father:** There you are. Have you eaten anything since you left?

**Liam:** We went to the mill.

**Liam’s father:** That doesn't answer me.

**Tomas:** (amused) He asked about buns.

**Liam:** There weren't any there.

**Liam’s father:** Then you've come to the right place for bread, at least. Is that your lantern?

**Liam:** The frame. We haven't put the paper on.

**Liam’s father:** I see. It's taller than I expected.

**Liam:** It's a tower.

**Liam’s father:** So it is. Mind the corner as you come past.

**Caelan:** Mira helped him make it. The drawing and paper are with me.

**Liam’s father:** Thank you, both of you. There's room to put them down here.

**Liam:** Can I leave the frame where Daisy won't knock it?

**Liam’s father:** On the table. Set it well back from the edge, and we'll keep the food at this end.

**Tomas:** Your mother's still at the school?

**Liam’s father:** She'll still be at the school. I'll hear all about the afternoon when she's back.

**Liam:** Ada got paste on her sleeve.

**Liam’s father:** Then perhaps I'll hear about that, too.

**Caelan:** She knew we might stop at some of the preparations.

**Liam’s father:** Good. Have a break before you go anywhere else. You look ready for one.

**Liam:** Can you keep the paper flat?

**Caelan:** Yes. Let's put everything down carefully.

## Snack

**Liam’s father:** There. Bread and something to drink. You two help yourselves as well.

**Caelan:** Thank you.

**Tomas:** I will. Thank you.

**Liam:** The wheel was really loud.

**Liam’s father:** At the mill?

**Liam:** You could hear it before we got there. Halden showed us where to stand.

**Liam’s father:** Did you stay there?

**Liam:** Yes. The water doesn't go in the flour.

**Liam’s father:** That's good to hear.

**Tomas:** He wanted to know what it could grind.

**Liam:** I wasn't going to put anything in.

**Caelan:** He asked first. Halden was happy to let us look.

**Liam’s father:** Then I'm glad you went. Finish that bite before you tell me the rest.

**Liam:** There is more.

**Liam’s father:** I thought there might be.

**Liam’s father:** There's more bread if you'd like it, Tomas.

**Tomas:** Yes, please.

**Liam:** You already had some.

**Liam’s father:** So did you.

**Tomas:** (grinning) I don't think we've finished.

**Liam’s father:** Help yourselves. Yours goes on the table when you're done, Liam.

**Caelan:** There's time to sit a little longer, Liam.

**Liam:** I want to try the paper now. My feet don't have to do anything for that.

## Paper Practice

**Liam:** It fitted before.

**Tomas:** On the drawing?

**Liam:** Yes. This bit's too small.

**Caelan:** Show me where you want the last step.

**Liam:** There. But then there's no room for the window.

**Tomas:** Could the stairs turn sooner?

**Liam:** They're meant to go all the way round.

**Caelan:** Would another piece underneath help you try it?

**Liam:** Don't draw it for me.

**Caelan:** I won't. Show me where to hold it.

**Liam:** This corner. It's moving when I rub that bit.

**Liam’s father:** You can use a fresh piece. There's no need to rub through this one.

**Liam:** But I got the top right.

**Caelan:** Keep it beside you, then. You can look at the top while you try the stairs again.

**Liam:** (frustrated) I know what I want it to look like.

**Tomas:** I know. The drawing helped Mira see it, didn't it?

**Liam:** Yes.

**Tomas:** Could you try a few bigger steps on this piece? Just to see.

**Liam:** There wouldn't be as many.

**Tomas:** You'd have room for the window.

**Liam:** Maybe. Can I keep the other piece, too?

**Liam’s father:** Of course. I'll keep the food away from it.

**Caelan:** I'm holding this corner. Tell me when you want to move it.

**Liam:** All right. Don't let go yet.

## Brothers

**Tomas:** (quietly) I thought Elira would be glad not to carry it.

**Caelan:** Her lantern?

**Tomas:** Yes. I thought I was helping.

**Caelan:** She was glad you asked.

**Tomas:** But she still wanted to carry it herself.

**Caelan:** Yes.

**Tomas:** I didn't think about that.

**Caelan:** She told you. You said all right.

**Tomas:** I nearly tried to persuade her.

**Caelan:** I'm glad you didn't.

**Tomas:** So am I. I can mind the door.

**Caelan:** She'll be expecting you now.

**Tomas:** I'll be there.

**Caelan:** I wasn't sure what to say when she went quiet.

**Tomas:** You offered to stay.

**Caelan:** I wanted to.

**Tomas:** I'm glad we did.

**Liam:** Caelan? Can you look at this bit?

**Caelan:** Coming. How are the stairs?

**Liam:** There's room for the window now. I haven't decided about the bottom.

## Liam Questions

**Liam:** Why does Elira take the lantern for her husband?

**Caelan:** To remember him when everyone gathers.

**Liam:** She already remembers him.

**Caelan:** She does. Taking it lets her do that with other people.

**Liam:** Is that why it's called Returning Light?

**Caelan:** We believe the life in us returns to the gods when we die. The festival is part of how we remember that.

**Liam:** What comes back?

**Tomas:** The life. That's what Caelan means by returning.

**Liam:** But Elira still wanted him here.

**Caelan:** Yes. You heard how much she misses him.

**Liam:** I liked when she was laughing.

**Tomas:** So did I.

**Liam:** Can we ask Serene about it?

**Caelan:** Of course. She'll give you time to ask.

**Liam’s father:** If you're going up to the sanctuary, leave the frame here. I'll keep it safe.

**Liam:** And the drawing. I want to use that piece again.

**Liam’s father:** All of it stays together on the table.

**Caelan:** Would you like some more to drink before we go?

**Liam:** No, thank you. I'm ready.

**Tomas:** Thank you for the bread.

**Liam’s father:** You're welcome. Stay together, you three.

**Liam:** We will. Don't let Daisy have the paper.

**Liam’s father:** I'll keep an eye on it. Go and have your talk.

## Sanctuary Welcome

**Mother Serene:** Hello, you three. Have you come to show me the lantern?

**Liam:** It's at home. The frame's finished, but the paper isn't.

**Mother Serene:** Then I shall look forward to seeing it when you're ready.

**Caelan:** Liam had some questions after our visit to Elira.

**Mother Serene:** Did you enjoy seeing her?

**Liam:** Yes. She told us how her husband got the song wrong.

**Tomas:** Every year, apparently.

**Mother Serene:** (smiling) That sounds like a conversation worth having.

**Liam:** Am I keeping you from that?

**Mother Serene:** These papers can wait a little. Tell me what you wanted to ask.

**Liam:** Caelan said the life goes back to the gods.

**Mother Serene:** Yes. That's what we believe.

**Liam:** I don't think I understood it.

**Mother Serene:** All right. We can take our time.

**Caelan:** We're not in a hurry.

**Tomas:** We've had something to eat, too.

**Mother Serene:** Good. Then we'll be comfortable.

## Returning Life

**Liam:** What part of Elira's husband comes back?

**Mother Serene:** We speak of the life that was in him. The life in all of us.

**Liam:** Like the life in me?

**Mother Serene:** Yes. We believe it returns to the gods when we die.

**Liam:** Does any of it go inside the lantern?

**Mother Serene:** No, love. It's still a lamp. Elira carries it to remember her husband.

**Liam:** Why does she light it, then?

**Mother Serene:** It gives her something to carry when we gather. She can stand with the people who remember him, and with people who want to keep her company.

**Liam:** Even if they didn't know him?

**Mother Serene:** Yes. You hadn't heard him sing, had you?

**Liam:** No. I wanted to.

**Mother Serene:** You listened when she told you about him.

**Liam:** He put a garden gate in it.

**Tomas:** And sang it louder when she tried to tell him.

**Mother Serene:** (laughing softly) Poor Elira. I can imagine her face.

**Liam:** She laughed, too. And then she got quiet.

**Mother Serene:** You can remember something funny and wish the person were here to laugh with you.

**Liam:** Does she still believe what you said? About the gods?

**Mother Serene:** I believe she does.

**Liam:** But she still wants him here.

**Mother Serene:** Of course. She misses sharing her days with him. Her faith doesn't stop her missing that.

## Remembering

**Liam:** Is the whole festival for people who've died?

**Mother Serene:** Remembering them is part of it. So is being here together.

**Liam:** Like taking bread to the Venns?

**Mother Serene:** Yes. They're still part of the celebration if they spend the evening at home.

**Caelan:** Their mother said she'd speak to you when they know what they can manage.

**Mother Serene:** Then we'll wait to hear from her. I'm glad you visited.

**Tomas:** They've been choosing colors for a decoration.

**Liam:** Yellow at both ends. Their mum's doing the ties.

**Mother Serene:** That sounds lovely.

**Liam:** What if someone doesn't want to sing?

**Mother Serene:** They can listen. You don't have to do every part of the evening.

**Liam:** Elira said some people just listen.

**Mother Serene:** She was right.

**Liam:** I was mostly thinking about the food before.

**Mother Serene:** Are you still looking forward to it?

**Liam:** Yes.

**Mother Serene:** So am I.

**Liam:** I want to hear Elira sing. She said we could find her.

**Mother Serene:** Then I think she'll be glad to see you.

## Sanctuary Close

**Mother Serene:** Was there something else you wanted to ask?

**Liam:** I don't think so. Not yet.

**Mother Serene:** You can ask another time if you think of something.

**Liam:** I thought Elira might not want to come if it made her sad.

**Mother Serene:** She can decide what she wants to do. We can make sure she's welcome when she comes.

**Tomas:** I'm going to mind her door when she brings the lantern out.

**Mother Serene:** Good. That sounds useful.

**Caelan:** Thank you for giving us the time.

**Mother Serene:** I'm glad you brought him. How did the work at Mira's go?

**Liam:** It's still a tower. We had to change the roof a bit.

**Tomas:** And leave somewhere for the heat to go.

**Liam:** I know about that now. The stairs are the hard part.

**Mother Serene:** Are they coming along?

**Liam:** I've tried them on a spare piece. There's room for the window.

**Mother Serene:** Then you've made some progress today.

**Liam:** (pleased) I tied one of the corners myself.

**Mother Serene:** You'll have to show me when it's finished.

**Liam:** I will. And Elira. And Mum hasn't seen it yet.

**Caelan:** It's safe at home. We can think about the rest when you're ready.

**Liam:** All right. Thank you, Serene.

**Mother Serene:** You're very welcome, Liam.
