# New title music — piano and cello

The old title track is discarded and replaced in the shipped WAV/Ogg. The
oscillator-based flute/harp composition is no longer generated by the build tool.

- [Clean 40-second piano/cello excerpt](sketch.ogg)
- [Same excerpt with metronome](sketch_with_click.ogg)
- [Full 93-second composition](../../../assets/ui/title/eldenmere_theme.ogg)
- [Title-screen video with the new composition](../title_water_music/title_with_music.mp4)
- [Measured onset-alignment report](timing_audit.json)

The composition is **Eldenmere — A Place to Return To**: warm sampled piano,
an answering solo cello, restrained strings and three soft bell accents.
G major is shaded by E minor and a brief C-minor chord. The melody is new;
none of the discarded theme's motif or note patterns are reused.

| Passage | Begins | Arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piano | 0:00 | Complete melody with gentle half-note chord support |
| Cello answer | 0:27 | New lower-register answering phrase over piano |
| Together | 0:53 | Piano melody and cello answer with restrained strings |
| Quiet return | 1:20 | Texture recedes into a new closing phrase |

Meter is **4/4 at a fixed 72 BPM**. Every instrument's written note starts and
releases fall on numbered beats. Durations are quarter, half, dotted-half or
whole notes. There are no randomized timing offsets, syncopated starts,
independent instrument loops or rhythmic delay echoes.

At 44.1 kHz a beat is exactly 36,750 samples. Rendering uses that shared sample
clock. Every unique instrument/pitch/velocity sample is calibrated for its
attack, with note triggering advanced as needed. Independent isolated probes
then measure the audible entrances against the target beat using a causal
5 ms RMS window at 12% of each probe's peak. The recorded piano, cello, strings
and bell probe errors are all under one millisecond; the audit's allowed
tolerance is 4 ms. This is an onset measurement, not a claim that the peaks of
different instruments' natural envelopes occur simultaneously.

The click reference uses exactly the same mixed audio as the clean excerpt,
with a higher click on beat one and lower clicks on beats two through four.
Both excerpts end after 12 bars (40 seconds), with a short end fade for review.
They are not the runtime loop; the game plays the full 28-bar composition.

GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 supplies the samples under its music-production license.
See `assets/source/audio/title_piano_cello/` for attribution, license, source
revision and exact SoundFont fingerprint. Only the renderer needs FluidSynth;
the game still requires only Pygame.

The title scenery, menu, water animation, fullscreen presentation and music
on/off controls retain their existing behavior. Pull the current revision and
launch `python main.py` to hear the new track in-game.
