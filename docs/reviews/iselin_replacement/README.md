# Iselin narrative replacement history

The original replacement made Iselin the live regent across the opening,
MQ02/MQ03 dialogue, portraits, arrival scenes, Gallery playback and post-rescue
resolution. Her existing title, quest stages and outcomes were preserved. The
approved biography and disclosure limits remain in
[ISELIN.md](../../narrative/game_01/characters/ISELIN.md).

The native artwork integration preserves those story and save contracts while
replacing the superseded detailed sprite bank. The earlier detailed-art images,
preview animations, source masters and dedicated builders were removed at the
user's request. Their obsolete export commands and visual checks no longer
serve as current reproduction instructions.

Current art, production captures and verification are documented in the
[native integration review](../iselin_native_integration/README.md).
The ordinary approved idles/walks and dialogue portrait remain byte-identical
to their respective native-art approvals.

The original story replacement also corrected detailed-canvas copying in the
road-pan effect and missing presentation in the Sanctuary scene. Shared canvas
copy/alpha/clip behavior remains a renderer concern, independent of Iselin's
now-native artwork. Exact aerial handoff continuity and quest/save regressions
retain their dedicated tests; this historical note does not certify a new
full battle/road playthrough.
