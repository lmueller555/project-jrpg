# Opening dialogue pass — actual New Game review

Captured through normal production-game progression, including the Venn magick failure.

## opening_wake

Tomas: `Caelan? You're awake, aren't you?`

Tomas: `Mother Serene says if you're still in bed when the last bell rings, you're helping me clean the courtyard.`

Caelan: `She didn't say that.`

Tomas: `No. But she might, once I suggest it.`

Caelan: `I'm getting up.`

Tomas: `Breakfast won't wait forever!`

## opening_greeting

Tomas: `There you are. I saved you some bread.`

Caelan: `You did?`

Tomas: `*grins* Mother Serene wouldn’t let me eat it.`

Mother Serene: `Sit down, Caelan. Before your brother decides you aren’t coming.`

Caelan: `Have you been up long?`

Mother Serene: `Long enough to wish there were another pair of hands in the kitchen.`

Tomas: `I offered.`

Mother Serene: `You offered to taste things.`

Tomas: `Someone ought to.`

Caelan: `*smiles* Very public-spirited of you.`

Tomas: `Thank you.`

## opening_breakfast

Mother Serene: `Enjoy a quiet breakfast while you can. Tomorrow we’ll have everyone coming and going for the Festival of Light.`

Caelan: `And today?`

Mother Serene: `Today we make sure everything—and everyone—is ready for it.`

Tomas: `That sounds like rather more work.`

Mother Serene: `*with a small smile* It usually is.`

## opening_kindness

Caelan: `Your cup’s here, Mother.`

Mother Serene: `Thank you, Caelan. Now, could you both spare me a little time?`

## serene_quest_offer

Mother Serene: `I promised to look in on three households this morning, but there’s more here than I can leave unattended.`

Caelan: `Who needs us?`

Mother Serene: `*with concern* Halden first. This is his medicine. His joints have been troubling him again.`

Tomas: `He’ll tell us they’re perfectly well.`

Mother Serene: `He probably will. He’s milling flour for the Festival of Light, and I suspect he’s trying to carry every sack himself.`

Caelan: `Is there much left to do?`

Mother Serene: `Enough to keep the village kitchens waiting if he overworks himself. He should be at his cottage, just south of the mill.`

Tomas: `Across the upper bridge?`

Mother Serene: `Yes. Go with Caelan and see whether he needs a hand.`

Mother Serene: `There’s bread and lamp oil for Elira, too. She’ll be getting her lantern ready for the Festival of Light.`

Caelan: `I’ll call on her.`

Mother Serene: `She should be at home, by the southern bridge. Please ask how she’s doing while you’re there.`

Caelan: `Of course.`

Mother Serene: `And these supplies are for the Venns, up on the eastern terrace. Their little one’s still recovering.`

Tomas: `*more quietly* Any better?`

Mother Serene: `A little, last I heard. They’ve had their hands full between that and the plot.`

## opening_acceptance

Caelan: `I’ll see to them.`

Mother Serene: `Thank you. Halden first, then visit the other two in whichever order suits you.`

Tomas: `Shall I come for all three?`

Mother Serene: `Come back after Halden. I’ll need your help with the sanctuary preparations.`

Tomas: `I was hoping for something interesting.`

Mother Serene: `Then start by helping someone who needs you.`

Caelan: `*to Tomas* Come on. You can decide how interesting it was afterward.`

## mira_working

Mira: `Nearly there. This fitting has other ideas.`

## halden_arrival

Master Halden: `*grumbles* Serene sent you? I’m not so frail that I need someone counting my aches.`

Caelan: `She sent the medicine you asked about last week.`

Master Halden: `I said I’d think about asking.`

Tomas: `*grins* That’s practically a yes, for you.`

Master Halden: `Hmph. And which one of you came to do actual work?`

Caelan: `What needs moving?`

Master Halden: `Those three sacks of flour by the door. Onto the dry pallet. I’ve more coming through the mill and nowhere sensible to put it.`

Tomas: `All this for tomorrow?`

Master Halden: `People want their flour before they start baking for the Festival of Light. They’ve been very particular about telling me that.`

Caelan: `We’ll make some room.`

Master Halden: `One sack at a time. They’re heavier than they look.`

Tomas: `I’ll take the middle one.`

Master Halden: `You’ll wait until your brother’s ready.`

## halden_conclusion

Master Halden: `There. That’s better.`

Tomas: `You can actually get through the doorway now.`

Master Halden: `I could get through it before.`

Caelan: `Carrying one of those?`

Master Halden: `*after a pause* …I spent forty years carrying these myself.`

Caelan: `Then perhaps you’ve earned the right to let someone else carry a few.`

Master Halden: `Hmph. Tell Serene I’ll take the medicine.`

Caelan: `She’ll be pleased.`

Master Halden: `And if she asks, the wheel’s been making a dreadful racket. I’ll have it looked at.`

Tomas: `Ask Mira. If anyone can repair it, she can.`

Master Halden: `She’s got her own repairs to finish.`

Caelan: `One more reason to ask before it becomes a bigger job.`

Master Halden: `You’ve been listening to Serene.`

Tomas: `*with a small smile* Both of us have. It’s difficult not to.`

## halden_goodbye

Tomas: `I’d better get back. Mother needs help with the preparations.`

Caelan: `Go on. I’ve still got Elira by the southern bridge and the Venns up on the eastern terrace.`

Tomas: `You’ll be back before we’ve finished, then?`

Caelan: `That depends how much you get done.`

Tomas: `*rolls his eyes* Oh, very good.`

Caelan: `And try not to turn the courtyard into a workshop.`

Tomas: `*grins* No promises.`

## elira_arrival

Elira: `*with a wry smile* A delivery from Serene? I swear she thinks I live on bread and stubbornness.`

Caelan: `She mentioned lamp oil, too.`

Elira: `Sensible woman. I was nearly out. Come in, Caelan.`

Caelan: `Getting ready for tomorrow?`

Elira: `*sighs* Trying to. I put the lantern safely out of the way, and now I can’t reach the wretched thing.`

Caelan: `Up there?`

Elira: `Yes. The stool’s beneath the shelf. Would you fetch it for me?`

Caelan: `Of course.`

Elira: `Gently, mind. I’ve got enough to mend without giving myself another job.`

## elira_resolution

Elira: `*looks over the lantern* Thank you. I can clean it here.`

Caelan: `You’re carrying this one for your husband again?`

Elira: `I am. He used to say he only came to the Festival of Light to hear me sing.`

Caelan: `Did he?`

Elira: `*laughs* The old liar couldn’t carry a tune himself. Never stopped him joining in.`

Caelan: `Will tomorrow be difficult?`

Elira: `*her smile softens* Parts of it, perhaps. I still turn to tell him things. Then I remember.`

Elira: `*after a quiet moment* But remembering him isn’t the same as losing him again. There’s plenty I’m glad to remember.`

Caelan: `I’m glad you’re coming.`

Elira: `So am I. And you’ll be there?`

Caelan: `I wouldn’t miss it.`

Elira: `Good. Tell Serene I’ll bring the lantern myself. She’s done enough carrying things for me today.`

Caelan: `I’ll tell her.`

## venn_arrival

Mrs. Venn: `Caelan. Is that from the sanctuary?`

Caelan: `Serene asked me to bring these round. How’s the little one?`

Mrs. Venn: `*with relief* Finally keeping food down. We’ve had a quieter morning, thank the gods.`

Caelan: `That’s good to hear.`

Mrs. Venn: `Please thank Serene for us. I meant to come down myself, but—`

Caelan: `*gently* You’ve had enough to manage.`

Mr. Venn: `If this channel would cooperate, we might get a few things finished.`

Mrs. Venn: `What’s happened now?`

Mr. Venn: `*sighs* The gate’s caught. There’s water in the brook and hardly any reaching the far end of our plot. I can’t keep carrying buckets across all afternoon.`

Caelan: `May I take a look?`

Mr. Venn: `Please. It’s east of the cottage. Look at the pin, then the channel below it.`

Mrs. Venn: `Borrow what you need from the tool box.`

Caelan: `I’ll see what I can do.`

## venn_pin

Caelan: `The pin’s caught at an angle. I’ll need to take the pressure off before I set it straight.`

## venn_channel

Caelan: `Twigs have collected below the gate. I’ll need to clear those, too.`

## venn_choice

Caelan: `I could use their tools. Or perhaps I can push the water back long enough to free the pin.`

## venn_magicks_failed

Caelan: `*quietly* I can’t move it. I’ll need the tools.`

## venn_resolution

Mr. Venn: `There it goes. That should reach the far end now.`

Mrs. Venn: `One less thing to worry about.`

Caelan: `Has it been catching often?`

Mr. Venn: `Often enough. They’ve got pumps in the capital that could fill these channels in half the time.`

Mrs. Venn: `And payments that would take twice as long to finish.`

Mr. Venn: `*defensively* I know. I’m only saying it would be useful.`

Caelan: `For today, at least, you’ve got the water moving again.`

Mrs. Venn: `You’ve done us a kindness. Let Serene know we’ll be all right.`

Caelan: `I will. I hope we see you at the festival.`

Mrs. Venn: `We’ll see how the little one feels. I’d like that.`

Mr. Venn: `And if we do get there, I intend to sit down for a while.`

Mrs. Venn: `*with a small laugh* You say that now.`

Caelan: `I’ll leave you to it. Take care.`

## tomas_greeting

Tomas: `Caelan, can you look at this?`

Caelan: `What's wrong?`

Tomas: `It turns most of the way, then it sticks. I can't see what's catching.`

Caelan: `Is the pin bent?`

Tomas: `I thought so, but it looks straight.`

Caelan: `Have you shown Mira?`

Tomas: `Not yet. I wanted to get it working myself.`

Caelan: `You can still ask her.`

Tomas: `I know. I will if I can't find it.`

## tomas_repeat

Tomas: `One more adjustment. Then I really am getting back to the preparations.`

Caelan: `Should I tell Mother Serene that?`

Tomas: `Tell her the preparations are progressing. That covers both things.`

## serene_quest_report

Mother Serene: `There you are. How is everyone?`

Caelan: `Halden finally accepted the medicine. We moved his flour sacks into the dry.`

Mother Serene: `Did he let you help without an argument?`

Caelan: `Not entirely.`

Mother Serene: `*smiles* I’d have worried if he had.`

Caelan: `Elira has the bread and oil. She’s bringing her husband’s lantern tomorrow.`

Mother Serene: `Good. I’m glad she wants to come.`

Caelan: `And the Venns have their supplies. The little one’s keeping food down. Their irrigation gate was caught, so I got the channel running again.`

Mother Serene: `You’ve had a busy morning.`

Caelan: `A useful one, I think.`

Mother Serene: `You have. And how were they, aside from what needed fixing?`

Caelan: `Halden’s doing more than his joints can bear. Elira misses her husband. The Venns are tired—and worried about what next season will cost them.`

Mother Serene: `I’m glad you stayed long enough to hear that.`

Caelan: `You knew I’d find more than deliveries.`

Mother Serene: `I hoped you’d have time for people. It’s easy to think our work is finished when the baskets are empty.`

Caelan: `Then I suppose we keep showing up.`

Mother Serene: `*warmly* That’s a good place to begin.`

## opening_mill_arrival

Master Halden: `Caelan! That dreadful wheel’s finally stopped altogether.`

Caelan: `You said it was making a noise.`

Master Halden: `And now it isn’t making any noise. That’s worse!`

Mother Serene: `Did you ask Mira to look at it?`

Master Halden: `That’s why I’m here. I could use her help—and another pair of hands, if you can spare him.`

Caelan: `I’ll go with her.`

Master Halden: `There are kitchens waiting on that flour. I’d rather they didn’t all come asking me about it at once.`

Mother Serene: `Mira should be at her workbench, outside the workshop before the upper bridge.`

Caelan: `I’ll stop there on my way to the mill.`

Master Halden: `*with relief* Thank you.`

Mother Serene: `And be careful along the channel. Both of you.`

## mill_reunion

Mira: `*sets the fitting aside* You have the look of someone bringing me another job.`

Caelan: `Could that wait a little?`

Mira: `That depends. Is someone hurt?`

Caelan: `No. Halden’s mill has stopped.`

Mira: `Ah. I wondered when he’d ask me about that noise.`

Caelan: `He came to the sanctuary himself.`

Mira: `Then it must be serious.`

Caelan: `He needs the flour ready for the Festival of Light. I think he’s already imagining everyone asking where their bread is.`

Mira: `He’ll have plenty to say if they ask him at once.`

Caelan: `He had plenty to say when it was only me.`

Mira: `*laughs* Some things keep working.`

Caelan: `Will you take a look?`

Mira: `Of course. Let me leave this somewhere I can find it again.`

Caelan: `Shall I carry anything?`

Mira: `I’ve got my tools. Come on—we’ll see what the wheel’s doing before he takes anything apart.`

## mill_mechanism

Caelan: `*studies the axle* The axle’s still seated. I can’t see anything caught in the wheel.`

Mira: `Neither can I. And nothing’s shifted out of place.`

Caelan: `So we leave it together?`

Mira: `For now, yes.`

Caelan: `Halden will be pleased.`

Mira: `Let’s give him a working mill before we tell him how pleased to be.`

## mill_race

Caelan: `*frowns at the trickle* The brook’s moving, but there’s hardly anything coming down this channel.`

Mira: `There should be a steady flow reaching the wheel.`

Caelan: `Just a trickle. That won’t drive it.`

Mira: `No. We’ll need to look further upstream.`

## mill_diagnosis

Mira: `The wheel isn’t getting enough water to turn under load. Something’s restricting the intake.`

Caelan: `A branch, perhaps?`

Mira: `Could be. We’ll know when we see it.`

Caelan: `We’ll need the upper path.`

Mira: `Back over the upper bridge, then north past the sanctuary steps. Take the road out of the village—the intake’s along that trail.`

Caelan: `All right. Let’s see what’s caught there.`

## mill_reminder_intake

Mira: `We need the north road, past the sanctuary steps. The intake’s along the trail beyond the village.`

## mill_intake

Caelan: `*points to the limb* There. That limb’s wedged across the screen.`

Mira: `Look how the water’s pressing it into the bars.`

Caelan: `We won’t pull it clear like that.`

Mira: `No. We’ll take the pressure off first.`

Caelan: `And the catch?`

Mira: `Under the water in that little stone recess. We’ll deal with it once the intake’s isolated.`

Caelan: `Tell me where you need me.`

Mira: `I’ll set the bypass below the screen. Keep clear while I turn it.`

## mill_prepare

Caelan: `Here?`

Mira: `*checks Caelan is clear* That’s fine. Give me a moment.`

## mill_prepare_done

Caelan: `The pressure’s easing.`

Mira: `Good. We’ve still got water trapped around the catch, but the main flow’s off it now.`

## mill_spell_intro

Caelan: `I could move that into the drain.`

Mira: `Just the water in the recess?`

Caelan: `Yes. There’s nowhere for more to come through now.`

Mira: `All right. I’ll keep clear.`

## mill_encouragement_failed

Caelan: `There. It worked.`

Mira: `It did. I can reach the catch now.`

Caelan: `*a small smile* I thought it would.`

Mira: `You sound surprised.`

Caelan: `Pleased. Mostly.`

Mira: `And the rest?`

Caelan: `*his smile fades a little* I couldn’t do it at the Venns’. I wanted to spare them the trouble, and I still ended up borrowing their tools.`

Mira: `You were fighting a current there. This was a smaller job.`

Caelan: `I know. I just wish I’d managed both.`

Mira: `You got their water running again, didn’t you?`

Caelan: `Yes.`

Mira: `*gently* And you’ve cleared this catch for me. Give yourself that much.`

Caelan: `All right. I’ll try.`

## mill_repair

Mira: `I’ll release the catch. Take the rope and keep it steady.`

Caelan: `Ready.`

Mira: `Wait until I say. We want the limb clear of the screen.`

Caelan: `*nods* I’ve got it.`

Mira: `Now. Pull.`

## mill_repair_done

Mira: `*with relief* There. Hold it a moment… Good. You can let go.`

Caelan: `That’s the water coming through.`

Mira: `It is. Let’s go back and see what the wheel makes of it.`

## mill_reminder_verify

Mira: `Let’s get back to the wheel and check it’s taking the load. Then we can give Halden some good news.`

## mill_halden_waiting

Master Halden: `I’ll leave the checking to you and Mira. There’s plenty of festival flour still to grind.`

## mill_verify

Caelan: `*smiles* That sounds more like it.`

Mira: `The wheel’s taking the load.`

Caelan: `Halden’s dreadful racket.`

Mira: `He’ll be calling it a lovely sound by now.`

Caelan: `Should we let him enjoy it?`

Mira: `After we tell him what happened. I want him to know which bits he doesn’t need to take apart.`

## mill_resolution

Master Halden: `*listens to the wheel* There it is. I was beginning to miss that dreadful racket.`

Caelan: `Mira said you would.`

Mira: `A limb had jammed the intake screen. We freed it and reset the catch.`

Master Halden: `*raises an eyebrow* We?`

Mira: `Caelan cleared the water from the inspection recess. And did his share of the pulling.`

Caelan: `Mira knew what we were looking at.`

Master Halden: `And you listened. That helps.`

Mira: `The wheel itself was sound. You can leave it together.`

Master Halden: `I hadn’t started.`

Caelan: `We thought we’d best hurry.`

Master Halden: `Hmph. You can both laugh when the flour’s finished.`

Mira: `How much have you got left?`

Master Halden: `Enough that I’d rather hear the wheel than the two of you. There are people waiting to start their festival baking.`

Caelan: `We’ll leave you to it, then.`

Master Halden: `Caelan.`

Caelan: `Yes?`

Master Halden: `*more quietly* My thanks. To both of you.`

Mira: `You’re welcome.`

Master Halden: `There’ll be flour for the Festival of Light. You can tell Serene that.`
