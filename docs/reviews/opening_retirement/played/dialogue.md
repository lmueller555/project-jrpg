# Opening dialogue pass — actual New Game review

Captured through normal production-game progression, including the Venn magick failure.

## opening_wake

Tomas: `Cael? Breakfast’s ready.`

Caelan: `*sleepily* All right. I’m coming.`

Tomas: `Did I wake you?`

Caelan: `Yes.`

Tomas: `Oh. I thought I heard you moving.`

Caelan: `That was me trying to get comfortable.`

Tomas: `*a small laugh* Well, get uncomfortable. Mother’s already sent me up once.`

Caelan: `Give me a minute.`

Tomas: `I’ll be downstairs.`

## opening_greeting

Mother Serene: `Good morning, Caelan. There’s bread beside your brother. Help yourself.`

Caelan: `Morning. Sorry—I didn’t mean to sleep so late.`

Mother Serene: `You haven’t missed anything. Sit down.`

Tomas: `Except the courtyard.`

Caelan: `You’ve finished?`

Tomas: `Most of it. There are still the tables to move.`

Caelan: `I’ll help you with those.`

Tomas: `Good. I wasn’t going to try them on my own.`

## opening_breakfast

Tomas: `Do you want the clean cups back on the shelf, Mother?`

Mother Serene: `Leave them by the hearth until they're dry.`

Tomas: `I could wipe the table while they’re there.`

Mother Serene: `Yes, that would help. Finish your breakfast first.`

Tomas: `And when we’re finished, could I go over to Mira’s?`

Caelan: `What are you working on?`

Tomas: `Nothing yet. I want to ask her about the arm brace. I can follow each linkage on its own, but I can’t work out how they help her lift without getting in the way of her hand.`

Caelan: `You could ask her to slow him down.`

Tomas: `I did. She started explaining, and then someone brought her a broken hinge.`

Mother Serene: `She’ll be busy today. Ask whether she has time before you settle in.`

Tomas: `I will.`

Caelan: `Will you need me all afternoon?`

Mother Serene: `I need some help this morning. What did you have in mind?`

Caelan: `I wanted to practise my magicks. Down by the brook, perhaps.`

Tomas: `You had your book out last night.`

Caelan: `I know.`

Tomas: `I wasn’t complaining.`

Caelan: `*a little embarrassed* No. I just keep going back to the same part.`

Mother Serene: `Is there something in it you don’t understand?`

Caelan: `It makes sense when I read it. Then I try, and I can’t tell whether I’ve misunderstood something or whether I simply need more practice.`

Mother Serene: `Would you like someone with you?`

Caelan: `For now, I’d rather try on my own.`

Mother Serene: `All right. We’ll leave you some time this afternoon.`

Caelan: `Thank you.`

Tomas: `You can leave the table to me. I can wipe it down.`

Caelan: `All right.`

Mother Serene: `Eat another piece before you go, both of you. There’s plenty to do today; I expect we’ll all forget to stop for lunch.`

Tomas: `I won’t.`

Mother Serene: `Then remind the rest of us.`

## opening_kindness

Caelan: `Here, Mother.`

Mother Serene: `Thank you.`

Mother Serene: `When you’ve finished, come and see me. There are a few things I’d like taken into the village.`

Caelan: `We’ll come over.`

## serene_quest_offer

Mother Serene: `Caelan, could you take these into the village for me? I promised to call on a few people, and I’m going to be needed here.`

Caelan: `Yes. Who are we going to?`

Mother Serene: `Halden first. Take the upper bridge to his cottage, just south of the mill. This is the medicine he asked for.`

Tomas: `Are his hands troubling him again?`

Mother Serene: `They were when I saw him. He’s still trying to finish the flour orders before his hands get too stiff.`

Caelan: `We can help with the carrying while we’re there.`

Mother Serene: `Please ask him. He may be more willing to let you help if he’s the one telling you where everything goes.`

Tomas: `He’ll tell us that anyway.`

Mother Serene: `*a small smile* Quite possibly.`

Mother Serene: `There’s bread and lamp oil for Elira, too. She was running low on oil for her lantern.`

Caelan: `At home?`

Mother Serene: `She should be. Her cottage by the southern bridge. If she wants company, stay a little.`

Mother Serene: `And these are for the Venns, up on the eastern terrace. Their little one’s still recovering.`

Tomas: `Still not well enough to come outside?`

Mother Serene: `I haven’t heard. Ask how this morning has been. They’ve hardly had a chance to think about anything beyond the next meal and the next bit of sleep.`

Caelan: `Do they need anything else?`

Mother Serene: `They may. Let them tell you.`

## opening_acceptance

Caelan: `We’ll start with Halden.`

Mother Serene: `Thank you. Then Elira and the Venns in whichever order suits you.`

Tomas: `Am I coming for all three?`

Mother Serene: `Come back after Halden. You and I can get those courtyard tables into place.`

Caelan: `I said I’d help with those.`

Tomas: `We can manage. You’ve got the other visits.`

Caelan: `All right. Take the smaller bundle, then. I’ll carry the rest.`

Tomas: `Ready.`

## mira_working

Mira: `Nearly there. This fitting has other ideas.`

## halden_arrival

Master Halden: `Morning, you two. Mind that sack by the door.`

Caelan: `Serene sent your medicine.`

Master Halden: `Ah. Thank you. I meant to go and fetch it.`

Tomas: `She said your hands were hurting.`

Master Halden: `They are. Particularly when people keep asking me to tell them about it.`

Caelan: `Would moving these help?`

Master Halden: `It would. I need the doorway clear before I bring any more flour through.`

Tomas: `Where do you want them?`

Master Halden: `On the dry pallet by the north wall. Three sacks. Keep them off the floor.`

Caelan: `There’s more waiting at the mill?`

Master Halden: `There are flour orders waiting, and people need time to bake. I’d like to get it done.`

Tomas: `I’ll take the middle one.`

Caelan: `Let me—`

Tomas: `I can carry it. Just help me get it settled.`

Caelan: `All right. We’ll lift it together.`

Master Halden: `Keep it close once you’ve got it. And put it down if you need to. There’s no prize for getting there without stopping.`

## halden_conclusion

Master Halden: `That’s much better. I can get through here again.`

Tomas: `Are they far enough back?`

Master Halden: `Yes. Leave them there.`

Caelan: `You’ve still got a lot to do.`

Master Halden: `I have. But I can manage the mill without carrying everything in and out as well.`

Caelan: `We can tell Serene you need another pair of hands.`

Master Halden: `For the carrying, perhaps. Tell her I asked, before she decides I need someone watching me all day.`

Caelan: `I’ll tell her.`

Master Halden: `And thank you. Both of you.`

Master Halden: `The wheel’s been making an awkward noise, too. I’ll have to look at it.`

Tomas: `Would Mira know what it is?`

Master Halden: `She might. She learned her way around that mill working with me. These days she notices things I’ve walked past twice.`

Caelan: `Have you asked her?`

Master Halden: `Not yet. She’s got people coming to her workshop with their own repairs.`

Caelan: `She’d probably rather hear about it before something breaks.`

Master Halden: `*rubs his hand* Yes. I suppose she would.`

## halden_goodbye

Tomas: `I’d better get back. Mother will be waiting to move the tables.`

Caelan: `I’ve still got Elira by the southern bridge and the Venns on the eastern terrace.`

Tomas: `You don’t have to hurry back for us.`

Caelan: `I wasn’t going to hurry.`

Tomas: `You were about to tell me to wait for you.`

Caelan: `*after a pause* I was going to say I’d help when I got back.`

Tomas: `We’ll leave you something if there’s anything left.`

Caelan: `All right. See you later.`

## elira_arrival

Elira: `Caelan. Come in—there’s room, if you can get past the washing.`

Caelan: `I’ve brought bread and lamp oil from Serene.`

Elira: `Oh, good. I was trying to remember whether I had enough oil left.`

Caelan: `For your lantern?`

Elira: `That, and for seeing what I’m doing tonight. I keep finding one more thing that needs attention.`

Caelan: `What are you working on?`

Elira: `My lantern, once I get it down. I put it on that shelf to keep it safe. Now I can’t reach it safely myself.`

Caelan: `I can fetch it.`

Elira: `Would you? The stool’s underneath. Bring it over to the table when you’ve got it.`

Caelan: `Is the handle secure?`

Elira: `It should be, but take it by the bottom as well. I’d rather you didn’t find out halfway down.`

## elira_resolution

Elira: `There. Now I can see what needs cleaning.`

Caelan: `The frame’s still sound.`

Elira: `It’s lasted well. My husband was very particular about putting it away properly. I used to think he made a performance of it.`

Caelan: `You’ve kept doing it.`

Elira: `Yes. Apparently I was listening.`

Elira: `I like to light it while I sit by the window.`

Caelan: `Did he keep it there?`

Elira: `Beside his chair. He’d polish the brass until I could see myself in it.`

Caelan: `*a small smile* Every evening?`

Elira: `Whenever he found a mark. He’d hold it up and ask whether I could still see one.`

Elira: `Sometimes I still turn to tell him he’s missed a spot.`

Caelan: `…`

Elira: `*after a moment* It could use a little work now.`

Caelan: `I know. I wasn’t sure what to say.`

Elira: `You don’t have to say anything. You’ve brought my lantern down. Stay a minute while I have a look at it.`

Caelan: `Shall I tell Serene you’ve got everything you need?`

Elira: `Yes, please. And thank her for the bread and oil.`

Elira: `Come and find me when you’ve finished helping everyone else.`

Caelan: `I will.`

## venn_arrival

Mrs. Venn: `Hello, Caelan. Come in quietly, would you? We’ve finally had a little sleep.`

Caelan: `Of course. Serene asked me to bring these.`

Mrs. Venn: `Thank you. I meant to get down to Serene’s house yesterday.`

Caelan: `How has this morning been?`

Mrs. Venn: `Better. A little food stayed down, and there hasn’t been so much restlessness.`

Mr. Venn: `We both managed to sit down for a while.`

Mrs. Venn: `Until you went out to the plot.`

Mr. Venn: `I thought I could get the watering finished before it got warm.`

Caelan: `Did something happen?`

Mr. Venn: `The irrigation gate’s jammed. There’s plenty of water in the brook, but hardly any getting through to the far end of our plot.`

Mrs. Venn: `Can it wait?`

Mr. Venn: `Not all day. I’ve been carrying buckets over, but I can’t keep doing that.`

Caelan: `I could look at the gate while I’m here.`

Mr. Venn: `Would you? It’s just east of the cottage. Check the pin at the side, and the channel below the board.`

Mrs. Venn: `The tools are in the box. Borrow what you need.`

Caelan: `I’ll come back and tell you how I get on.`

## venn_pin

Caelan: `The pin’s wedged crooked. The water’s pressing the board against it.`

## venn_channel

Caelan: `There are twigs caught underneath as well. Even if I free the pin, I’ll need to clear those.`

## venn_choice

Caelan: `The mallet should help with the pin. Or I could try pushing the water back for a moment—just enough to take the pressure off.`

## venn_magicks_failed

Caelan: `Come on…`

Caelan: `*quietly* Nothing. I’ll get the tools.`

## venn_resolution

Mr. Venn: `It’s reaching the far end now. I saw it coming through.`

Caelan: `The pin was caught at an angle. I set it straight and cleared what was under the board.`

Mr. Venn: `Thank you. I’d have spent the rest of the morning hauling water.`

Mrs. Venn: `Then you can spend some of it in here.`

Mr. Venn: `Yes. I can.`

Caelan: `Does it catch like that often?`

Mr. Venn: `Often enough that I keep thinking about a pump. They’ve got them in the capital that could do this without all the lifting.`

Mrs. Venn: `We’d still have to buy it. And get it repaired when something went wrong.`

Mr. Venn: `I know. I’m not ordering one. I’d just like to get the watering done without losing half a day to it.`

Caelan: `Would you want Mira to look at the gate?`

Mr. Venn: `If it catches again, I’ll ask her. For now, you’ve got it working.`

Mrs. Venn: `Please tell Serene we’re grateful. And that this morning’s been better.`

Caelan: `I will. Will you let Serene know if there’s anything else you need?`

Mrs. Venn: `We will. She’s done plenty for us already.`

Caelan: `All right. I’ll let you have some quiet.`

Mr. Venn: `Thank you, Caelan.`

## tomas_greeting

Tomas: `Caelan, can you look at this?`

Caelan: `What's wrong?`

Tomas: `It turns most of the way, then it sticks. I can't see what's catching.`

Caelan: `Is the pin bent?`

Tomas: `I thought so, but it looks straight.`

Caelan: `Have you shown Mira?`

Tomas: `Not yet. I wanted to get it working myself.`

Caelan: `You can still ask her.`

Tomas: `I know. I will if I can't find it.`

## serene_quest_report

Mother Serene: `Hello, Caelan. How did you get on?`

Caelan: `Everyone has their supplies. Halden asked whether someone could help with the carrying while he finishes the flour orders.`

Mother Serene: `He asked?`

Caelan: `He wanted me to make sure you knew that.`

Mother Serene: `*smiles* I’ll remember. Did the medicine reach him?`

Caelan: `Yes. We moved the three sacks out of his doorway as well.`

Mother Serene: `Good. I’ll speak to him about the rest.`

Caelan: `Elira’s lantern is down on her table. She wants to clean it this afternoon.`

Mother Serene: `I thought she might. Was she pleased to have it down?`

Caelan: `Yes. She asked me to stay for a little while.`

Mother Serene: `I’m glad you could.`

Caelan: `And the Venns said this morning’s been better. Their little one’s kept some food down.`

Mother Serene: `Oh, that is good.`

Caelan: `Their irrigation gate was jammed. I repaired it while I was there.`

Mother Serene: `Is it working properly now?`

Caelan: `The water’s reaching the far end again. Mr. Venn said that was what he needed.`

Caelan: `*hesitates* I tried a spell on it first. I thought I could push the water back while I freed the pin.`

Mother Serene: `How did it go?`

Caelan: `It didn’t move. I ended up using the tools.`

Mother Serene: `Do you want to tell me about it?`

Caelan: `Later, perhaps.`

Mother Serene: `All right.`

Mother Serene: `Thank you for going round. I’ll follow up with them.`

Caelan: `Is there anything else you need before I go?`

Mother Serene: `Nothing that needs you now. Take the time you asked for this morning.`

Caelan: `I’ll go down to the brook, then.`

## opening_mill_arrival

Master Halden: `Serene? Is Caelan still here?`

Caelan: `I’m here. What’s happened?`

Master Halden: `The wheel’s stopped. I went back to the mill and there wasn’t enough water coming through to turn it.`

Mother Serene: `Have you been along the channel?`

Master Halden: `As far as the bank. I couldn’t see what was holding it up. Mira needs to look at it.`

Caelan: `I’ll find her.`

Master Halden: `Could you help her, too? There may be something to clear, and I’m not much use for that today.`

Mother Serene: `Caelan, you were just going out—`

Caelan: `I can practise later. I’ll go with her.`

Master Halden: `*with relief* Thank you. There’s still grain waiting, and people need their flour before they can start baking.`

Mother Serene: `Try her workbench outside the workshop, before the upper bridge. She should be there.`

Caelan: `I’ll tell her what you’ve seen.`

Master Halden: `I’ll head back to the mill. You can find me there.`

Mother Serene: `Keep to the bank until you know what’s wrong, Caelan.`

Caelan: `We will.`

## mill_reunion

Caelan: `Halden’s mill has stopped. He came to the house looking for help.`

Mira: `Stopped completely?`

Caelan: `He said there wasn’t enough water reaching the wheel. He’s been along the bank, but he couldn’t see what was holding it up.`

Mira: `All right. I’ll come.`

Caelan: `He also asked whether I could help you.`

Mira: `Good. We may need another pair of hands.`

## mill_mechanism

Caelan: `Nothing’s caught between the paddles. The axle looks seated, too.`

Mira: `It is. Look along the supports—nothing’s moved there.`

Caelan: `So the wheel itself might be all right.`

Mira: `I think it is. Let’s see what’s reaching it.`

## mill_race

Caelan: `The brook’s still flowing. It’s only this channel that’s low.`

Mira: `Which means we follow it back to the intake.`

Caelan: `The north road?`

Mira: `Yes. Back over the upper bridge, then past the steps by Serene’s house. The intake’s along the river trail outside the village.`

## mill_diagnosis

Mira: `The wheel’s sound, but it isn’t getting enough water. We’ll follow the millrace back to its intake.`

Caelan: `Back over the upper bridge, then north past the steps by Serene’s house.`

Mira: `That’s right. Take the road out of the village. The intake’s along the river trail.`

## mill_warning

Mira: `Keep close once we’re out there. Animals come down to the water, and some of them won’t move aside for us.`

Caelan: `Is that why you fitted the arm rig?`

Mira: `Yes. The brace helps me steady the emitter, and I can still use my hand. Let’s hope all we need are the tools.`

Caelan: `All right.`

Mira: `And leave the sword sheathed while we’re walking. We’ll want our footing on that bank.`

## mill_reminder_encounter

Mira: `The intake is beyond the bend in the north trail. Stay close—we’ll keep an eye on the riverbank together.`

## mill_encounter_intro

Caelan: `That tree’s down across the water.`

Mira: `Look at the trunk. Something’s torn into it.`

Caelan: `Mira. Beyond it.`

Mira: `I see it.`

Caelan: `It brought that down?`

Mira: `With tusks that size… It must have. A limb could easily have washed into the intake.`

Caelan: `It’s coming this way.`

Mira: `Caelan, back towards me. Slowly.`

## mill_aftermath

Mira: `Caelan. Are you hurt?`

Caelan: `*steadies his breathing* Give me a moment.`

Mira: `All right.`

Caelan: `I thought I’d be able to use it.`

Mira: `You did use it.`

Caelan: `I couldn’t make the water work. I just kept pushing until something else came through.`

Mira: `Then we’ll look at that somewhere quieter. Right now, I want to know whether you can walk comfortably.`

Caelan: `Yes. I can.`

Mira: `Good. Keep it sheathed while we’re working. The intake’s just ahead.`

## mill_intake

Caelan: `*points to the limb* There. That limb’s wedged across the screen.`

Mira: `A fresh break, just like the tree by the path. That’s where it came from. Look how the water’s pressing it into the bars.`

Mira: `*disengages the brace’s powered supports* I’ll leave the joints free for the tools.`

Caelan: `We won’t pull it clear like that.`

Mira: `No. We’ll take the pressure off first.`

Caelan: `And the catch?`

Mira: `Under the water in that little stone recess. We’ll deal with it once the intake’s isolated.`

Caelan: `Tell me where you need me.`

Mira: `I’ll set the bypass below the screen. Keep clear while I turn it.`

## mill_prepare

Caelan: `Here?`

Mira: `*checks Caelan is clear* That’s fine. Give me a moment.`

## mill_prepare_done

Mira: `That’s taken the main flow off it. There’s still water sitting around the catch.`

## mill_spell_intro

Caelan: `It can’t fill again while the bypass is open?`

Mira: `No. There’s only what’s trapped there.`

Caelan: `I want to try moving it into the drain.`

Mira: `With the blade?`

Caelan: `Yes. Just this pocket.`

Mira: `All right. I’ll stand clear.`

## mill_encouragement_failed

Caelan: `*a small, surprised laugh* There. That went where I wanted it.`

Mira: `And I can see the catch now.`

Caelan: `It felt different. I could keep track of it.`

Mira: `You had time to do that here.`

Caelan: `I’d like to try it again.`

Mira: `When we’re back. I’d like to see it properly, too.`

Caelan: `I wish I’d had this when I was working on the Venns’ gate.`

Mira: `Was the water still flowing against it?`

Caelan: `Yes. I was trying to hold it back.`

Mira: `That’s a much heavier job. We’ve stopped anything feeding this pocket.`

Caelan: `I know. I still wanted to manage it.`

Mira: `Then show me what you were trying when we’ve got some time. For now, you’ve cleared what I needed.`

## mill_repair

Mira: `I can release the catch now. Take the rope.`

Caelan: `Ready.`

Mira: `Keep the limb clear of the bars when I lift the screen.`

Caelan: `Tell me when.`

Mira: `Now.`

## mill_repair_done

Mira: `Hold it there… Good. You can let go.`

Caelan: `The water’s coming through.`

Mira: `I’ll collect the tools and secure the brace. Then we’ll check the wheel.`

Caelan: `Mira?`

Mira: `Yes?`

Caelan: `Thank you for bringing it out today.`

Mira: `I’m glad I did.`

Caelan: `I want to spend some time learning it. Without something trying to knock me over.`

Mira: `Come by the workshop when you’re ready. Bring your book.`

## mill_reminder_verify

Mira: `Let’s get back to the wheel and check it’s taking the load. Then we can give Halden some good news.`

## mill_halden_waiting

Master Halden: `I’ll leave the checking to you and Mira. There’s still grain waiting to be milled.`

## mill_verify

Caelan: `*smiles* It’s turning.`

Mira: `Steadily, too. The wheel’s taking the load again.`

Caelan: `Do we need to check anything else?`

Mira: `That’s enough. Let’s tell Halden what was caught.`

## mill_resolution

Master Halden: `There you are. I heard the wheel start.`

Mira: `A limb was jammed across the intake screen. We cleared it.`

Caelan: `The wheel itself was all right.`

Master Halden: `Good. Was there much damage up there?`

Mira: `There was a ridgeback tusker by the path. It must have brought the tree down.`

Master Halden: `*his expression changes* You ran into it?`

Caelan: `We got past it together.`

Mira: `It’s gone. But the bank’s torn up—warn anyone carrying loads along there.`

Master Halden: `I will. Are you both all right?`

Mira: `Yes.`

Master Halden: `Sit down if you need to. The flour can wait another few minutes.`

Caelan: `We’re all right. I think I’d rather hear it running for a bit.`

Master Halden: `So would I.`

Master Halden: `*after a moment* Thank you, both of you. People can get their flour again.`

Caelan: `I should get back home.`

Mira: `I thought you were going down to the brook.`

Caelan: `I was. But I said I’d help Mother this morning. There’s still time to see what she needs.`

Mira: `All right. I need to get these tools back before someone brings me another repair.`

Caelan: `See you later.`

Mira: `Bring your book when you come.`

## mill_sanctuary_return

Mother Serene: `There you are. How’s the mill?`

Caelan: `Turning again. There was a limb caught across the intake. Mira and I cleared it.`

Mother Serene: `Good. Halden must be relieved.`

Caelan: `He stood listening to it for a while.`

Mother Serene: `*smiles* I can imagine.`

Caelan: `Is there anything left to do here? I said I’d help this morning.`

Tomas: `The table's clean. Can you bring over the cups?`

Mother Serene: `You could bring those clean ones over, if you have a moment.`

Caelan: `Yep, on it.`

## mill_sanctuary_setting

Caelan: `Are these the clean ones?`

Mother Serene: `Yes. Three beside the jug, please. The rest can go on the shelf.`

Caelan: `This one's still warm.`

Mother Serene: `It was nearest the hearth. I left them there to dry.`

Tomas: `Do I get the chipped one?`

Mother Serene: `Only if you choose it, Tomas.`

Tomas: `*relieved* I'll take the other one.`

Caelan: `Is this close enough?`

Mother Serene: `Yes. Thank you, love.`
