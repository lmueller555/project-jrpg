# What the Village Carries — dialogue

Captured through the actual game from the shipped PR1 frame checkpoint.

## Departure

**Liam:** You're leaving the point on, aren't you?

**Mira:** The point stays. I'm fastening the handle, remember?

**Liam:** I know. I was checking.

**Tomas:** (amused) You've checked twice.

**Mira:** And he can check again when he comes back. I'll leave the drawing beside it.

**Liam:** Do we have to wait here?

**Mira:** You don't have to stand here watching me. I'll be getting on with my work, too.

**Liam:** What else is everyone making?

**Caelan:** Halden's busy with the festival flour. We could go past the mill.

**Liam:** Can we see the wheel?

**Tomas:** It's turning again. You'll see it from the path.

**Mira:** Stay on that path. Ask Halden where it's safe to stand.

**Liam:** I will.

**Caelan:** And we stay together, like your mum asked.

**Liam:** Can I look at the frame once more?

**Mira:** Of course. There it is. I'll take care of it.

**Liam:** All right. See you later!

**Mira:** See you, all three of you.

## Mill Arrival

**Master Halden:** There you are again, Caelan. And you've brought an audience.

**Caelan:** Only a small one. Is this a bad time?

**Master Halden:** You'll be out of my way there. Liam, keep this side of me.

**Liam:** Is it all right if I watch?

**Master Halden:** From there, yes. You can see the wheel well enough.

**Liam:** Does it grind everything?

**Master Halden:** Grain. What were you planning to put in it?

**Liam:** Nothing.

**Tomas:** The wheel turns the machinery inside. The grain goes between the stones.

**Liam:** So the water doesn't go in the flour?

**Tomas:** No, it turns the wheel, and then the shaft—

**Master Halden:** You've answered him, Tomas. Let him look for a moment.

**Tomas:** Oh. Right.

**Liam:** It's bigger up close.

**Master Halden:** And stronger than it looks. That's why you keep to the path.

**Liam:** Mira said that.

**Master Halden:** Then she's saved me repeating myself.

**Caelan:** You've had a busy afternoon.

**Master Halden:** A useful one, at least. Better than standing here looking at a wheel that won't move.

**Tomas:** I'm glad it's working.

**Master Halden:** So am I, lad. So am I.

## Mill Flour

**Liam:** Is all that flour for tomorrow?

**Master Halden:** A fair bit of it. Bread first, then whatever else people have promised to bake.

**Liam:** Buns?

**Master Halden:** I supply the flour. You'll have to ask the people doing the baking.

**Tomas:** He knows which table he's going to first.

**Liam:** Mum said I have to eat something else before the sweet things.

**Caelan:** She may have noticed what you chose last time.

**Liam:** There was bread in it.

**Master Halden:** (chuckling) Best take that argument to your mother.

**Liam:** Who gets the bread you make?

**Master Halden:** The bakers make it. But at the shared tables, whoever comes can have some.

**Liam:** Even if they didn't bring anything?

**Master Halden:** Yes. Someone brings bread, someone brings a pot of something. Someone puts out the benches.

**Caelan:** And some people have their hands full at home.

**Master Halden:** There'll be loaves to send round to people who can't come, too.

**Liam:** Do they still get the nice kind?

**Master Halden:** (glancing at him) What kind were you planning to leave them?

**Liam:** I didn't mean— I just wondered.

**Master Halden:** I know. Yes, they get good bread.

**Tomas:** I can help with the benches tomorrow.

**Caelan:** You offered Serene already.

**Tomas:** I haven't forgotten.

**Master Halden:** Good. Leave the sacks to the people with carts. You've done enough moving them for me today.

**Caelan:** We'll let you get on. Thank you for letting us stop.

**Master Halden:** Off you go. And Liam—ask before you go near any machinery.

**Liam:** I did ask.

**Master Halden:** You did. Keep doing it.

## Venn Arrival

**Mrs. Venn:** Caelan! Come in. There's room if you don't all stand in the doorway.

**Caelan:** We were looking at the preparations. Would a short visit be welcome?

**Mrs. Venn:** A short one, yes. It's good to have something else to talk about.

**Mr. Venn:** And something else to do. I've had enough of waiting beside that road.

**Tomas:** You got the cart back.

**Mr. Venn:** I did. It's been useful already. I'm glad that business is settled.

**Venn child:** Liam, come and look.

**Liam:** What are you making?

**Venn child:** Something to hang up. I picked those colors.

**Liam:** I'm making a lantern. It's a tower.

**Venn child:** Where is it?

**Liam:** Mira's got it. The handle tipped at first, but we moved it.

**Tomas:** Liam helped tie the frame.

**Liam:** And there's going to be stairs on the paper. All the way round, I think.

**Venn child:** Can you show me when it's finished?

**Liam:** Yes. What are the colors for?

**Venn child:** Mum's tying the pieces together. That one goes next.

**Liam:** This one?

**Venn child:** No, the yellow. Then the blue.

## Venn Preparations

**Mrs. Venn:** You can finish choosing this part. Then you're resting.

**Venn child:** I am resting.

**Mrs. Venn:** (gently) You know what I mean.

**Liam:** Do you want both yellow ones together?

**Venn child:** One here. One at the other end.

**Mrs. Venn:** All right. I'll keep them there while I tie this.

**Venn child:** Can I do that bit?

**Mrs. Venn:** Let me manage the ties today. You tell me where each piece goes.

**Mr. Venn:** It'll look well over the window. We can see it from here.

**Liam:** Are you putting it up here, then?

**Venn child:** Can I carry it to the festival?

**Mrs. Venn:** We'll see how you feel tomorrow.

**Venn child:** You said that already.

**Mrs. Venn:** I know, love. I still can't promise.

**Caelan:** If you stay here, would you want someone to bring food over?

**Mrs. Venn:** That would be welcome. I'll speak to Serene when we know.

**Mr. Venn:** We can hear some of the singing from the path. If it's a quiet evening, we might sit by the open door.

**Venn child:** I want to see the lights.

**Mrs. Venn:** I know. We'll do what we can.

**Liam:** I can still show you the tower.

**Venn child:** With the stairs?

**Liam:** If I can make them fit.

**Tomas:** There's quite a lot of stairs in the drawing.

**Venn child:** (a small laugh) I want to see it.

**Caelan:** We'll give you some quiet now. Thank you for letting us visit.

**Mrs. Venn:** Thank you for coming. You three enjoy your afternoon.

**Mr. Venn:** Elira will be getting her lantern ready, if you're going that way.

**Liam:** Can we see hers?

**Caelan:** We can ask. She may like some company.

## Elira Welcome

**Elira:** You've brought company, Caelan. Come in.

**Caelan:** Hello, Elira. We were hoping we wouldn't interrupt.

**Elira:** Only my thoughts. They can wait a little.

**Tomas:** Liam's been showing us how to build a tower.

**Liam:** Mira helped.

**Elira:** Did she? What sort of tower?

**Liam:** A lantern one. You can carry it, and it's got a point on top.

**Elira:** And where are you keeping this tower?

**Liam:** At the workshop. Mira's making sure the handle stays where we put it.

**Elira:** A sensible place to leave it, then.

**Liam:** I wanted it higher, but it'd be too heavy.

**Tomas:** We talked about that for a while.

**Liam:** It's still tall.

**Elira:** You'll have to show me when the paper's on. I should like to see it.

**Liam:** I will. Is that yours?

**Elira:** It is. Caelan brought it down for me earlier.

## Elira Lantern

**Liam:** Have you had it a long time?

**Elira:** A good while. It needs a little looking after.

**Liam:** Is the handle loose?

**Elira:** Not this one. I've checked it, too.

**Tomas:** (smiling) Liam understands about handles now.

**Liam:** Mine's in the right place. Mira said.

**Elira:** Then we'll both be able to carry them tomorrow.

**Liam:** Are you making a new one as well?

**Elira:** No. This is the one I want to take.

**Caelan:** The one for your husband.

**Elira:** Yes.

**Liam:** Did he make it?

**Elira:** He helped me mend it. He liked telling me how he would have done it.

**Tomas:** Was he right?

**Elira:** Sometimes. I tried not to make too much of those occasions.

**Caelan:** You said he came to hear you sing.

**Elira:** That was his excuse.

## Elira Memory

**Liam:** Did he sing too?

**Elira:** Unfortunately.

**Tomas:** (laughing) That bad?

**Elira:** He got the last line wrong every year.

**Liam:** On purpose?

**Elira:** Some years I wondered.

**Caelan:** Did you tell him?

**Elira:** Of course I told him. He said he knew the words perfectly well.

**Tomas:** And then he sang it wrong again?

**Elira:** Louder. As though I might have misheard him.

**Liam:** What did he say instead?

**Elira:** He put a garden gate in it once. I asked him which song he thought we were singing.

**Caelan:** What did he say?

**Elira:** (laughing) That he couldn't remember the words with me interrupting.

**Tomas:** Did anyone else notice?

**Elira:** Everyone standing near us. Some of them began waiting to hear what he'd do.

**Liam:** Was he cross?

**Elira:** He pretended to be. He always joined in again.

**Liam:** I wish I'd heard it.

**Elira:** (her voice softening) Yes. So do I.

## Elira Company

**Liam:** Did I say something wrong?

**Elira:** No, love.

**Liam:** You went quiet.

**Elira:** I was remembering him.

**Liam:** But you were laughing.

**Elira:** I was. It was funny.

**Liam:** And it made you sad?

**Elira:** A little. I'd have liked to hear him get it wrong again.

**Caelan:** Would you like us to stay a little?

**Elira:** I would. Thank you.

**Tomas:** We aren't in a hurry.

**Elira:** That's a pleasant thing to hear from you, Tomas.

**Tomas:** (a little embarrassed) I'm not always in a hurry.

**Elira:** No. Not always.

**Elira:** How did your mother get on with all that paper, Liam?

**Liam:** Ada got her sleeve stuck to some.

**Tomas:** I held the paper while she tried to get it off.

**Liam:** Mum said we should let it dry.

**Elira:** (smiling) Was Ada willing to wait?

**Liam:** She kept looking at it.

**Caelan:** Your mum had us helping before we'd got through the door.

**Elira:** Good for her.

**Liam:** Are you still going to sing tomorrow?

**Elira:** Yes. I mean to.

**Liam:** Will people sing with you?

**Elira:** Some will. Some would rather listen.

**Caelan:** We'll come and find you.

**Elira:** I'd like that. Liam, bring your tower where I can see it.

**Liam:** All right. I'll show you the stairs as well.

## Elira Goodbye

**Tomas:** I could carry your lantern down for you tomorrow.

**Elira:** Thank you. I'd like to carry this one myself.

**Tomas:** All right.

**Elira:** You can mind the door when I bring it out. It catches when I've got both hands full.

**Tomas:** I can do that.

**Caelan:** Then we'll see you tomorrow. Will you be all right here?

**Elira:** Yes, Caelan. Thank you for the company.

**Liam:** Thank you for showing me your lantern.

**Elira:** You're welcome, love. I'm glad you asked.

**Liam:** Mira hasn't put the paper on mine yet.

**Elira:** There's still time today. Let her finish the part she's doing.

**Tomas:** We'll leave you to get ready.

**Elira:** Go on, then. And don't let that tower get away from you.

**Liam:** I'll keep hold of it.

## Reflection

**Liam:** Will Elira really sing tomorrow?

**Caelan:** She said she would.

**Liam:** Even if it makes her miss him?

**Caelan:** Yes. I think she wants to.

**Tomas:** We can go and listen.

**Liam:** I want to show her the tower.

**Caelan:** We will. Let's give her some quiet now.

**Liam:** All right.
