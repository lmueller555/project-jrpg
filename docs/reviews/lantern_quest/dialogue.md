# A Light of His Own — PR1 dialogue

Captured through the actual game. Only MQ01–MQ03 prerequisites were seeded.

## Invitation

**Tomas:** There you are. I thought I'd have to come and fetch you.

**Caelan:** You said you wanted me to look at the lanterns.

**Tomas:** I do. At the school. Liam's mother asked if we could help this afternoon.

**Caelan:** Both of us?

**Tomas:** She said another pair of hands would be welcome. I told her you'd probably come.

**Caelan:** Probably?

**Tomas:** (a little sheepishly) I hadn't asked you yet.

**Caelan:** You could have started with that.

**Tomas:** Will you come?

**Caelan:** Yes. Let me check with Serene first.

**Mother Serene:** I heard you. Go along. I have that request to write, and the children will be glad of the help.

**Caelan:** Are you sure you don't need anything?

**Mother Serene:** A little quiet, perhaps.

**Tomas:** Come on. We can manage that somewhere else.

**Mother Serene:** (amused) Thank you, Tomas.

**Caelan:** All right. Let's see what they're making.

## Classroom

**Liam’s mother:** There you are! I was beginning to wonder whether I'd have to come and borrow you myself.

**Caelan:** Sorry. The visit ran on a little.

**Liam’s mother:** I heard. You're here now. Come in, both of you.

**Finn:** Can we light them yet?

**Liam’s mother:** Tomorrow evening, Finn. We need to finish making them first.

**Finn:** Mine's nearly finished.

**Tessa:** You haven't put the bottom in.

**Finn:** Oh.

**Liam’s mother:** Tessa, would you show him which piece it is? Thank you.

**Ada:** I've got some on my sleeve.

**Liam’s mother:** The paste? Let it dry, love. It'll come off more easily.

**Ada:** But now it's stuck to the paper.

**Tomas:** Here. Keep your arm still. I'll hold the other end.

**Pippa:** Is there any blue left?

**Rowan:** I'm using that.

**Pippa:** All of it?

**Rowan:** Only this bit. You can have the rest.

**Liam’s mother:** Thank you, Rowan. Caelan, Liam's at the preparation table. He's been waiting to show someone his drawing.

**Liam:** Mum, I wanted to show Mira.

**Liam’s mother:** You can show Caelan first. Mira will still get to see it.

**Caelan:** May I?

**Liam:** Yes. But I haven't finished the top.

## Design

**Caelan:** That's quite a roof.

**Liam:** It's a tower.

**Tomas:** I thought those were stairs.

**Liam:** They are. That's how you get to the top.

**Caelan:** Have you built this one with your blocks?

**Liam:** A smaller one. Daisy knocked it down.

**Tomas:** Was she helping?

**Liam:** No. She wanted the piece at the bottom.

**Liam:** This one's going to have a handle. So she can't reach it.

**Caelan:** How big are you imagining it?

**Liam:** This big.

**Liam’s mother:** Something you can carry all evening, remember.

**Liam:** I can carry lots of things.

**Liam’s mother:** I know. I'd like to see you carrying this one yourself tomorrow.

**Liam:** I am carrying it myself.

**Caelan:** You didn't carry one last year?

**Liam:** Mum let me hold hers for a bit. But this one will be mine.

**Liam’s mother:** He usually wants to get straight to the food and games. This year he asked to make his own.

**Liam:** I still want to go to the games.

**Tomas:** So do I.

**Liam:** Did yours look like this when you were little?

**Caelan:** Mine was square.

**Tomas:** Most lanterns are square.

**Caelan:** He asked about mine.

**Liam:** Mine can be a tower, though?

**Liam’s mother:** Let's ask Mira what we can make from those frame pieces she offered us.

## Permission

**Liam’s mother:** Would you two take him over to the workshop? Mira said she could help with a frame.

**Caelan:** Of course. Are you expecting him back here?

**Liam’s mother:** Come and find me afterward. I'll be here putting things away, or at home once we're finished.

**Liam’s mother:** If you stop to see the other preparations, stay together. Don't go out of the village.

**Tomas:** We will.

**Liam:** Can I take my drawing?

**Liam’s mother:** That's what you made it for. Let this corner dry first.

**Caelan:** I'll lift it from this side—

**Liam:** Wait. I was doing that.

**Caelan:** (drawing his hand back) All right. Tell me when you want a hand.

**Liam:** I can get it.

**Liam’s mother:** Mira will want to hear your idea from you, too. Give her time to finish what she's doing.

**Liam:** I know.

**Liam’s mother:** Have a good time, love.

**Liam:** I will. Come on!

**Caelan:** We're coming. The three of us go together.

## Workshop

**Mira:** Hello, you three. Is that the drawing I've been hearing about?

**Liam:** It's a tower lantern.

**Mira:** Come and show me. There's room on this side.

**Tomas:** We almost added a second tower on the way here.

**Mira:** Did you? Let me see the first one before you put me out of business.

**Liam:** That's next year.

**Mira:** Ah. Plenty of warning, then.

**Caelan:** His mother said you had some pieces we could use.

**Mira:** I put the light strips aside. The short ends should do for the roof.

**Liam:** So it can have a roof?

**Mira:** Let's have a look. Where does the light go?

**Liam:** Inside. Here.

**Mira:** And where would the heat go?

**Liam:** Out the door.

**Tomas:** (laughing) He's got you there.

**Mira:** Then we'd better give it a chimney.

**Liam:** A real one?

**Mira:** An opening at the top. We can keep that pointed shape around it.

**Liam:** Can we keep the stairs?

**Mira:** We could draw them on the paper. Wooden stairs would make it heavier.

**Liam:** I'll draw them round this side. So you can see them.

**Mira:** That should work. Now, shall we try the frame?

## Test Shape

**Mira:** These strips will bend a little. They won't stand up on their own until we join them.

**Liam:** Do I hold this one?

**Mira:** Yes, near the middle. Caelan, can you steady the opposite side?

**Caelan:** Here?

**Mira:** A little lower. There.

**Tomas:** The roof's pulling it to one side.

**Liam:** I haven't let go.

**Mira:** I know. The top piece is longer on this side. See where it sticks out?

**Liam:** Can you cut that bit off?

**Mira:** I can. Or we could make the other side longer.

**Liam:** Then it'd be too big.

**Mira:** I think you're right. I'll trim it.

**Liam:** Does that mean it won't be a tower?

**Mira:** Look at your drawing. Which part makes it a tower to you?

**Liam:** The tall bit. And the point on top.

**Mira:** Those can stay. We just need both sides to match.

**Liam:** (relieved) All right.

## Assemble

**Mira:** We'll tie these corners first. The paper comes later.

**Tomas:** There's still enough room at the top for a handle.

**Mira:** Good. Leave the tie loose there until we've checked where it hangs.

**Liam:** Can I do a knot?

**Mira:** Of course. This corner. Keep hold of it while you pull.

**Liam:** It's moving.

**Caelan:** Would you like me to steady it?

**Liam:** Just that bit.

**Caelan:** Just this bit.

**Tomas:** Try pulling both ends together.

**Liam:** Like this?

**Mira:** That's it. I'll check each join before we lift it.

**Liam:** And then I can carry it?

**Mira:** Then we can see how it feels in your hand.

## Lift

**Mira:** Ready to try? Keep your other hand underneath at first.

**Liam:** I'm ready.

**Caelan:** There's room beside you. I'll stand back.

**Liam:** It's tipping!

**Mira:** Set it down gently. You've got it.

**Liam:** Did I break it?

**Mira:** No. The handle's a little off the middle. The frame's all right.

**Tomas:** We left that tie loose. We can move it.

**Liam:** This way?

**Mira:** A little. We'll try it there.

**Liam:** Will you hold the bottom while I lift it?

**Caelan:** Yes. Tell me when to let go.

**Liam:** Now. Slowly.

## Frame Ready

**Liam:** It's staying!

**Tomas:** Try turning a little.

**Liam:** Like this?

**Mira:** Gently. Yes, that's much better.

**Liam:** It looks like my drawing.

**Caelan:** It does.

**Liam:** Except the stairs.

**Tomas:** You haven't drawn them on yet.

**Liam:** Oh. Can we put the paper on now?

**Mira:** First I need to secure that handle. Then we can fit the paper without it moving around.

**Liam:** You won't change where it goes?

**Mira:** No. We found the right place together.

**Liam:** (pleased) I did that knot.

**Mira:** You did. It held when you lifted it, too.

**Liam:** Can you leave it where I can see it?

**Mira:** Right here on the table. I'll be careful with it.

**Caelan:** Would you like to keep the drawing beside it?

**Liam:** Yes. So you know where the stairs go.

**Mira:** I won't forget the stairs.
