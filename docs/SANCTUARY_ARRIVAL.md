**Status: Removed from current runtime.**

The old MQ03 worker-rescue arrival controller, injured-worker staging and mission handoff have been removed. The reserve remains an ordinary explorable place; the cave can be entered without accepting a rescue mission or automatically recruiting companions.

intact bridge. MQ01 and MQ02 remain playable with ordinary household and mill
framing; finishing MQ02 returns to exploration.

Historical review captures and the original text remain in Git history; they are not current reproduction instructions.
