## Narrative contract

Caelan is 18, Tomas 14 and Liam 8. The brothers already know the annual Festival of Light. Liam remembers its food, games and lights; this year he has asked to make and carry his own lantern. His first conscious exploration of its meaning is not a claim that he has never attended before, nor an established village-wide coming-of-age rule.

The invitation follows completion of MQ03, including the rescue and return resolution on new journeys. Serene stays with the written request and relief arrangements established with Iselin. Liam’s mother, Eldenmere’s teacher, hosts a special festival preparation session using the existing six-child classroom. Caelan helps within his familiar household and village relationships. Liam’s mother explicitly agrees to the workshop outing, asks the three to stay together inside the village, and expects them to find her afterward. This agreement will be honored in the full quest’s concluding return.

Liam wants a tower because he likes building with blocks. Mira asks him to explain his drawing, helps him preserve what matters to him and introduces manageable changes for weight, balance and ventilation. She retains craft expertise. Tomas contributes useful ideas; Caelan learns to wait for Liam to ask for help. Liam performs a small but real part of the work and asks for help when the handle test worries him.

The lantern stays unlit. The frame is secured and covered in later preparation scenes; this PR ends with a visible frame, a successful balance test and Liam’s pride in his own contribution. Its personal purpose is still open. There is no new Caelan spell, invented bereavement, festival ceremony, excavation authorization or hidden cosmological revelation in these scenes.

The preparation session occurs on the day before the festival. The four-PR MQ04 sequence precedes MQ05, the festival itself. The complete MQ04 targets a comfortable 45–60-minute first playthrough; PR1 is intended as roughly 10–15 minutes with movement and interaction. These are playtest targets, not measured claims or reasons to pad scenes.

## Player responsibility and pacing

`Speak with Tomas → attend the school preparation session → inspect Liam’s drawing → agree the outing with his mother → accompany Liam and Tomas to Mira → discuss and test the design → help join the frame → test the handle → finish the reaction scene.`

Each substantial exchange is player-paced. Walking, looking at the drawing, helping at the bench and speaking again provide breathing room between exchanges. Optional replies do not replace the essential consent, craft stages or final reaction. Canceling or leaving a conversation does not commit its ending.

## Part 2 — What the village carries

**Status:** Implementation script for the approved second segment of MQ04.

## Narrative contract

Caelan is eighteen, Tomas fourteen and Liam eight. Liam’s mother already agreed that the three may stop to see other preparations while staying together inside the village. Their role is to accompany a curious child and visit people they know. Conversations advance the quest without inventing a new repair, collection or adjudication task.

The authored route is Mira’s workshop → Halden at the working mill → the Venn cottage → Elira’s cottage. The unlit tower frame and its drawing stay together on Mira’s craft table. Mira secures the handle and returns to her own workbench. PR3 must collect the materials explicitly before using them at home.

Halden’s festival flour and the restored mill are existing facts. This segment establishes Eldenmere’s shared festival food tables and portions taken to people who cannot attend. The Venn decoration is a particular household choice. Their recovering child selects colors while a parent manages the ties, remains resting and has no guaranteed attendance tomorrow. The family will arrange any food delivery with Serene; Caelan receives no new delivery objective.

Elira’s same memorial lantern has already been retrieved in MQ01. Her husband’s inability to sing was already established; the recollection of his confidently wrong words adds personal detail. Elira can laugh and still miss him. She keeps the decision to carry the lantern herself. Tomas’s promise to mind her door and the group’s plan to find her at the festival are later relationship callbacks, not extra errands in this segment.

The eleven main exchanges are separately player-paced. Optional walking conversations and repeat replies cannot complete an objective. Canceling unread prose does not commit a visit. The final reflection occurs only after leaving Elira’s cottage through its real doorway.

This segment targets a comfortable 15–20 minutes with movement and conversation; this is a playtest target rather than a measured duration or a reason to pad dialogue. It ends with Liam’s questions still open. The domestic break, Serene’s theology, Liam’s lantern purpose, festival ceremony and excavation escalation remain later work.
