# Game I — CH01_MQ04: A Light of His Own

**Status:** PR1 implementation script for the approved four-PR quest plan.  
**Implemented scope:** MQ03 handoff, school preparation session, agreed outing with Liam, Mira’s workshop and a finished unlit lantern frame. MQ04 remains active at `frame_ready`; later scenes are not completed by this segment.  
**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md), [The Regent’s Road](CH01_MQ03.md), [implementation guide](../../LANTERN_QUEST.md).

## Narrative contract

Caelan is 18, Tomas 14 and Liam 8. The brothers already know the annual Festival of Light. Liam remembers its food, games and lights; this year he has asked to make and carry his own lantern. His first conscious exploration of its meaning is not a claim that he has never attended before, nor an established village-wide coming-of-age rule.

Tomas’s invitation continues MQ03’s last exchange. Serene stays with the reply she owes Aldric. Liam’s mother, Eldenmere’s teacher, hosts a special festival preparation session using the existing six-child classroom. Caelan helps within his familiar sanctuary and village relationships. Liam’s mother explicitly agrees to the workshop outing, asks the three to stay together inside the village, and expects them to find her afterward. This agreement will be honored in the full quest’s concluding return.

Liam wants a tower because he likes building with blocks. Mira asks him to explain his drawing, helps him preserve what matters to him and introduces manageable changes for weight, balance and ventilation. She retains craft expertise. Tomas contributes useful ideas; Caelan learns to wait for Liam to ask for help. Liam performs a small but real part of the work and asks for help when the handle test worries him.

The lantern stays unlit. The frame is secured and covered in later preparation scenes; this PR ends with a visible frame, a successful balance test and Liam’s pride in his own contribution. Its personal purpose is still open. There is no new Caelan spell, invented bereavement, festival ceremony, excavation authorization or hidden cosmological revelation in these scenes.

The preparation session occurs on the day before the festival. The four-PR MQ04 sequence precedes MQ05, the festival itself. The complete MQ04 targets a comfortable 45–60-minute first playthrough; PR1 is intended as roughly 10–15 minutes with movement and interaction. These are playtest targets, not measured claims or reasons to pad scenes.

## Planned four-PR arc

| PR | Segment | Character result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invitation, school, agreed outing and workshop frame | Liam is taken seriously and helps make something of his own. |
| 2 | A few village preparation encounters and Elira’s remembrance scene | Liam sees different personal meanings in the celebration. |
| 3 | Domestic break, craft frustration, brother conversation and Serene’s sanctuary scene | Experience gives Liam room for honest questions about memory and faith. |
| 4 | Liam chooses his purpose, finishes the lantern and explains it to his mother | He can participate with his own understanding; MQ04 closes. |

The “lantern for someone who has no one to carry one” custom remains a proposal for PR4. It is not established by this segment. MQ05’s detailed activities and inciting event remain later design work. Preserve the existing separation between the chapter-closing authorization and the Chapter 3 catastrophe unless deliberately revised.

## Player responsibility and pacing

`Speak with Tomas → attend the school preparation session → inspect Liam’s drawing → agree the outing with his mother → accompany Liam and Tomas to Mira → discuss and test the design → help join the frame → test the handle → finish the reaction scene.`

Each substantial exchange is player-paced. Walking, looking at the drawing, helping at the bench and speaking again provide breathing room between exchanges. Optional replies do not replace the essential consent, craft stages or final reaction. Canceling or leaving a conversation does not commit its ending.

meal and Caelan's exchange with Serene. Tomas and Serene remain in the room for
their replies; the brothers then leave through the hall for the school.
