# Game I — Chapter 1: Life in Eldenmere

**Status:** APPROVED OPENING STRUCTURE; details labeled PROPOSED or OPEN are not canon.  
**Version:** 1.0 (structure)  
**Parent:** [Act Outline](ACT_OUTLINE.md) · [Game I Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md) · [Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md)

## Approved chapter structure

Chapter 1 is the **welcoming, predominantly peaceful opening** of Game I. Players spend time living in **Eldenmere**, building attachment to the community and Caelen's relationships before the inciting catastrophe. The excavation disaster occurs in **Act I, Chapter 3**, not in Chapter 1.

- **Four mandatory main quests**, introducing Caelen, Serene, Tomas, Mira, Aldric, the sanctuary, everyday faith, practical magicks and technology, and the political dispute.
- **Five initial optional community quests** as flexible content ideas; the **optional-content model** is approved, but each specific side-quest premise remains PROPOSED.
- **The Festival of Returning Light** is the emotional high point of the opening.
- **Aldric receives a credible, sympathetic introduction**; he must earn the admiration he will later jeopardize.
- **Chapter end:** Serene learns that excavation near an ancient sanctuary has been authorized, then privately consults an old warning. This ends the chapter with concern rather than catastrophe.
- **Act I proceeds through three chapters:** Chapter 1 *Life in Eldenmere*; Chapter 2 *Beneath the Peace*; Chapter 3 *The Shattered Oath*.

Approximate pacing **targets**, not commitments: 60–90 minutes main narrative, with 30–60 minutes optional exploration/activities. Do not pad the game merely to meet a duration.

**Authority note:** Approving this chapter structure does not approve the precise character biographies, dialogue, town map, quest mechanics, story timings, or details of the coming disaster. The game's existing overarching restrictions still apply.

## Mandatory quests — approved sequence and functions

### MQ01 — A Day Like Any Other

**Premise:** Caelen starts the morning at Eldenmere's sanctuary. Serene asks him to deliver needed medicine or supplies to three households; Tomas accompanies him briefly.

**Purpose:** Let the player control Caelen quickly; introduce ordinary worship, service, village geography, family dynamics, and the sanctuary as a genuinely beneficial institution.

**Proposed staging:** Explore Caelen's room, sanctuary library and courtyard; deliver supplies; hear both grateful and skeptical villagers. The number of households, exact tasks, and tutorial interactions are adjustable.

**Emotional result:** Eldenmere is home rather than an exposition hub.

### MQ02 — A Little Trouble at the Mill

**Premise:** Caelen reunites with his childhood friend Mira after the watermill fails; together they investigate an obstructed channel outside the village and restore its operation.

**Purpose:** Provide a small, complete adventure; demonstrate Mira's engineering knowledge and Caelen's learned magicks; develop affectionate, credible differences over technological progress and inherited restrictions.

**Proposed staging:** A short woodland route, a mechanical/environmental obstacle, and optional low-threat encounters. The exact cause, solution, difficulty, and combat use are OPEN.

**Emotional result:** Mira and Caelen succeed through complementary strengths rather than one worldview defeating the other.

### MQ03 — The Regent's Road

**Premise:** Aldric visits Eldenmere. The village welcomes him, and Caelen helps with preparations. Aldric demonstrates fair, humane judgment before a measured disagreement with Serene over excavation policy.

**Purpose:** Establish why Caelen and the villagers admire Aldric; give both government development and religious protection a legitimate argument; introduce the covenant without lore dumping.

**Proposed staging:** Aldric resolves a small local dispute, acknowledges Caelen personally, and speaks privately or publicly with Serene. The exact dispute and the history between Caelen and Aldric are OPEN.

**Emotional result:** The future antagonist's character feels sincerely admirable in the present.

### MQ04 — The Festival of Returning Light

**Premise:** The village gathers for an observance of the belief that life force returns to the gods. Caelen helps Serene with preparations and shares a quiet moment with Tomas.

**Purpose:** Present faith through comforting everyday practice, celebrate community, and make the main relationships emotionally resonant.

**Proposed staging:** Lanterns, songs, games and local conversations. Tomas shares a desire to see the wider world; Caelen expresses contentment in Eldenmere. Exact symbols, festival doctrine, lines, and interactive activities are OPEN.

**Emotional result:** A warm, memorable high point before the conflict deepens.

## Optional community quests — proposed catalog

These are **ideas**, not commitments to quest count, rewards, or plot canon. Optional activities may add later dialogue callbacks but must not be required for understanding the core narrative.

| Working title | Candidate activity | Relationship / worldbuilding role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Miller's Ledger | Help resolve an ordinary mill supply dispute | Local economy and community cooperation |
| Lanterns for the Lost | Help households prepare memorial offerings | The lived meaning of death and returning aether |
| Tomas's Big Idea | Assist with a humble mechanical experiment | Tomas's curiosity and admiration for Mira |
| An Old Song | Recover missing verses of a festival hymn | Oral history and local religious culture |
| A Merchant's Burden | Help with village deliveries | Trade, roads, and regional tensions |

**Design rule:** Prioritize specific people and small payoffs over repetitive collection or combat. Optional content must not gate important plot evidence, essential relationship context, or later survival outcomes by default.

## Closing beat — the authorization letter

After the festival, Caelen helps Serene close the sanctuary. A messenger brings word that the government has **formally authorized excavation** near a protected sanctuary, escalating the dispute.

Serene is shaken but shares only that the conflict with the regent is becoming more serious. Alone, she retrieves and examines an inherited warning about disturbances beneath the mountains. **The player does not read the full warning.** The chapter closes with uncertainty, not an immediate disaster.

Her precise knowledge is limited: she does not know the cosmic catastrophe, the Great Sacrifice's full truth, or the identities of the four survivors.

## Character introductions — approved ages, other biography details OPEN

- **Caelen (Caelan):** **18 years old** at the game opening; Tomas's older foster brother. Local sanctuary acolyte/record keeper (working role); service-oriented and trusting, but observant. Give him agency beyond carrying documents.
- **Serene:** Loving guardian and community caretaker; sincere faith and a plausible reason for preserving limited warnings.
- **Mira:** Childhood friend and mechanically curious engineer; believes responsible development can improve lives.
- **Tomas:** **14 years old** at the game opening; Caelan's younger foster brother, restless and inventive; an autonomous person, not simply a future tragedy.
- **Aldric:** Respected ruler who has tangibly helped the region; his later moral failure should not erase his authentic achievements.

Caelan's age of **18** and Tomas's age of **14** are approved canon; see
[approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md). Other ages and biography details
remain **working proposals pending character approval** unless separately approved.

## Chapter pacing and prerequisites

- **Mandatory spine:** MQ01 → MQ02 → MQ03 → MQ04 → closing scene.
- **Optional activities:** Become available naturally as the village opens up; ideally remain available through the festival where continuity permits.
- **Player agency:** Free exploration and low-pressure activities between main sequences; avoid long mandatory exposition.
- **No catastrophe in Chapter 1:** First a meaningful ordinary life, then Chapter 2 warnings and exploration, then Chapter 3 disaster and departure.

## Exit conditions and continuity checks

Players should understand why Caelen loves Eldenmere, why Serene's order is useful to the community, how Mira differs from Caelen without becoming a caricature, why Tomas is loved, and why Aldric is respected. The conflict over sanctuary excavation should be understandable, but its true dangers and ancient origins remain concealed.

Mechanisms and magicks must obey conservation of aether. The festival's beliefs are in-world theology, not full confirmation of the ancient history. The four surviving Ancients remain hidden. Preserve the separate regional ending promised by the saga documents.
