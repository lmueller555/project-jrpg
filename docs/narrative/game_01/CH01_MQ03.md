# Game I — CH01_MQ03: The Regent's Road

**Status:** Implemented narrative script, revised 2026-11-06. The playable visit introduces Lucan and follows the direct route to Serene’s home. The former Venn cart section is removed.

**Scope:** Caelan welcomes Aldric and his guard, accompanies the visitors to a meal, hears the unresolved Elden Vale Sanctuary request, and speaks privately with Serene before Tomas's lantern invitation.

**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [MQ02's homecoming](CH01_MQ02.md), [Aldric's arrival](../../REGENT_ARRIVAL.md), [approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md), [regional surges](REGIONAL_SURGES.md), [Serene's planetary faith](../../world/ELDEN_VALE_FAITH.md), and [the sanctuary covenant](../../world/SANCTUARY_COVENANT.md).

## Narrative contract

The visit follows MQ02's acknowledged conclusion at home and Aldric's separate arrival cinematic. Caelan, eighteen, remains at home with Serene and his fourteen-year-old brother Tomas when control returns. Serene's short request separately accepts MQ03. The earlier cup-setting work and bow question are not repeated.

Aldric's party is Aldric, Lucan Rooke, his clerk and his driver. Lucan replaces the generic traveling attendant. He is Aldric's personal guard and an eventual companion, but does not join the playable party during this visit. His useful, unsolicited advice about Mira's new blade and his attention to the doorway introduce his confidence and protective habits. The guard's arrival artwork and town actor use the same approved identity. No new mentorship, weapon ability or combat tutorial is granted here.

The extraordinary lightning incidents and Aldric's relief visit are already established by the arrival. Only a handful of communities have been affected; their deeper cause remains unknown. Stone and transport shortages persist. Aldric belongs to Merovar's ruling family and is the reigning monarch's younger brother. Tomas's practical question reveals that relationship without assigning an unapproved dynasty or named monarch.

Elden Vale Sanctuary is the regional ridge site, separate from Serene’s home where she, Caelan and Tomas live. Its reserve includes a real natural aether confluence, a living memorial grove and buried foundations. Serene explains the communal keeping of the trees and ground, alongside the standing buildings. People tend the grove in remembrance; no particular tree, named family memorial or doctrine of individual souls in trees is established. The confluence belongs to the planetary cycle, not the Aethersphere, and its detailed workings remain unresolved.

Serene shares the public foundation plan and maintenance account. The historic discharge injured workers who opened a passage; its cause is unknown and its relationship to the recent lightning unproven. Aldric proposes a shallow supervised trial, stopping at masonry. They agree on surface inspection and obtaining records, with existing courtyard stone and crew meals offered immediately. The trial cut remains disputed. Neither the road nor the bridge is repaired by this conversation, and no excavation authorization is issued.

Caelan serves as guide, host and witness. He does not inspect receipts, adjudicate a hire, decide the engineering question or authorize the use of protected land. Mr. Venn remains in his own household life; MQ03 never borrows him or stages a cart dispute. Aldric's sincere relief work and Serene's practical assistance give Caelan reasons to care about both positions.

Selected delivery and action cues use literal asterisks in the game. The escaped asterisks below display those markers literally. Dialogue remains player-paced and cancelable; transient action and dialogue clocks are never saved as completed objectives.

## Critical path and player responsibility

`Serene's welcome request → meet Aldric and Lucan by the lower bridge → escort the party directly through the courtyard and hall → meal at Serene’s home → unresolved ridge discussion → private exchange with Serene → Tomas's lantern invitation.`

The four ordered objectives remain `meet_visitors`, `accompany_visit`, `welcome_party` and `speak_serene`. There is no road-stop, cart-release, receipt-reading or replacement errand between the greeting and Serene’s home. The relief wagon stays at the village approach while the driver joins the meal.

## S03 — Share the meal

Guide the four visitors through the existing courtyard entrance, hall at Serene’s home and dining-room door. The player keeps control during the walk. Aldric travels near Caelan and Lucan takes a protective trailing position. The relief wagon remains parked. Reaching the dining room with Aldric records `accompany_visit`; the meal waits until the same party is gathered inside. Tomas’s interest in the brake and Caelan’s breakfast callback make the household feel continuous. Lucan remains alert while eating. Finishing the welcome records `welcome_party`.

## S04 — The ridge request

After the meal has had room to finish, Caelan helps clear the table and Aldric opens the survey. Tomas remains nearby; his question concerns the real limits of royal authority. The grove’s cultural and natural significance comes before the uncertainty about the first cut. Serene opens the foundation plan and public account, and Aldric has time to read. The offered stone and meals help without supplying all the material he needs. Finishing this exchange commits only the discussion; the proposed excavation remains unresolved.

<!-- regent-script: discussion -->
- **Aldric:** “Before I lose you to the festival preparations, could we look at the survey for Elden Vale Sanctuary?”
- **Mother Serene:** “Of course. Caelan, let me move those plates.”
- **Caelan:** “I’ve got them.”
- **Aldric:** “The surveyors marked the nearer seam here. They believe it could supply stone for several of the repair crews.”
- **Mother Serene:** “What about the other quarries?”
- **Aldric:** “They are already mined day and night, but we can't keep up with the demand. The same thing that happened here to the bridge is happening across Merovar.”
- **Tomas:** “Couldn’t the crown send more?”
- **Aldric:** “I’ve asked.”
- **Tomas:** “Aren't you Her Majesty's brother? Why would she say no?”
- **Aldric:** “People haven’t been refusing me, Tomas. They still have to cut the stone and bring it. There are other communities waiting for the same loads.”
- **Aldric:** “A nearer source would give us more room to work. The proposed face is clear of the standing sanctuary walls.”
- **Mother Serene:** “The protected ground extends beyond those walls.”
- **Aldric:** “I know. I believe we can preserve the place while working the seam. I wouldn’t have brought you the proposal otherwise.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Aether gathers there naturally. People have cared for that place for generations. They tend the grove in remembrance of those they’ve lost.”
- **Mother Serene:** “The sanctuary buildings are part of that keeping. So are the trees, and the ground beneath them.”
- **Aldric:** “Then the plan needs to account for all of it.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Yes. And there is still a great deal we cannot account for.”
- **Mother Serene:** “These foundations continue into the hillside. The plan never shows where they end.”
- **Aldric:** “My engineer could supervise a shallow trial, away from the marked walls. If we encounter masonry, we stop.”
- **Mother Serene:** “We would already have opened the ground by then.”
- **Aldric:** “That is how we establish what is there.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Ordinarily, yes. But there’s an account I want your engineer to read first.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Workers opened a passage beneath the sanctuary. A discharge injured them. They closed the passage again.”
- **Aldric:** “\*reads for a moment\* Did they find what caused it?”
- **Mother Serene:** “They left no explanation.”
- **Aldric:** “Could it have anything to do with the recent lightning?”
- **Mother Serene:** “I can’t tell you that. This account is much older.”
- **Aldric:** “Then we need someone to examine the site.”
- **Mother Serene:** “We do. Your engineer and I can walk it together. Inspect the surface, compare it with the plan, and see what further records we can obtain.”
- **Aldric:** “And the trial cut?”
- **Mother Serene:** “I can agree to the inspection. I cannot endorse opening the ground yet.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Take the stone we set aside for the courtyard repairs.”
- **Aldric:** “You still need that work done.”
- **Mother Serene:** “We can put it off. I’ll arrange meals for the crew as well.”
- **Aldric:** “\*his expression softens\* Thank you. That will help.”
- **Mother Serene:** “But it won’t be enough.”
- **Aldric:** “No.”
- **Mother Serene:** “I know.”
- **Aldric:** “I’ll have my engineer speak with you. Please send for the records.”
- **Mother Serene:** “I will.”
- **Aldric:** “And when we’ve done the inspection, we’ll need to discuss the seam again.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Yes.”
<!-- /regent-script -->

## S05 — Caelan and Serene

Aldric steps aside to speak with his clerk before Caelan’s private exchange becomes available. Lucan remains with the visitors. Caelan reacts to Aldric’s visible relief at the offered stone, then admits that liking him makes the disagreement harder. Serene shares that discomfort without resolving it in a lecture. Tomas’s invitation supplies the spoken transition to MQ04. The final acknowledgment records `speak_serene`, completes MQ03 once and unlocks the separate lantern quest.

<!-- regent-script: aftermath -->
- **Caelan:** “\*quietly\* He looked relieved when you offered the stone.”
- **Mother Serene:** “There are people waiting for it.”
- **Caelan:** “I thought that might settle things for a while.”
- **Mother Serene:** “It settles what we can give him today.”
- **Caelan:** “And he’ll ask about the quarry again.”
- **Mother Serene:** “I expect so.”
- **Caelan:** “\*after a moment\* I liked him.”
- **Mother Serene:** “So do I.”
- **Caelan:** “That makes it harder.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Yes.”
- **Tomas:** “Caelan? When you’re finished, can you come and look at the lanterns?”
- **Caelan:** “What’s wrong with them?”
- **Tomas:** “Nothing. I want to show you.”
- **Mother Serene:** “Go on. I have a letter to write.”
- **Caelan:** “All right. I’m coming.”
<!-- /regent-script -->

## Current runtime state contract and continuity

The version-two `regent_quest` record stores a single committed `stage`: `idle`, `assigned`, `escorting`, `welcome`, `hosted`, `discussion` or `done`. The ordered objectives are unchanged. Dialogue pages, reading positions, action clocks, follow trails and routes are transient.

- `assigned` follows the fully acknowledged request from Serene.
- `escorting` follows the fully acknowledged greeting and records `meet_visitors`.
- `welcome` follows Aldric reaching the dining room at Serene’s home and records `accompany_visit`.
- `hosted` follows the completed meal and records `welcome_party`.
- `discussion` follows the completed ridge conversation, while its request remains unresolved.
- `done` follows the private exchange, records `speak_serene`, completes MQ03 and unlocks MQ04.

Version-one `met`, `dispute`, `releasing` and `escorting` checkpoints migrate to the direct `escorting` stage. The player can continue home with the same visitor party without playing a removed stop or receiving an invented cart settlement. Earlier and later checkpoints retain their corresponding progress. Completed journeys remain completed. No old save receives excavation authority, a new ability or a recruited Lucan.

The same Serene and Tomas actors transfer from MQ02's homecoming into the visit. The arrival must be seen before the visitors' town staging appears. Room-aware triggers cannot start from matching outdoor coordinates. Existing dining furniture and the clear doorway aisle support the seven diners. MQ04 later borrows Tomas and Serene explicitly, and MQ03 stops moving or posing each actor after that handoff. New Game releases visit ownership and restores the ordinary opening.

The surviving main-scene cues are `caelan_scabbard`, `meal_settle`, `pass_bread`, `tomas_eat`, `lucan_watch`, `open_survey`, `clear_plates`, `open_plan` and `open_account`. They select physical scene beats; they do not change the approved spoken lines or replace acknowledgment boundaries.

## Knowledge and later story work

The promised inspection, records, stone and meals are limited agreements. They do not themselves enact deliveries, restore the damaged crossing, open a quarry or identify the buried works. Formal excavation authorization and Serene's inherited private warning remain later chapter events.

No scene identifies Elden Vale Sanctuary or Serene’s home as the original sacrificial temple, reveals the Aethersphere's purpose, connects the confluence to an ancient machine, or settles the cause of the recent lightning. The public records justify caution without guaranteeing a catastrophe. Both Aldric and Serene remain sincere.

## Standing rule for future quest design

Start with Caelan's relationships, responsibilities, knowledge and choices, then choose a credible level of involvement. Supporting and witnessing other people's decisions can be meaningful gameplay when the scene gives the player room to speak, ask and notice. Do not add a collection or inspection task merely to keep the protagonist busy.
