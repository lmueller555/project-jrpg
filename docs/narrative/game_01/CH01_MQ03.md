# Game I — CH01_MQ03: The Regent's Road

**Status:** APPROVED REVISED NARRATIVE SCRIPT — documentation only. **The runtime dialogue assets and quest controller still use the previous version; this documentation change does not implement the revised scenes or the caravan arrival in gameplay.**

**Scope:** Caelan welcomes and guides Aldric's party, witnesses the regent settle a road-repair dispute, helps host the visitors, and hears Aldric and Serene discuss relief needs and disagree over a trial excavation within protected sanctuary ground.

**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [Mira's mill quest and the arrival boundary](CH01_MQ02.md), [Sanctuary Covenant](../../world/SANCTUARY_COVENANT.md), [approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md). Caelan is 18 and Tomas is 14. Aldric's age and exact biography remain unspecified. The road foreman, clerk and driver use role titles; their personal names and histories remain open.

## Narrative contract

Before the game opens, an extraordinary lightning surge shatters the road south from Eldenmere toward the next town. A handful of similar incidents have occurred elsewhere in the region. Aldric has visited a couple of the affected communities to provide aid. The incidents are serious local emergencies, not the later excavation catastrophe; their ultimate cause is unknown to these characters. The next town's name and the precise mechanics of the surges remain unspecified.

Aldric's visit was arranged by letter before MQ02's mill repair. He has come to oversee the relief and repair work and discuss a nearer source of stone with Serene. Regional demand for reconstruction has stretched the available supply; the request concerns damaged communities waiting for material, as well as the cost and difficulty of moving it. His intention to share the festival is part of the visit's human context. Neither the festival nor news of the repaired mill is its principal cause.

Caelan's involvement follows from his position in the sanctuary household and his knowledge of Eldenmere. Serene asks him to welcome the visitors and bring them through the sanctuary hall to the dining room. He is a guide and host. Serene and Tomas prepare the meal and seating inside that room. Aldric has responsibility for the road project; the foreman explains its requirements, Mr. Venn explains his own needs, and Aldric's clerk checks the hire note. Caelan has no receipt-inspection objective, legal investigative role or authority over the settlement. His one contribution during the dispute is confirming that Halden's mill is taking deliveries again, which he knows firsthand from MQ02.

The visit has room for ordinary human conversation before its political business. Tomas is curious and a little nervous. Serene considers food, seating and the visitors' comfort. Aldric shows practical interest in the mill and takes responsibility for an extra road cost without treating Mr. Venn's time as public property. The foreman is managing delayed material and paid labor, not acting maliciously. Mr. Venn is frustrated and eager to get on with his work; he does not become an audience for a moral lesson.

The later disagreement concerns a stone seam inside the protected boundary of an old ridge sanctuary, a separate site from Eldenmere's inhabited sanctuary. The covenant covers the hillside and buried works as well as the standing buildings. Serene openly shares an old plan showing foundations extending into the hillside and a maintenance account recording workers injured by an unexplained small discharge after opening a passage, which they closed again. These are public records, distinct from the inherited warning she privately consults at the chapter's end. They give her a concrete reason for caution without telling her the site's full extent, function or relationship to the recent lightning.

Aldric proposes a shallow trial cut under his engineer's supervision, away from known walls, stopping if old masonry appears. Serene cannot establish that the first cut would avoid the buried works. She agrees to surface inspection and offers courtyard stone already set aside, meals for the crew, a boundary walk with the engineer and help obtaining further records. These useful agreements do not meet the region's full material need or settle the disputed trial excavation. Neither the southern road nor the other damaged communities are repaired by this conversation.

**No formal excavation authorization is issued in MQ03.** That event remains the post-festival chapter-closing beat. There is no hidden-warning reveal, buried-machine discovery, new magical ability, combat, grand political ruling by Caelan or invented old mentorship between him and Aldric.

Brief italic action and delivery cues appear in selected dialogue lines. They leave room for warmth, fatigue and uncertainty without assigning an emotion to every sentence. The future implementation should keep ordinary dialogue live and cancelable, with room to pause between exchanges.

## Critical path and player responsibility

`Sanctuary preparations → meet visitors by the brook → accompany Aldric → witness the cart dispute → see the cart released and the crew resume work → return through the sanctuary hall to the dining room → welcome and meal → unresolved excavation discussion → personal exchange with Serene.`

The approved arrival belongs at the end of [MQ02](CH01_MQ02.md); its presentation is separate from accepting MQ03 and remains unimplemented. MQ02 completion makes MQ03 available. Finishing Serene's preparations exchange accepts it. Ordered objectives remain `meet_visitors`, `accompany_visit`, `welcome_party` and `speak_serene`. The dispute is an event during the visit, not a player investigation task. Completion follows the final personal exchange with Serene; festival preparation is the story transition, while MQ04 remains separate work.

## S01 — Preparations at the sanctuary

Speak with Serene after MQ02's arrival boundary. She and Tomas are inside the dining room: Tomas arranges chairs while she prepares food and cups at the counter. Finishing the exchange accepts MQ03 and sends Caelan to meet the visitors. Leaving it unread accepts nothing.

- **Caelan:** "Was something wrong with the chair over there?"
- **Tomas:** "It was facing the sun."
- **Caelan:** "Now it's facing the wall."
- **Tomas:** "Yes, I noticed."
- **Mother Serene:** "Caelan, would you meet our visitors by the brook? Bring them in here when they're ready. I'd rather not leave all this unattended."
- **Caelan:** "Of course. How many are coming?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Four, according to the letter. I've prepared for six."
- **Caelan:** "Is he here about the southern road?"
- **Mother Serene:** "That, and the stone for the repairs. He asked to speak with me about the old sanctuary on the ridge."
- **Tomas:** "Do we have to bow when he gets here?"
- **Mother Serene:** "A greeting is enough."
- **Tomas:** *relieved* "Good. I didn't want to be the only one doing it."
- **Mother Serene:** "Tomas, leave the chair there for now. Help me with these cups."

## S02 — Meet the visitors

Meet Aldric's party at the village approach by the brook. Caelan introduces himself and speaks about the repaired mill from firsthand experience. Finishing the greeting records `meet_visitors`; the same Aldric actor then accompanies him.

- **Caelan:** "Regent Aldric? Mother Serene sent me."
- **Aldric:** "Ah. Thank you. Have we kept her waiting?"
- **Caelan:** "She's still getting everything ready."
- **Aldric:** *with a small, relieved laugh* "Then perhaps our timing isn't quite as bad as I feared."
- **Caelan:** "I'm Caelan. I'll show you the way."
- **Aldric:** "Thank you, Caelan. I heard Halden was having trouble with his mill."
- **Caelan:** "We've just got it working again."
- **Aldric:** "You were helping him?"

- **Aldric:** "Any damage?"
- **Caelan:** "Nothing serious."
- **Aldric:** *his concern easing* "Good. He'll have enough work before the festival without that."

## S03 — The cart dispute

Raised voices at the road-repair staging area cause Aldric to stop. Mr. Venn's one-day cart hire has expired. Short stone deliveries amid the regional reconstruction demand have delayed the haul and left the foreman trying to retain transport while paid workers wait. Aldric's clerk checks the hire note within the adults' discussion. The player never inspects the note or determines the settlement. After the last line, show the cart being released, Mr. Venn leaving on his delivery, and the crew resuming other preparation work. The road remains damaged; releasing the cart settles only the expired hire. Then continue the visit.

- **Mr. Venn:** "No. Listen to me. I agreed to a day."
- **Road foreman:** "We spent half of it waiting for enough stone to make the haul."
- **Mr. Venn:** "I know. I was here."
- **Road foreman:** "Then you know why we haven't finished."
- **Mr. Venn:** *his temper rising* "And you know why I need my cart back!"
- **Road foreman:** "My lord. I hadn't seen you there."
- **Aldric:** "Please, go on. What was agreed?"
- **Mr. Venn:** "The cart for yesterday. Back by evening."
- **Road foreman:** "For the stone haul. The delivery was short. The other repair crews are waiting on the same supply."
- **Aldric:** "Was there an agreement to keep it longer?"
- **Road foreman:** "No. I expected we'd be finished before he needed it."
- **Clerk:** "The hire note gives yesterday. There isn't an extension."
- **Mr. Venn:** "My grain's ready. I've already lost time waiting for the mill."
- **Aldric:** "Is it taking deliveries again?"
- **Caelan:** "Yes. Halden's working through what was waiting."
- **Mr. Venn:** "Which is why I'd like to get there before everyone else does."
- **Aldric:** "The hire has ended. Return the cart."
- **Road foreman:** "Then I'll need another hire, or another day. The men still need paying while they wait."
- **Aldric:** "Put the additional cost in the road account."
- **Road foreman:** "That leaves less for the upper section."
- **Aldric:** *rubbing his brow* "I understand. Send me the revised figures. We'll deal with that separately."
- **Aldric:** "What can the crew do while you arrange the transport?"
- **Road foreman:** "We can prepare the bedding along the lower stretch. It changes the order of the work, but we can start there."
- **Aldric:** "Then do that. Mr. Venn, would you consider another hire once your delivery is finished?"
- **Mr. Venn:** "Perhaps. I've other things waiting."
- **Aldric:** "Then it will need another agreement."
- **Mr. Venn:** "Thank you. And—well. I wasn't trying to hold up the road."
- **Aldric:** "I didn't think you were."
- **Mr. Venn:** *exhaling* "Right. Can I take it now?"
- **Road foreman:** "Yes. We'll clear the way."

## S04 — Welcome and a meal

Bring the party through the existing sanctuary entrance and hall into the dining room. The arrival completes `accompany_visit`; Serene welcomes them, Tomas asks about the journey, and Caelan helps pass the food. Let this scene finish before opening the excavation discussion. Finishing it records `welcome_party`.

- **Mother Serene:** "There you are. Sit down before it all goes cold."
- **Aldric:** "I'm sorry. We stopped at the road crew."
- **Mother Serene:** "Is everything all right?"
- **Aldric:** "It should be now. There'll be another account for me to read tomorrow."
- **Mother Serene:** "Eat something first. You can worry about tomorrow afterward."
- **Tomas:** "Did you come all the way over the ridge in that wagon?"
- **Aldric:** "Most of the way."
- **Tomas:** "Most?"
- **Aldric:** "I walked the steep part."
- **Tomas:** *surprised* "You walked?"
- **Aldric:** *laughing* "After sitting in it all morning, I was glad to."
- **Driver:** "He made better time than we did, too."
- **Mother Serene:** "Have some bread. All of you. Caelan, would you pass that plate?"
- **Caelan:** "Here you are."

## S05 — The unresolved request

Aldric and Serene speak after the meal, with Caelan nearby as a member of the household helping clear the table. Serene has brought the public plan and maintenance account for the old ridge sanctuary. She shares both openly. Caelan's one question seeks an explanation of the proposed work; he is not asked to assess engineering, interpret the records or decide between them.

The dialogue makes a useful but limited agreement: surface inspection, a boundary walk, sharing and seeking records, courtyard stone and crew meals. The shallow trial cut remains disputed. No formal excavation authorization or excavation-start flag is set. No cutting or opening of the ground begins at the ridge during this quest.

- **Aldric:** "Thank you for the meal. Before I lose you to the festival preparations, may we look at the ridge survey?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Of course. I've brought what we have about the old sanctuary. Let me move these cups."
- **Caelan:** "I'll take them."
- **Aldric:** "The nearer seam is here. The surveyors believe we could reach it without going near the standing walls."
- **Mother Serene:** "Inside the sanctuary boundary."
- **Aldric:** "Yes. I haven't forgotten your letter."
- **Mother Serene:** "I know you haven't. But the covenant covers the hillside and the works beneath it too. The walls we can see are only part of what we've agreed to care for. That keeping belongs to all of us. I can't give it away on my own."
- **Mother Serene:** "In our faith, we receive life and return it to the gods. These places were entrusted to the living in the same spirit. I owe that keeping to the people who come after us."
- **Aldric:** "And the stone we can use is already promised to repair crews across the region. More can come from farther away, but every longer haul ties up a cart another crew needs. The supply is stretched as it is."
- **Mother Serene:** "The southern road still has people cut off from their usual route. I understand why you want another source."
- **Aldric:** "That surge broke stone we thought would outlast us. Now every damaged crossing and stretch of road needs material at once. A nearer quarry could make a difference well beyond Eldenmere."
- **Mother Serene:** "There is something here your engineer should read before deciding where to cut. This is an old maintenance account."
- **Aldric:** "May I?"
- **Mother Serene:** *passes him the account* "The workers opened a passage beneath the sanctuary. A small discharge injured them. They couldn't explain what caused it. They closed the passage again."
- **Aldric:** *reads for a moment* "Does it say where the discharge came from?"
- **Mother Serene:** "No. Or how far the passage went. I wish it did."
- **Aldric:** "Can you tell whether it has anything to do with the lightning we've been seeing?"
- **Mother Serene:** "I can't. This tells us what happened to those workers. It doesn't explain the recent surges."
- **Caelan:** "May I ask what the work would involve?"
- **Aldric:** "A small cut, with my engineer present. If we encounter old masonry, we stop."
- **Mother Serene:** *unfolds the old plan* "These foundations disappear into the hillside. We don't know where your first cut would meet them."
- **Aldric:** "Then help us establish that. I've come through two other communities waiting for repairs. They need an answer too."
- **Mother Serene:** "Take the stone we've set aside for the courtyard. I'll have food prepared for the crew, and I'll walk the boundary with your engineer."
- **Aldric:** *his expression softens* "Thank you. That will help. But it won't give us enough."
- **Mother Serene:** *quietly* "I know. I'm asking you to investigate ground we have reason to distrust. I owe you my help in doing that."
- **Aldric:** "The trial would be shallow, clear of every wall marked here. I'm asking to find out what stone is there."
- **Mother Serene:** "And I can't tell you that the unmarked ground is clear. Let us inspect the surface together. Your engineer can have copies of both records, and I'll request any further plans and accounts the order holds."
- **Aldric:** "You're agreeing to the inspection, then?"
- **Mother Serene:** "On the surface, yes. I am not endorsing the trial cut. That account concerns an opening too; we don't yet know what made it dangerous."
- **Aldric:** *looks back at the plan* "I'll have my engineer speak with you. And please send for those records."
- **Mother Serene:** "I will. We'll arrange the stone and the meals as well."
- **Aldric:** "Thank you. I still need an answer about the seam."
- **Mother Serene:** "I know. I haven't put your request aside."

The old account establishes a limited historical hazard; it does not identify a buried machine, explain the site's workings or establish a connection to the lightning. Serene's practical contributions are sincere assistance. Aldric's concern for the waiting communities remains sincere even though they do not reach agreement on excavation.

- **Caelan:** "Was he angry with you?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Some of it, I think."
- **Caelan:** "He looked tired."
- **Mother Serene:** "It's been a difficult journey. There are people waiting for him to make things better."
- **Caelan:** "You still have more records to find?"
- **Mother Serene:** "I hope so. I'll send the request while the meal is cleared."
- **Caelan:** "I liked him."
- **Mother Serene:** "So do I."
- **Tomas:** "Caelan? Can you come and look at the lanterns?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Go and help him. We still have a festival to get ready for."
- **Caelan:** "I'm coming."

## Current runtime state contract and continuity

**The following describes the existing runtime contract, retained for future implementation and save compatibility. It does not indicate that the revised dialogue or MQ02 arrival has been implemented.** The optional version-one `regent_quest` journey record stores a single committed `stage`: `idle`, `assigned`, `met`, `dispute`, `releasing`, `escorting`, `welcome`, `hosted`, `discussion` or `done`. The controller owns the visit's staging and checks the stage against quest progress. Dialogue page positions, routes and animation clocks are transient.

- `assigned` follows acceptance at Serene's preparations scene.
- `met` follows the completed greeting; `meet_visitors` is recorded.
- `dispute` stages the road disagreement.
- `releasing` follows the completed settlement and shows it taking effect.
- `escorting` continues the visit after the cart is released.
- `welcome` follows the route through the courtyard and hall into the dining room and records `accompany_visit` there; the retained `courtyard` station key is a compatibility alias.
- `hosted` follows the meal and records `welcome_party`.
- `discussion` follows the existing unresolved excavation exchange; the revised exchange above still requires a runtime update.
- `done` follows the personal exchange, records `speak_serene` and completes MQ03.

Unfinished dialogue never accepts a task, completes an objective or issues permission. A read settlement and its released cart should not become an unread dispute after loading. The same Mr. Venn actor used in MQ01 participates in this visit, and prior household deliveries, Mira's completed adventure and the working mill remain intact.

Aldric's visit must not grant formal excavation authorization or expose Serene's inherited warning. His acceptance of help and Serene's consent to surface inspection do not authorize the trial cut or open a quarry. Caelan's admiration and his relationship with Serene can coexist at the close. No optional reply is required for understanding either character's position.

After completion, Serene and Tomas remain at the dining room's preparation stations under the visit controller's staging ownership until MQ04 takes over. New Game releases the visit's ownership and restores the normal sanctuary opening; completion does not resume their pre-visit routines in the meantime.

## Standing rule for future quest design

Start with Caelan's relationships, responsibilities, knowledge and choices, then choose a credible level of involvement. A local fact he knows firsthand can matter without making him an official investigator. Supporting and witnessing other people's decisions can be meaningful gameplay when the scene gives the player room to speak, ask and notice. Do not add a collection or inspection task merely to keep the protagonist busy.
