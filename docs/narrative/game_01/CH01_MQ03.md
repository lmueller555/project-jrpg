# Game I — CH01_MQ03: The Regent's Road

**Status:** Implementation script for the approved revised quest.  
**Scope:** Caelan welcomes and guides Aldric's party, witnesses the regent settle a road-work dispute, helps host the visitors, and hears Aldric and Serene disagree over preliminary excavation.  
**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [Mira's mill quest](CH01_MQ02.md), [approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md). Caelan is 18 and Tomas is 14. Aldric's age and exact biography remain unspecified. The road foreman, clerk and driver use role titles; their personal names and histories remain open.

## Narrative contract

Caelan's involvement follows from his position in the sanctuary household and his knowledge of Eldenmere. Serene asks him to welcome the visitors and bring them to the courtyard. He is a guide and host. Aldric has responsibility for the road project; the foreman explains its requirements, Mr. Venn explains his own needs, and Aldric's clerk checks the hire note. Caelan has no receipt-inspection objective, legal investigative role or authority over the settlement. His one contribution during the dispute is confirming that Halden's mill is taking deliveries again, which he knows firsthand from MQ02.

The visit has room for ordinary human conversation before its political business. Tomas is curious and a little nervous. Serene considers food, seating and the visitors' comfort. Aldric shows practical interest in the mill and takes responsibility for an extra road cost without treating Mr. Venn's time as public property. The foreman is managing delayed material and paid labor, not acting maliciously. Mr. Venn is frustrated and eager to get on with his work; he does not become an audience for a moral lesson.

The later disagreement concerns Aldric's proposed survey of a nearer stone seam within protected land near an old sanctuary. Serene explains that the covenant extends beneath the buildings, acknowledges the limits of her knowledge, and offers to seek records and a joint review. Aldric's frustration, apology and weariness coexist with the fair judgment the player has just witnessed. They part courteously with the request unresolved.

**No formal excavation authorization is issued in MQ03.** That event remains the post-festival chapter-closing beat. There is no hidden-warning reveal, buried-machine discovery, new magical ability, combat, grand political ruling by Caelan or invented old mentorship between him and Aldric.

Brief parenthetical delivery cues appear in selected dialogue text. These convey relief, surprise, concern, laughter or frustration without assigning an emotion label to every line. Ordinary dialogue remains live and cancelable; the people can pause without the scene rushing to its next objective.

## Critical path and player responsibility

`Sanctuary preparations → meet visitors by the brook → accompany Aldric → witness the cart dispute → see the cart released and the crew resume work → return through the sanctuary hall to the dining room → welcome and meal → unresolved excavation discussion → personal exchange with Serene.`

MQ02 completion makes MQ03 available. Finishing Serene's preparations exchange accepts it. Ordered objectives are `meet_visitors`, `accompany_visit`, `welcome_party` and `speak_serene`. The dispute is an event during the visit, not a player investigation task. Completion follows the final personal exchange with Serene; festival preparation is the story transition, while MQ04 remains separate work.

## S01 — Preparations at the sanctuary

Speak with Serene after MQ02. Tomas is arranging dining-room chairs while she prepares food and cups at the counter. Finishing the exchange accepts MQ03 and sends Caelan to meet the visitors. Leaving it unread accepts nothing.

- **Caelan:** "Was something wrong with the chair over there?"
- **Tomas:** "It was facing the sun."
- **Caelan:** "Now it's facing the wall."
- **Tomas:** "Yes, I noticed."
- **Mother Serene:** "Caelan, would you meet our visitors by the brook? Bring them in here when they're ready. I'd rather not leave all this unattended."
- **Caelan:** "Of course. How many are coming?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Four, according to the letter. I've prepared for six."
- **Tomas:** "Do we have to bow when he gets here?"
- **Mother Serene:** "A greeting is enough."
- **Tomas:** "(relieved) Good. I didn't want to be the only one doing it."
- **Mother Serene:** "Tomas, leave the chair there for now. Help me with these cups."

## S02 — Meet the visitors

Meet Aldric's party at the village approach by the brook. Caelan introduces himself and speaks about the repaired mill from firsthand experience. Finishing the greeting records `meet_visitors`; the same Aldric actor then accompanies him.

- **Caelan:** "Regent Aldric? Mother Serene sent me."
- **Aldric:** "Ah. Thank you. Have we kept her waiting?"
- **Caelan:** "She's still getting everything ready."
- **Aldric:** "(with a small, relieved laugh) Then perhaps our timing isn't quite as bad as I feared."
- **Caelan:** "I'm Caelan. I'll show you the way."
- **Aldric:** "Thank you, Caelan. I heard Halden was having trouble with his mill."
- **Caelan:** "We've just got it working again."
- **Aldric:** "You were helping him?"
- **Caelan:** "With Mira. There was a limb caught at the intake."
- **Aldric:** "Any damage?"
- **Caelan:** "Nothing serious."
- **Aldric:** "(his concern easing) Good. He'll have enough work before the festival without that."
- **Driver:** "We're ready here, my lord."
- **Aldric:** "Then let's not keep our hosts waiting."

## S03 — The cart dispute

Raised voices at the road staging area cause Aldric to stop. Mr. Venn's one-day cart hire has expired, but delayed stone and waiting labor have left the foreman short of transport. Aldric's clerk checks the hire note within the adults' discussion. The player never inspects the note or determines the settlement. After the last line, show the cart being released, Mr. Venn leaving on his delivery, and the crew resuming other preparation work. Then continue the visit.

- **Mr. Venn:** "No. Listen to me. I agreed to a day."
- **Road foreman:** "We spent half of it waiting for the stone."
- **Mr. Venn:** "I know. I was here."
- **Road foreman:** "Then you know why we haven't finished."
- **Mr. Venn:** "(his temper rising) And you know why I need my cart back!"
- **Road foreman:** "My lord. I hadn't seen you there."
- **Aldric:** "Please, go on. What was agreed?"
- **Mr. Venn:** "The cart for yesterday. Back by evening."
- **Road foreman:** "For the stone haul. The delivery was delayed."
- **Aldric:** "Was there an agreement to keep it longer?"
- **Road foreman:** "No. I expected we'd be finished before he needed it."
- **Clerk:** "The hire note gives yesterday. There isn't an extension."
- **Mr. Venn:** "My grain's ready. I've already lost time waiting for the mill."
- **Aldric:** "Is it taking deliveries again?"
- **Caelan:** "Yes. Halden's working through what was waiting."
- **Mr. Venn:** "Which is why I'd like to get there before everyone else does."
- **Aldric:** "The hire has ended. Return the cart."
- **Road foreman:** "Then I'll need another hire, or another day. The men still need paying while they wait."
- **Aldric:** "Put the additional cost in the road account."
- **Road foreman:** "That leaves less for the upper section."
- **Aldric:** "(rubbing his brow) I understand. Send me the revised figures. We'll deal with that separately."
- **Aldric:** "What can the crew do while you arrange the transport?"
- **Road foreman:** "We can prepare the bedding along the lower stretch. It changes the order of the work, but we can start there."
- **Aldric:** "Then do that. Mr. Venn, would you consider another hire once your delivery is finished?"
- **Mr. Venn:** "Perhaps. I've other things waiting."
- **Aldric:** "Then it will need another agreement."
- **Mr. Venn:** "Thank you. And—well. I wasn't trying to hold up the road."
- **Aldric:** "I didn't think you were."
- **Mr. Venn:** "(exhaling) Right. Can I take it now?"
- **Road foreman:** "Yes. We'll clear the way."

## S04 — Welcome and a meal

Bring the party through the existing sanctuary entrance and hall into the dining room. The arrival completes `accompany_visit`; Serene welcomes them, Tomas asks about the journey, and Caelan helps pass the food. Let this scene finish before opening the excavation discussion. Finishing it records `welcome_party`.

- **Mother Serene:** "There you are. Sit down before it all goes cold."
- **Aldric:** "I'm sorry. We stopped at the road crew."
- **Mother Serene:** "Is everything all right?"
- **Aldric:** "It should be now. There'll be another account for me to read tomorrow."
- **Mother Serene:** "Eat something first. You can worry about tomorrow afterward."
- **Tomas:** "Did you come all the way over the ridge in that wagon?"
- **Aldric:** "Most of the way."
- **Tomas:** "Most?"
- **Aldric:** "I walked the steep part."
- **Tomas:** "(surprised) You walked?"
- **Aldric:** "(laughing) After sitting in it all morning, I was glad to."
- **Driver:** "He made better time than we did, too."
- **Mother Serene:** "Have some bread. All of you. Caelan, would you pass that plate?"
- **Caelan:** "Here you are."

## S05 — The unresolved request

Aldric and Serene speak after the meal, with Caelan nearby as a member of the household helping clear the table. His one question seeks an explanation of the proposed work. He is not asked to decide between them. No permission or excavation-start flag is set.

- **Aldric:** "I've looked at the survey again. The proposed cuts are well clear of the sanctuary buildings."
- **Mother Serene:** "They're still inside the protected boundary."
- **Aldric:** "I know. That is why I'm asking."
- **Mother Serene:** "And I'm trying to explain why the distance from the buildings doesn't settle it. The covenant protects the ground beneath them too."
- **Aldric:** "The stone the crew was waiting for comes from three days away. There is a nearer seam on the survey."
- **Mother Serene:** "Within that boundary."
- **Aldric:** "Yes. I need someone to explain what it protects."
- **Mother Serene:** "I can tell you what the covenant requires. I can't give you a complete account of why every boundary was drawn."
- **Aldric:** "That is what I keep being told."
- **Mother Serene:** "I know."
- **Aldric:** "Meanwhile, every additional journey costs money that could have repaired something else."
- **Mother Serene:** "I understand that it's costing more."
- **Aldric:** "More money. More time. Work left unfinished elsewhere."
- **Mother Serene:** "Yes."
- **Aldric:** "(frustration showing) Then I need more than another letter telling me to wait."
- **Aldric:** "I'm sorry. You have answered my letters."
- **Mother Serene:** "(quietly) I'm frustrated too. I would like to give you something more useful."
- **Caelan:** "May I ask what the work would involve?"
- **Aldric:** "A preliminary excavation to examine the seam. The surveyors propose working in the ridge, clear of the buildings."
- **Mother Serene:** "But within the protected ground. That is the part we still disagree about."
- **Mother Serene:** "I will request the relevant records and ask for a joint review. Your surveyors should be able to put their questions directly to the order."
- **Aldric:** "How soon could that happen?"
- **Mother Serene:** "I can send the request today. I can't promise when they'll answer."
- **Aldric:** "(wearily) Everyone needs more time."
- **Mother Serene:** "I know you're tired of hearing it."
- **Aldric:** "Please send it. And let me know when you have a reply."
- **Mother Serene:** "I will. I am still not endorsing excavation on what we know today."
- **Aldric:** "I understand your answer. Thank you for hearing me out."

## S06 — Caelan and Serene

Aldric steps away courteously. Caelan asks about the disagreement and admits that he liked the visitor. Serene acknowledges her own respect for Aldric. Tomas calls about the lanterns, providing the transition toward the festival. Finishing this exchange records `speak_serene` and completes MQ03.

- **Caelan:** "Was he angry with you?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Some of it, I think."
- **Caelan:** "He didn't seem angry earlier."
- **Mother Serene:** "He had an answer he could give Mr. Venn."
- **Caelan:** "And you couldn't give him one."
- **Mother Serene:** "(after a moment) Not the one he wanted. I still owe him the answer I promised."
- **Caelan:** "I liked him."
- **Mother Serene:** "So do I."
- **Tomas:** "Caelan? Can you come and look at the lanterns?"
- **Mother Serene:** "Go and help him. We still have a festival to get ready for."
- **Caelan:** "I'm coming."

## State and continuity

The optional version-one `regent_quest` journey record stores a single committed `stage`: `idle`, `assigned`, `met`, `dispute`, `releasing`, `escorting`, `welcome`, `hosted`, `discussion` or `done`. The controller owns the visit's staging and checks the stage against quest progress. Dialogue page positions, routes and animation clocks are transient.

- `assigned` follows acceptance at Serene's preparations scene.
- `met` follows the completed greeting; `meet_visitors` is recorded.
- `dispute` stages the road disagreement.
- `releasing` follows the completed settlement and shows it taking effect.
- `escorting` continues the visit after the cart is released.
- `welcome` places the party in the courtyard and records `accompany_visit`.
- `hosted` follows the meal and records `welcome_party`.
- `discussion` follows the unresolved excavation exchange.
- `done` follows the personal exchange, records `speak_serene` and completes MQ03.

Unfinished dialogue never accepts a task, completes an objective or issues permission. A read settlement and its released cart should not become an unread dispute after loading. The same Mr. Venn actor used in MQ01 participates in this visit, and prior household deliveries, Mira's completed adventure and the working mill remain intact.

Aldric's visit must not grant excavation authorization or expose Serene's inherited warning. Caelan's admiration and his relationship with Serene can coexist at the close. No optional reply is required for understanding either character's position.

After completion, Serene and Tomas remain at the dining room's preparation stations under the visit controller's staging ownership. MQ04 will take over that staging when implemented. New Game releases the visit's ownership and restores the normal sanctuary opening; completion does not resume their pre-visit routines in the meantime.

## Standing rule for future quest design

Start with Caelan's relationships, responsibilities, knowledge and choices, then choose a credible level of involvement. A local fact he knows firsthand can matter without making him an official investigator. Supporting and witnessing other people's decisions can be meaningful gameplay when the scene gives the player room to speak, ask and notice. Do not add a collection or inspection task merely to keep the protagonist busy.
