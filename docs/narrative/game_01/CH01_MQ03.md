**Status:** Implemented through the Sanctuary arrival checkpoint. The revised post-meal emergency exposes Aldric’s secret deployment and Lucan’s surprise. The Sanctuary arrival cutscene remains later work.

## Narrative contract

The visit follows MQ02's acknowledged conclusion at home and Aldric's separate arrival cinematic. Caelan, eighteen, remains at home with Serene and his fourteen-year-old brother Tomas when control returns. Serene's short request separately accepts MQ03. The earlier cup-setting work and bow question are not repeated.

Aldric's party is Aldric, Lucan Rooke, his page and his driver. Lucan replaces the generic traveling attendant. He is Aldric's personal guard and an eventual companion, but does not join the playable party during this visit. His useful, unsolicited advice about Mira's new blade and his attention to the doorway introduce his confidence and protective habits. The guard's arrival artwork and town actor use the same approved identity. No new mentorship, weapon ability or combat tutorial is granted here.

The extraordinary lightning incidents and Aldric’s relief visit are already established by the arrival. Repairs are needed across Merovar; their deeper cause remains unknown. Stone and transport shortages persist. Aldric belongs to Merovar’s ruling family and is the reigning queen’s younger brother. Tomas’s practical question reveals that relationship without assigning a dynasty or personal name.

Elden Vale Sanctuary is the regional ridge site, separate from Serene’s home. It was deliberately built near a natural aether confluence to guide the region’s dead back to the planet and to remember them. This is an active societal responsibility entrusted to the caretakers. Mining within the reserve would disrupt their work. The confluence belongs to the planetary cycle; the scene does not explain the buried works or assign souls to the memorial trees.

Since the bridge incident, the caretakers have heard growling within the cave and closed its entrance. Aldric has secretly sent an advance crew to establish Crown control of the working ground and prepare mining. His public request conceals that deployment. The survivor’s arrival reveals the operation; Lucan was not told and is visibly taken aback. The cause and identity of the creature, the extent of the crew’s work and any causal connection to the bridge remain for later scenes.

Caelan’s involvement grows from the emergency and his relationships. He volunteers to help and brings Mira for her equipment and experience. Serene treats the survivor, with Tomas and the driver helping at home. Aldric, Lucan, the page, Caelan and Mira make for the Sanctuary. Lucan remains a story escort rather than joining the combat roster. Mr. Venn is not borrowed and no cart dispute returns.

Selected delivery and action cues use literal asterisks in the game. The escaped asterisks below display those markers literally. Dialogue remains player-paced and cancelable; transient action and dialogue clocks are never saved as completed objectives.

## S05 — The survivor and the secret deployment

Serene’s questions expose Aldric’s advance party and orders. Aldric confirms that the dispatch preceded his visit. Lucan’s surprise is explicit; his ignorance is not an excuse for Aldric’s conduct. He then asks the questions needed to help the stranded crew. The survivor does not identify the creature or explain why it attacked.

## Knowledge and later story work

The Sanctuary’s guidance of the dead and the disruption mining would cause are established responsibilities. Aldric’s advance deployment and concealment are deliberate. Lucan did not know about them. The report establishes an attack from within the cave and people left behind; it does not establish the miniboss’s identity, what caused the growling, whether mining provoked it, or its relationship to the bridge incident.

The regional Sanctuary remains distinct from Serene’s home and is not identified as the original sacrificial temple. No hidden planetary mechanism or Aethersphere function is disclosed. The future Sanctuary arrival scene will determine what the group finds and how MQ03 proceeds. Tomas’s lantern invitation must be placed after that sequence when its resolution is designed.

## Standing rule for future quest design

Start with Caelan’s relationships, responsibilities, knowledge and choices, then choose a credible level of involvement. Here he volunteers to help people in danger and brings someone whose practical abilities matter. He does not gain legal authority, resolve the Crown’s claim or receive a busywork collection task.
