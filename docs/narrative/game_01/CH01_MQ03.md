## Narrative contract

The visit follows MQ02's acknowledged conclusion at home and Iselin's separate arrival cinematic. Caelan, eighteen, remains at home with Serene and his fourteen-year-old brother Tomas when control returns. Serene's short request separately accepts MQ03. The earlier cup-setting work and bow question are not repeated.

Iselin's party is Iselin, Lucan Rooke, her page and her driver. Lucan replaces the generic traveling attendant. He is Iselin's personal guard and an eventual companion, and joins Caelan and Mira temporarily once the Sanctuary rescue is acknowledged. His useful, unsolicited advice about Mira's new blade and his attention to the doorway introduce his confidence and protective habits. The guard's arrival artwork and town actor use the same approved identity. No new mentorship, weapon ability or combat tutorial is granted here.

The extraordinary lightning incidents and Iselin’s relief visit are already established by the arrival. Repairs are needed across Merovar; their deeper cause remains unknown. Stone and transport shortages persist. Iselin belongs to the ruling family of a northern kingdom in Merovar and is the reigning queen’s younger sister. Tomas’s practical question reveals that relationship without assigning a dynasty or personal name.

Elden Vale Sanctuary is the regional ridge site, separate from Serene’s home. It was deliberately built near a natural aether confluence to guide the region’s dead back to the planet and to remember them. This is an active societal responsibility entrusted to the caretakers. Mining within the reserve would disrupt their work. The confluence belongs to the planetary cycle; the scene does not explain the buried works or assign souls to the memorial trees.

Since the bridge incident, the caretakers have heard growling within the cave and closed its entrance. Iselin has secretly sent an advance crew to establish Crown control of the working ground and prepare mining. Her public request conceals that deployment. The survivor’s arrival reveals the operation; Lucan was not told and is visibly taken aback. The cause and identity of the creature, the extent of the crew’s work and any causal connection to the bridge remain for later scenes.

Caelan’s involvement grows from the emergency and his relationships. He volunteers to help and brings Mira for her equipment and experience. Serene treats the survivor, with Tomas and the driver helping at home. Iselin, Lucan, the page, Caelan and Mira make for the Sanctuary. Lucan remains a story escort on the journey, then joins the combat roster temporarily for the cave rescue. Mr. Venn is not borrowed and no cart dispute returns.

The Sanctuary’s guidance of the dead and the disruption mining would cause are established responsibilities. Iselin’s advance deployment and concealment are deliberate. Lucan did not know about them. The report establishes an attack from within the cave and people left behind; it does not establish the miniboss’s identity, what caused the growling, whether mining provoked it, or its relationship to the bridge incident.

## Standing rule for future quest design

Start with Caelan’s relationships, responsibilities, knowledge and choices, then choose a credible level of involvement. Here he volunteers to help people in danger and brings someone whose practical abilities matter. He does not gain legal authority, resolve the Crown’s claim or receive a busywork collection task.

### Author continuity: the Echo Chamber rupture

The large aether surge responsible for the lightning storm and destroyed bridge
also disturbed the Sanctuary cave's natural aether confluence. A rupture opened
in the Echo Chamber, allowing subterranean monsters to climb into the upper
cave. This precedes the attack on the Crown's secretly dispatched crew.

The deeper region is later-game content. For now the opening cannot be entered.
Rising aether, fresh fractured limestone and climbing marks establish the visual
evidence. The inspection lets Mira identify the marks and the need for descent
equipment, while Caelan senses aether rising. They do not yet assert the full
causal chain. The opening remains after the miniboss is defeated; this does not
complete MQ03 or replace its later rescue/resolution scenes.
