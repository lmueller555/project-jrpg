# Game I — CH01_MQ04 PR2: What the Village Carries

**Status:** Implementation script for the approved second segment of MQ04.  
**Scope:** Continue from the unlit-frame checkpoint, visit Halden and the Venn family, spend time with Elira, and leave together. MQ04 remains active with its first four objectives complete.  
**Continuity:** [PR1 script](CH01_MQ04.md), [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [character facts](CHARACTERS.md), [quest guide](../../LANTERN_QUEST.md).

## Narrative contract

Caelan is eighteen, Tomas fourteen and Liam eight. Liam’s mother already agreed that the three may stop to see other preparations while staying together inside the village. Their role is to accompany a curious child and visit people they know. Conversations advance the quest without inventing a new repair, collection or adjudication task.

The authored route is Mira’s workshop → Halden at the working mill → the Venn cottage → Elira’s cottage. The unlit tower frame and its drawing stay together on Mira’s craft table. Mira secures the handle and returns to her own workbench. PR3 must collect the materials explicitly before using them at home.

Halden’s festival flour and the restored mill are existing facts. This segment establishes Eldenmere’s shared festival food tables and portions taken to people who cannot attend. The Venn decoration is a particular household choice. Their recovering child selects colors while a parent manages the ties, remains resting and has no guaranteed attendance tomorrow. The family will arrange any food delivery with Serene; Caelan receives no new delivery objective.

Elira’s same memorial lantern has already been retrieved in MQ01. Her husband’s inability to sing was already established; the recollection of his confidently wrong words adds personal detail. Elira can laugh and still miss him. She keeps the decision to carry the lantern herself. Tomas’s promise to mind her door and the group’s plan to find her at the festival are later relationship callbacks, not extra errands in this segment.

The eleven main exchanges are separately player-paced. Optional walking conversations and repeat replies cannot complete an objective. Canceling unread prose does not commit a visit. The final reflection occurs only after leaving Elira’s cottage through its real doorway.

This segment targets a comfortable 15–20 minutes with movement and conversation; this is a playtest target rather than a measured duration or a reason to pad dialogue. It ends with Liam’s questions still open. The domestic break, Serene’s theology, Liam’s lantern purpose, festival ceremony and excavation escalation remain later work.
