# Project JRPG — Game I Story Bible

## Current regent direction

[Regent Iselin](characters/ISELIN.md) is the current royal character. Her
kingdom is **Adamanthra** in northern Merovar, ruled by her elder sister,
[Queen Althea](CHARACTERS.md#queen-althea). Its capital is the party's eventual
destination; the capital's name remains open. The intended first half
to roughly two-thirds of the adventure accompanies her through affected regions
before her late-story Ice Reaper descent. Her sheet owns her background,
existing ice proficiency, concealed mission, motive and future identity; these
author-facing facts are not opening exposition or implemented later quests.
Historical act sketches do not override that approved direction.

**Geography revised, 2026-11-25:** The
[canonical world map](../world/WORLD_ATLAS.md#approved-world-map) places
Elden Vale and Eldenmere in far-southern Merovar, between two north–south
rivers in a separate watershed from the central Leth. The shared journey
therefore crosses a long western continent toward far-northern Adamanthra.
Exact routes, crossings and elapsed travel time remain open; this approval
does not adopt proposed destinations, regional bosses or side adventures.

## Creative brief (CANON)

Game I is the **first self-contained installment** in a multi-game saga. It
tells a largely **regional**, personal story with mature and morally complex
themes, a protagonist with meaningful motivation, and human decisions with
lasting consequences. Its developing conflict concerns elemental
Worldveins, Carcovia's artificial harvesting, the cannon that harvesting
feeds, and Iselin's transformation into the Ice Reaper. The required final
resolution is specified below; broader political outcomes and other Worldveins
remain open.

Its fantasy setting incorporates developing technology and the interdependent
elemental Worldveins. Approved geography is recorded in the [World Atlas](../world/WORLD_ATLAS.md);
opening characters, quests and selected religious practices are recorded in
[Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md) and its linked guides. Nation-level constitutions,
the wider period of industrialization and unapproved religious details remain open.

## Worldveins, Veinholds and the cannon

**Author-established direction, 2026-11-24; not implemented:** Elemental
Worldveins can occasionally experience brief natural imbalances. These can
produce natural disasters whose damage persists after the imbalance itself
passes. A brief imbalance does not imply brief or harmless consequences.

**Carcovia**, an industrious, shrewd kingdom on Serevin seeking expansion into
Merovar through aether-powered military weapons, artificially harvests
elemental aether at installations called **Veinholds**. Some opponents
disdainfully call them **"clots"**. This harvesting causes greater, sustained
disruption across interdependent Worldveins: effects
cannot be understood solely by examining one element in isolation. The physical
premises belong to [Foundational Lore](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md#1-foundations-of-existence).

The harvested elemental aether is combined to feed a huge cannon. Carcovia's
war ultimatum, backed by this weapon, forces the protective decision
that leads Iselin to create the Ice Dome. The artificial imbalance and the
weapon have a shared cause in harvesting; natural Worldvein variation remains
a distinct phenomenon. The barrier responds to this weapon-backed ultimatum.

### Middle-game direction

The northward journey develops the difference between temporary natural
variation and the greater sustained imbalance associated with Veinholds.
The party's observations, affected communities and Iselin's covert Crown
investigation lead into the harvesting conflict, the cannon threat and efforts
to prevent war. The precise discoveries, evidence, participants and order of
disclosure remain to be written. This direction creates no new quest IDs,
recruitment scenes or fixed act assignments.

Carcovia's identity and its goal of expansion into Merovar are established.
The [World Atlas](../world/WORLD_ATLAS.md#named-kingdoms) owns the kingdoms'
geography, adjective forms and naming intentions. Carcovia's ruler, capital,
dynasty, exact borders, detailed campaign aims and further motives remain open.
The first firing, any demonstration, exact ultimatum terms, harvesting-to-weapon
logistics, and the cannon's condition during the three surface years are not
selected. Reservoir shipping, an enemy goal of targeting the Ice Worldvein,
party sabotage of the cannon, and an upstream Worldvein route to the summit
cave remain unapproved
suggestions rather than reasons to fill gaps in the chronology.

shows Caelan and Mira sharing a brief, somber rest at a damaged wayside sanctuary,
then returns to the Eldenmere morning. The current intended location for the
eventual reprise is the threshold between Acts IV and V. This approves the
opening scene, not the full five-act plot, the final mechanism or anyone else's
fate. Later writing must earn and continue this moment; the late-game reprise
itself is not implemented yet. The expanded opening establishes evacuation

this frame. It leaves the crisis's cause and the final plan concealed.

The later author revision adds hand-holding, mechanical lamp restoration,
Caelan's evacuation flare and a closing focus on an anonymous bloody helmet
resting on a sword in stone. This approves a deliberate hint of an unidentified
death by that point in the journey, without assigning the loss to Tomas or

unimplemented later-act events.

## Approved act and companion structure

**Approved structural progression:** Act I comprises three chapters: **Chapter 1 — Life in Eldenmere** (welcoming and predominantly peaceful; five mandatory introductory quests plus optional worldbuilding activities), **Chapter 2 — Beneath the Peace** (exploration, warnings, and growing political tensions), and **Chapter 3 — The Shattered Oath** (excavation catastrophe, its personal consequences, and the decision to investigate). The disaster occurs in Chapter 3, **not Chapter 1**. The approved Chapter 1 structure, proposed scenes, and unresolved details are documented in [CHAPTER_01.md](CHAPTER_01.md). The detailed sequencing of Chapters 2–3 remains proposed.

**Approved companion introduction:** Lucan Rooke, the Armsmaster, enters during
Act I as Iselin's personal guard. Iselin later entrusts him to Caelan's party,
providing the approved basis for recruitment rather than a generic road escort.
His character foundation is recorded in the [Lucan sheet](characters/LUCAN.md);
the exact recruitment quest remains open.

**Established departure story beat:** Near or after the end of Act I, as the
party prepares to leave Eldenmere, Mira creates a bot to run her workshop
while she is gone. The [Mira departure beat](characters/MIRA.md#planned-departure-beat) records
the planned sprite work and workshop-management routines. Exact placement and whether this is a
cutscene, dialogue sequence or short quest remain open; no implementation is
implied by this entry.

**Approved companion introductions:** Rhea Sorel, the naturalist Aethermage,
enters early in Act II while investigating unusual aether behavior. Edran Hale,
the Sage, enters at the end of Act II when the party's account connects
injuries and failed protective workings he has encountered. They become major
sources of lore through their complementary observations during travel.
See [Rhea](characters/RHEA.md) and [Edran](characters/EDRAN.md).

The introduction windows above do not approve the surrounding historical
five-act outline. The current companion sheets own detailed foundations:
[Lucan](characters/LUCAN.md), [Rhea](characters/RHEA.md), and

### Royal authorization and betrayal

**Source / status:** General story direction approved by the author on
2026-11-24; future material, not implemented scenes. This section owns the
causal sequence. Detailed dialogue, political procedure and battlefield
logistics remain open.

1. Iselin returns with evidence gathered during the journey and warns her
   elder sister, Queen Althea of Adamanthra, of the threat behind Carcovia's cannon
   ultimatum. What the Crown already knows, the evidence she brings and the
   detailed threat assessment remain to be authored; neither sister is
   omniscient about Worldvein behavior or enemy decisions.
2. Althea privately authorizes the protective intervention and promises
   to support Iselin. Their prior trust and genuine affection must be developed
   before this promise is broken.
3. Iselin creates the dome. It protects substantial numbers of people, but
   closing the southern approach also strands people outside and causes real
   losses, including deaths. These consequences remain meaningful even when
   the later accusation is exposed as unfair.
4. The public accusation claims she defied Althea's orders, deliberately
   abandoned the Adamanthran army and acted against the kingdom. The claim of
   acting against royal authorization is false. Protective intent does not prove
   every judgment was sound, but the account of unauthorized treachery is not
   objective author truth.
5. Military leaders and bereaved families have legitimate reasons to demand
   answers. A truthful inquiry would examine the queen's authorization and
   royal decisions concerning protection, evacuation and withdrawal. Not all
   accusers are knowingly dishonest or secretly conspiring.
6. Althea could acknowledge her responsibility and defend Iselin, at the
   risk of losing military support and exposing government failures during a
   political and military crisis. She instead allows the false account to
   stand. This is her consequential choice, not an unavoidable procedure or
   proof that every alternative was impossible.
7. Althea secures exile instead of execution and privately regards this as
   saving her sister's life. Iselin asks her to publicly acknowledge the
   authorization; the queen refuses. Her claim of mercy does not erase her
   responsibility for permitting the injustice.
8. The broken promise, public condemnation and refusal of acknowledgment
   provide specific experiences for Iselin's later conscious brooding. Her
   eventual extension of blame to the whole population remains her own moral
   development, not a justified consequence of the betrayal.

**Illustrative logistics, still proposed:** A royal rear guard holds the
southern road during civilian evacuation; the queen approves sealing before
everyone returns. The unit, exact orders, who knows them, victims, timing and
alternatives are not selected. No named character's death is established.
The precise military leverage, first-shot circumstances and any negotiation or
armistice terms also remain to be authored; the illustrative withdrawal does
not establish when or how often the cannon fires, whether war is prevented,
or how it ends.

The [Iselin sheet](characters/ISELIN.md#her-sisters-promise-and-betrayal) owns
her relationship and response. The
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md#royal-authorization-and-the-public-accusation)
separates private truth, the public account and each participant's knowledge.
The [cast index](CHARACTERS.md#queen-althea) owns Althea's supporting entry;
the [World Atlas](../world/WORLD_ATLAS.md#named-kingdoms) owns Adamanthra's
geographical context. These approved names do not settle further royal-family
proposals or political institutions.

### Post-timeskip aftermath and the collapsing dome

**Author-established direction, 2026-11-24; not implemented:** The party spends
time in the post-timeskip world and deals with the consequences of its absence
before the final confrontation. The ice dome still covers the kingdom, but
sections are collapsing. Huge falling chunks have already destroyed parts of
the kingdom, and its remaining population faces further devastation.

A search party is dispatched offscreen to locate Iselin, believed to be the
only person capable of saving the kingdom from the collapsing dome. Rhea leads
this expedition. The appeal creates a tragic reversal: the woman unfairly
exiled is now the person the kingdom needs. Iselin kills the other expedition
members; Rhea survives as her ice-bound prisoner. Her personal response belongs
to the [Iselin sheet](characters/ISELIN.md#the-kingdoms-appeal-and-deliberate-collapse),
and Rhea's purpose and captivity to
[Rhea's sheet](characters/RHEA.md#expedition-leadership-and-captivity).

### Barrier drain and Ice Worldvein resolution

**Author-established direction, 2026-11-24:** During her approximately three
conscious years encased in the naturally Ice-aspected summit cave, Iselin
absorbs the accumulating Ice aether **from the barrier**. That accumulation
transforms her and drains the barrier, causing its existing failure. The cave
is naturally Ice-aspected, but its ambient aether is not substituted for the
barrier as the established source of this accumulation.

This draining and her later intention to deliberately collapse the dome onto
the entire kingdom are distinct developments. The accumulating drain does not
automatically establish that every earlier falling chunk was a consciously
directed attack. The exact onset, rates and physical failure sequence remain
open. The earlier proposed reopening/dismantling version of the post-exile
history is not adopted.

Defeating Iselin is necessary to release her accumulated excess Ice aether and
restore the Ice Worldvein's normal flow. This is an approved requirement of
the final confrontation. It does not determine whether she dies, repair all
other Worldveins, make the physical dome safe, or select anyone's final fate.
The [Iselin sheet](characters/ISELIN.md) owns her personal transformation and
choices; [Foundational Lore](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md#1-foundations-of-existence)
owns the elemental premises.

### Mountain search for Rhea

The player-facing sequence is:

1. Search for Rhea in the post-timeskip world and learn that she led the
   expedition sent to find Iselin.
2. Retrace its steps up the mountain through a dungeon segment.
3. Encounter the dead expedition members along the route and learn its fate.
   These are recent searchers, distinct from the hikers killed during Iselin's
   exile approximately three years earlier.
4. Reach a substantial Rhea–Iselin confrontation scene in which Rhea attempts
   to reason with her. Their extended exchange reveals to the player Iselin's
   intention to collapse the dome and her bitterness, betrayal and revenge.
5. Rhea is imprisoned before the main party finds her. The approach leads
   into the existing pre-boss rescue-and-duel sequence.

The [scene owner](../scenes/ICE_REAPER_PRE_BOSS.md#planned-confrontation-and-imprisonment)
records the conversation's dramatic requirements, staging proposal and handoff.
The expedition's dispatch remains offscreen. Viewpoint, chronology within the
confrontation, and how much the approaching party hears require authored
staging; a player-facing cutaway does not automatically inform the protagonists.
No expedition roster, named deaths, dungeon map, encounter count, dialogue
script or new runtime quest identifier is selected here.

## Ending requirements (CANON boundaries, not a chosen ending)

- Defeating Iselin is the only way to release her accumulated excess Ice aether
  and restore the Ice Worldvein's normal flow. The final confrontation must
  deliver this result; its precise mechanism remains open.
- Do not infer Iselin's death, a safe or repaired physical dome, restored balance
  in every other Worldvein, or a chosen final fate from that result.
- Preserve meaningful consequences. The broader war, political settlement,
  physical safety work and other character outcomes remain to be written.

## Game I player knowledge (CANON boundary)

Players encounter ordinary magicks and local belief before learning how the
Worldvein disturbances, artificial harvesting and cannon conflict connect.
Public reports, private Crown knowledge, Iselin's decisions, player-facing
cutaways and what absent party members know must remain distinct. The
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md) owns those disclosure decisions.
An observation of elemental disaster is not proof of a Veinhold, and discovering
harvesting does not establish the cause of every earlier incident.
