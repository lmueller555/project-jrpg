## Lucan Rooke

### Background and motivation

Lucan comes from a wagonwright's household in the
[Redstone March](../../world/continents/MEROVAR.md#redstone-march). He earned his
knighthood through service protecting road crews and caravans around the
Greyfold approaches. He dresses impeccably, maintains his equipment
obsessively and considers his reputation something others should already know.

He wants a prestigious command and lasting security for his family. His
confidence rests on real competence: he is brave, decisive and skilled at
keeping frightened people alive. His ambition and appetite for recognition
coexist with a serious commitment to public service.

### Personality, flaw and growth

Lucan is confident, arrogant and proud of his work. He enjoys applause, offers
unsolicited advice and struggles to admit that someone else has a better plan.
His competence should be visible in his actions, giving his confidence a
credible foundation.

His central flaw is confusing responsibility for others with authority over
their choices. When danger approaches, he starts issuing orders, including
to people who never agreed to follow him. His growth concerns protecting
people while respecting their judgment and agency.

His relationship with Caelan begins with condescending instruction and
develops into respect. Caelan challenges his decisions; Lucan helps Caelan
become more assertive. Their relationship needs occasions when each has
something useful to teach the other.

### Story entry and combat role

Introduce Lucan during Act I's growing tensions through a legitimate
road-security assignment. An escort initially brings him into Caelan's company.
As events undermine official explanations, his commitment to public service
gives him a reason to investigate. The exact assignment, quest and recruitment
sequence remain open.

He is the party's swordsman and protector, with two intended equipment options:

- **Sword and offhand shield:** Guarding, interception and controlling enemy
  attention emphasize his protective role.
- **Two-handed sword:** Powerful physical attacks emphasize damage.

These are approved combat directions. Weapon rules, ability definitions,
starting attributes and Aether Lattice paths require later design.

### Voice reference

Lucan speaks decisively and often assumes he is in charge. Pride, practical
instruction and a willingness to enter danger should all be audible.

> “You may dislike my manners. I'd appreciate you surviving long enough to
> complain about them.”

## Rhea Sorel

### Background and motivation

Rhea grew up in a small settlement on the wooded western slopes of the
[Greyfold Mountains](../../world/continents/MEROVAR.md#greyfold-uplands).
She received basic magical instruction locally and developed her abilities
through years spent studying streams, storms, forests and aether in those
environments. She reads other naturalists' accounts eagerly, then goes outside
to check their observations.

She is a lively, carefree student of nature. Her notebooks contain careful
measurements, sketches, questions, pressed leaves and increasingly impatient
arguments with earlier entries. Muddy boots, an open notebook and something
interesting just beyond the path are ordinary parts of her life.

Her ambition is to produce a field guide that helps ordinary people recognize
and understand the aether around them. As familiar patterns begin changing,
her work becomes urgent: communities need explanations they can act upon.

### Personality, flaw and growth

Rhea is upbeat, sociable and easily excited by discovery. She enjoys traveling,
welcomes unfamiliar food and makes friends readily. A difficult journey can
become an adventure in her telling. An unusual growth of moss can interrupt
a conversation; she occasionally forgets that her companions would like to
reach shelter before examining another hillside.

Her enthusiasm extends to people. Caelan's modest success at the mill
interests her: she wants to know what he felt, what changed and whether he
has tried it again. She helps him take his own progress seriously.

Her central blind spot is falling in love with a promising explanation before
testing it properly. She can move from noticing something to believing she
knows its cause with considerable enthusiasm. Her growth develops greater
care in what she claims to know while preserving her delight in discovery.

### Magicks and combat role

Rhea is an offensive Aethermage. Her studies of currents, rising heat,
pressure and aether inform the patterns she attempts in her casting.
She improvises around the free-flowing aether she senses nearby. Changing
conditions and unfamiliar combinations can make the results
unpredictable.

Her magicks are sometimes chaotic in their actual effects:

- A wind spell inspired by a whirlpool can curl into a spiraling burst
  instead of traveling straight.
- An attempt to concentrate heat can release several abrupt pulses instead
  of one clean explosion.
- An effective combination discovered during a fight can prove difficult
  for her to reproduce precisely afterward.

Surprises can include wasted effort or effects extending farther than
intended. She develops judgment about which observations she understands
well enough to trust under pressure. These examples establish characterization
and visual direction; they do not prescribe random spell results, friendly
fire, damage values or other combat rules.

Her casting remains subject to
[aether conservation and the limits of magicks](../../FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md#1-foundations-of-existence).
Studying living environments does not establish a new source of unlimited
energy or a mechanism for consuming their life force.

### Story entry and voice

Introduce Rhea early in Act II, already investigating unusual aether behavior
along a woodland or river route. Caelan's account gives her another observation
to compare with her own. Traveling together lets her follow the disturbance
into places she could not safely survey alone.

Her excitement should invite others to look and think with her. She is capable
of admitting that an explanation failed and being interested in the next
question.

> “I was wrong about the cause. The observation was good, though. Come and
> look—we've missed something.”

## Edran Hale

### Background and motivation

Edran is a practicing healer and teacher from the
[Lower Leth](../../world/continents/MEROVAR.md#lower-leth). He has spent years
moving between market-town clinics and smaller river settlements. His family
is alive, his colleagues expect him back and unfinished obligations accompany
every journey.

He wants dependable care between scattered communities: trained local healers,
shared supplies and help that survives the departure of one exceptional
practitioner. His reasons to travel grow from work and relationships that
continue to matter while he is away.

### Personality, flaw and growth

Edran is reserved, observant and wise through experience, including experience
of being wrong. He notices who has stopped eating, who is concealing pain and
who needs a practical task before they can speak. His humor is dry and
occasional.

His central flaw is waiting too long for certainty before sharing a concern.
Others can mistake his silence for reassurance. His growth concerns timely
honesty about what he knows, what he suspects and what remains uncertain.

His relationship with Caelan is reciprocal. Edran helps him judge consequences;
Caelan presses Edran to speak before every uncertainty has been resolved.

### Story entry, combat role and voice

Introduce Edran at the end of Act II. The party's account connects several
unusual injuries and failed protective workings he has encountered. After
arranging coverage for his patients, he travels with them to investigate
the source. The exact cases, location and recruitment scene remain open.

As the Sage, he specializes in healing, protective spells, strengthening allies
and weakening enemies. The approved attribute distinction remains in force:
Spr increases healing power; Mag increases damaging-spell power. Exact spells,
debuff rules and scaling require later design.

His speech is deliberate and grounded in what another person needs to hear.
Quietness should leave room for warmth and occasional dry humor.

> “*after a pause* I suspected something was wrong. I should have told you
> what I knew—and what I didn't.”

## Rhea and Edran as sources of lore

Rhea and Edran are major ongoing sources of lore during the group's travels.
Their expertise gives them different ways to interpret the same place.

| Rhea's observations | Edran's observations |
| --- | --- |
| Aether patterns across landscapes, habitats and local casting conditions | Exhaustion, injuries, disrupted healing and changes in familiar protective workings |
| Differences between sites and exciting new explanations | Patterns across cases and the limits of a diagnosis |

Begin these exchanges with something the group can see, touch or experience.
Let Caelan and the others ask reasonable questions, contribute observations
and act on what they learn. Rhea and Edran compare evidence, disagree and revise
their explanations together. Each should sometimes recognize something the
other overlooked.

This exchange is a voice reference, not a committed quest script:

> **Rhea:** “Feel this stone. Warm underneath, cold on top.”
>
> **Caelan:** “Sun hasn't reached it?”
>
> **Rhea:** “That was my first thought. Then I turned it over.”
>
> **Edran:** “And checked another?”
>
> **Rhea:** “*grins* Three. I knew you'd ask.”

Both begin as modern specialists whose knowledge develops through observation.
Their interpretations remain fallible. Game I's disclosures must follow the
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md); their expertise does not grant
them knowledge of the hidden cosmic history or resolve its unanswered questions.

## Party relationships and future work

Lucan wants decisions and an orderly plan. Rhea wants room to observe and
improvise. Edran wants enough information to judge consequences. Their
disagreements should develop from these priorities and allow each to contribute.
Rhea enjoys puncturing Lucan's self-importance; Edran can catch the point where
her excitement is overtaking her judgment. Caelan gradually learns when to
listen and when to choose for himself.

These foundations establish three intended combat roles, not a new mandatory
job system. Caelan retains his approved all-rounder baseline and earned
magical progression. Mira retains her established skills and relationship
with him.

Specific settlements, family identities, remaining poses and animations,
recruitment quest scripts, exact abilities, numeric attributes and Lattice
layouts remain open.
Later character outcomes and the identity associated with the cinematic's
bloody helmet remain undecided. Future scenes should preserve the
[opening cinematic's established limits](../../OPENING_CINEMATIC.md).
