# Game I — CH01_MQ02: A Little Trouble at the Mill

**Status:** Current implementation script for Mira’s early birthday gift, the Spellblade combat introduction, a deliberate water spell and Caelan’s return to help at Serene’s home.
**Scope:** The workshop request and sword presentation, mill diagnosis, armed riverbank outing, Guard, a failed Aqua Edge attempt and accidental Aether Edge, cooperative repair, Halden’s resolution and the homecoming before Aldric’s arrival.
**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [MQ01](CH01_MQ01.md), [character facts](CHARACTERS.md), [Mira](characters/MIRA.md) and [Caelan](characters/CAELAN.md).

## Narrative contract

Caelan and Mira are eighteen and already childhood friends. Halden raised Mira after her parents’ loss and taught her mechanics through their work on the mill. She prepared Caelan’s gift before this outing; it expresses her familiarity with his interests and his wish to practise magicks. It is not a reward for combat performance or a response to his later failure.

Mira presents the gear-and-gem sword before they leave the workshop. Its stable grip and rotating circular guard bring its single blue Water gem into position. The other three brass sockets are empty; no other elemental gem is granted by the gift. The presentation shows the mechanism and the friends’ reactions, not a successful elemental spell. Caelan supplies the magical control and effort. The first lesson offers only Aqua Edge as an experimental elemental technique. Red and White gem acquisition belongs to later cave progression and is not supplied here.

The riverbank fight makes his limits concrete. Guard comes first. His first Aqua Edge attempt fails; his effort inadvertently produces Aether Edge without asking the player to choose a second elemental attempt. The failed Aqua experiment then leaves Skills, and Aether Edge becomes his sole learned starter technique. It is now an immediate physical-plus-non-elemental-magical sword strike, not the older three-hit self-enchantment. Mira and Rivet contribute while the guided opening guarantees space for the lessons; normal combat follows the discovery.

A ridgeback tusker felled the riverbank tree. Its broken limb restricts the upstream intake, while the main brook continues flowing. This remains separate from the extraordinary lightning damage to Eldenmere’s southern road. Their repair first isolates the load mechanically. Caelan then uses the blue focus to move a small pocket of existing still water into its adjacent drain, exposing the catch. This deliberate success does not unlock Aqua Edge or prove that he can hold back a current. Tools, leverage and coordinated pulling remove the limb.

The quest remains a complete local adventure and friendship scene. Halden’s acknowledged report records the restored mill and releases Mira to her work. Caelan then chooses to return home and help with the remaining preparations. MQ02 completes only after his conversation with Serene and the visible cup-setting action are finished. Its existing rewards settle once before [Aldric’s arrival cinematic](../../REGENT_ARRIVAL.md), which introduces the relief visit and returns control to the same dining room without accepting MQ03 or explaining the surges’ hidden cause.
