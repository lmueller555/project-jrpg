## Narrative contract

The stopped mill brings Caelan and Mira together for a small, complete local adventure. A ridgeback tusker has felled a tree on the riverbank; a broken limb has washed into the upstream intake screen, restricting the millrace while the main stream continues flowing. Mira warns Caelan before they leave town and brings a short spear. Together they face the creature, then investigate, relieve the pressure, free the mechanism and restore the mill through complementary work.

Mira contributes engineering knowledge and the repair procedure. Caelan contributes observations, practical work and one useful, limited spell. Once the intake is isolated, he moves a shallow pocket of still water from an inspection recess into its adjacent drain, revealing a catch. This is a short, localized movement of existing water. It neither creates water nor proves that he can hold back a current.

If the player attempted magicks at the Venns, Caelan is pleased with this success but disappointed about that earlier failure. Mira acknowledges the difference in load and encourages him to value what he accomplished. If the player used tools at the Venns, the exchange contains no invented failed attempt.

The outing introduces the wait-ATB combat system through Caelan and a temporary, player-controlled Mira. Mira is more effective with her spear than unarmed Caelan. Her guidance concerns footing, openings and the creature’s visible charge tell; precise interface rules remain brief interface cues. The repair stays untimed, with no casting roll or mana charge for the small water movement. Their relationship remains affectionate and credible; this introduces no military biography for Mira or new explanation for Caelan’s strength.

## Durable progress and continuity

The quest controller stores completed inspections and the prepared, spell-done, encouraged, restored, verified and resolved milestones separately from transient action clocks. Loading a completed stage restores its visible result and the next unfinished interaction. Canceling dialogue does not accept an unread scene or complete its callback.

Mira remains one actor throughout work, accompaniment, repair and return. Her station and the mill's restored state survive room changes and reloads. The single restoration authority must govern the intake, millrace, wheel and map description consistently. Existing MQ01 saves at the Mira handoff continue into this quest.

The earlier Venn attempt remains a failure against a continuously flowing load. MQ02 establishes that Caelan can make a smaller, controlled movement after pressure has been removed. His precise training history, complete spell repertoire and future rate of growth remain unspecified.

The `prepare_done` and `repair_done` comments follow completed, saved physical actions. Leaving or reloading after one of those actions preserves its result without replaying the work or requiring a second completion. They add no save fields, objective IDs or resource economy. The encouragement remains an acknowledged narrative milestone distinct from the spell’s saved physical success.

## Encounter persistence and older journeys

The mill record is version 2. In addition to the existing inspection and repair facts it stores `warned`, `encounter_cleared`, `aftermath_done` and `legacy_bypass`. The two new quest objectives are `prepare_journey` after diagnosis and `secure_trail` before `reach_intake`.

Version-one journeys already at or beyond the intake retain their repair progress through an explicit `legacy_bypass`. Their route objectives migrate, but they receive no invented fight, warning, aftermath, monster report or battle rewards. An older upstream checkpoint that has not inspected the intake rejoins the safe arrival and follows the new warning and encounter. Completed mill and later chapter progress remain intact.

The normal town Bat becomes available after this encounter or the explicit older-route bypass, preserving the intended first-combat order for a new journey.
