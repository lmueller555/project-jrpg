# Game I — CH01_MQ02: A Little Trouble at the Mill

**Status:** Implementation script for the approved mill quest and small-magick-success revision.  
**Scope:** Mira's introduction, a cooperative investigation and repair, Caelan's modest magical success, and restoration of Halden's mill.  
**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [opening quest](CH01_MQ01.md), [approved character facts](CHARACTERS.md). Caelan is 18; his childhood friendship with Mira is established. Mira's age and any additional personal history remain unspecified.

## Narrative contract

The stopped mill brings Caelan and Mira together for a small, complete local adventure. A fallen limb has jammed the upstream intake screen, restricting the millrace while the main stream continues flowing. They investigate, relieve the pressure, free the mechanism and restore the mill through complementary work.

Mira contributes engineering knowledge and the repair procedure. Caelan contributes observations, practical work and one useful, limited spell. Once the intake is isolated, he moves a shallow pocket of still water from an inspection recess into its adjacent drain, revealing a catch. This is a short, localized movement of existing water. It neither creates water nor proves that he can hold back a current.

If the player attempted magicks at the Venns, Caelan is pleased with this success but disappointed about that earlier failure. Mira acknowledges the difference in load and encourages him to value what he accomplished. If the player used tools at the Venns, the exchange contains no invented failed attempt.

The quest is noncombat and untimed. It introduces no mana economy, random casting roll, major political revelation or new backstory explanation for Caelan's strength. The relationship remains affectionate and credible; their work does not settle the wider disagreement over technology and inherited restrictions.

## Critical path

`Mira's workbench → mill mechanism and race → upstream intake → bypass preparation → small spell → encouragement → cooperative repair → working mill → Halden's resolution.`

Finishing MQ01's stopped-wheel exchange unlocks MQ02. MQ02 is accepted only after the player finishes the reunion with Mira. Ordinary dialogue remains live and cancelable. Short authored physical actions briefly own the participants' poses.

**Action:** After the last line, accept MQ02, record `meet_mira`, stop Mira's bench work and let the same actor accompany Caelan. Her work fitting remains on the bench. She follows along the existing paths and through supported scene transfers.

**Tracker:** Investigate the stopped mill with Mira.

## S02 — Diagnose the mill

**Trigger:** Inspect both the mechanism and the millrace with Mira nearby. Each completed inspection records its own fact; leaving unread text records nothing.

**Mechanism:**

- **Caelan:** "The axle's still seated. I can't see anything caught in the wheel."
- **Mira:** "Nothing here explains it stopping. Let's look at the water reaching it."

**Millrace:**

- **Caelan:** "The stream's moving, but there's hardly anything coming down the millrace."
- **Mira:** "Then we'll follow the race back to its intake."

**Diagnosis after both observations:**

- **Mira:** "The wheel isn't getting enough water to turn under load."
- **Caelan:** "Something's blocking the intake?"
- **Mira:** "That's where I'd look first. The path follows the race upstream."
- **Caelan:** "Let's see what's caught there."

**Result:** Record `diagnose_mill`. The mill remains visibly stopped. Point the player toward the upstream path.

## S03 — Inspect the upstream intake

**Trigger:** Follow the millrace to the upstream area and inspect the obstructed screen.

- **Caelan:** "That limb's wedged across the screen."
- **Mira:** "And the catch is under that trapped water. We'll take the pressure off before we touch it."

**Result:** Record the intake inspection and `reach_intake`. The obstruction is mundane and visible. There is no concealed ancient mechanism or supernatural cause.

## S04 — Relieve the pressure

**Trigger:** Interact with the bypass control after inspecting the intake.

- **Mira:** "I'll set the bypass first. Stand clear of the screen."
- **Caelan:** "Then we can get at the catch?"
- **Mira:** "Once we clear the water left in its recess."

**Action:** Mira operates the bypass while Caelan stands clear in his normal idle stance. The completed action isolates the intake load and records `prepare_intake`. The small inspection pocket retains still water; the large obstruction has not moved. Feet remain on the dry work area and tools/controls make visible contact.

## S05 — A smaller magical success

**Trigger:** Interact with the inspection recess after preparing the intake.

- **Mira:** "The pressure's off. There's just water trapped in this recess."
- **Caelan:** "I can try clearing it."

**Action:** Caelan raises a comfortably bent arm from his normal standing stance. A small blue spark gathers. The existing pocket of water moves into the adjacent drain, exposing the catch. The effect ends cleanly and his arm lowers to idle. His body does not stretch, lean beyond its support or collapse.

**Result:** Record `small_spell` and the durable `spell_done` fact at the successful movement. Only the inspection pocket is cleared; the limb and jammed intake screen remain in place until the physical repair. The cleared recess persists. Loading or leaving afterward never repeats an already completed spell.

## S07 — Free the screen together

**Trigger:** Interact with the repair lever after the successful spell and encouragement.

- **Mira:** "I'll release the catch. Keep the rope steady, then pull when I say."
- **Caelan:** "Ready."
- **Mira:** "Now."

**Action:** Mira releases the exposed catch and operates the lever while Caelan grips the visible haul rope with his palm. Together they shift the lodged limb and free the screen, then return the intake to its operating configuration. The larger work is mechanical and cooperative. Caelan does not magically move the limb or lift the whole flow.

**Result:** Record `repair_intake` and the durable restored state only after the completed action. The intake and water route show their repaired appearances. This state also permits the town's mill to run.

**Tracker:** Return to the mill with Mira.

**Trigger:** Return to the mill and inspect the restored operation with Mira.

- **Mira:** "That's the wheel taking the load."
- **Caelan:** "Sounds like Halden's noise is back."

**Result:** Record `verify_mill` after the exchange. The wheel, millrace and related effects must agree with the restored world state. The main stream remains animated before and after the repair.

**Trigger:** Speak with Halden and Mira at the defined mill resolution station after verifying the repair. The same Halden actor is deliberately restaged from his MQ01 location; no duplicate miller appears.

**Result:** Finish `report`, complete MQ02, and let Mira return to her ordinary work. Do not complete the quest merely for arriving at the mill or leaving the final conversation unread. This scene does not invent the next main quest's dialogue or automatically accept it.
