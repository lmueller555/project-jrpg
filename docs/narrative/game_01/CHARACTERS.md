characterization and character design. Liam and his parents are a newly
requested NPC family; their surname and quest association are unassigned.

play-bone routines. Her single toy can be carried, braced between her paws,
chewed and dropped for a later retrieval. Their front, left, right and back idle sprite designs are approved.

**Daisy** is Liam's family's female dog. She has gray fur with white and black
spots, floppy gray ears and a fluffy tail, and is slightly smaller than Liam.
Her age is unspecified. Her front, left, right and back idle designs are approved.

Earlier narrative drafts spell Caelan's name **Caelen**; both spellings refer
to the same protagonist, whose current game and asset identity is **Caelan**.

## Caelan - protagonist foundation

**Confirmed, 2026-11-04:** Caelan is reserved, thoughtful and self-doubting.
Before the crisis, he wants to develop his magicks and find a vocation of his own.

**Profile notes approved, 2026-11-04:** His

His [combat progression concept](characters/CAELAN.md#combat-progression-concept),

### Caelan and Tomas - shared family background

**Confirmed, 2026-11-04:** The brothers share the same parents. Both were born
in Eldenmere and have lived there their whole lives. Their parents have been
missing for years, and Serene cares for them because of that absence.

**Author-only truth:** Their parents are alive at the game opening. The
brothers do not know this; although they do not talk about it, they probably
think their parents are dead by now.

**Early responsibility, confirmed 2026-11-04:** Caelan had to quickly learn
how to care for Tomas while still young and immature himself. Part of his
self-doubt comes from feeling he must act composed and have the answers for
his brother even when he does not know what the right answers are. Serene
remains their caregiver; this does not make Caelan Tomas's sole parent.

The disappearance's cause, exact timeline and circumstances, the parents'
whereabouts, what Serene knows, and the survival reveal remain open.
[Caelan's sheet](characters/CAELAN.md) keeps author knowledge separate from
character belief; no new dialogue reveals this information in the current game.

### Caelan and Mira - shared childhood bond

**Confirmed, 2026-11-04:** Both grew up without their parents, and this shared
sense of loss is a mostly unspoken part of their existing bond at the opening.
Mira's parents died when she was 12; Caelan's are missing and he probably
thinks them dead. Their connection does not establish that Mira knows the
author-only truth that his parents are alive.

They already share several childhood memories by the start of the game.
The player will learn about these later, as their shared past is explored
throughout the game. Specific memories, their chronology and their reveal
format and timing remain open. No particular childhood event, simultaneous
loss, shared household, romance or implemented flashback is established here.
Both [Caelan's sheet](characters/CAELAN.md) and
[Mira's sheet](characters/MIRA.md) record this shared foundation.

## Aldric — approved royal background

**Approved:** 2026-11-03.

Aldric belongs to Merovar's ruling family and is the younger brother of the
reigning monarch. One crown rules Merovar through regional governors and local
institutions. Aldric serves as regional regent; his immediate administrative
concerns remain Elden Vale and nearby upper-Leth road and quarry districts.
Royal birth does not make him the reigning monarch.

Lucan Rooke is Aldric's personal guard. Later in the story, Aldric entrusts
Lucan to Caelan's party, establishing the basis for his recruitment. The exact
occasion and scene remain open; this replaces the earlier generic
road-security escort recruitment premise.

MQ03 now introduces Lucan with Aldric's visiting party, in place of the generic
traveling attendant. His guard work, unsolicited practical advice and meal
conversation establish his opening dynamic with Caelan. He remains with Aldric;
this visit does not recruit him into the playable party. The ridge discussion
also establishes Aldric's relationship to the reigning monarch in dialogue.

The dynasty's name, the monarch's identity, Aldric's birthplace and age,
succession rules and the precise constitutional powers of the regency remain
unapproved. Membership in the ruling family does not yet assign a named
capital or royal residence.

### Ruling-family development — proposals, not canon

- **House Maroven:** A proposed dynasty name. Its legitimacy emphasizes keeping
  roads open, settlements supplied and regional institutions working. This is
  a royal ideal, not proof that every ruler has fulfilled it.
- **Queen Isolde Maroven:** Aldric's proposed elder sister and reigning monarch.
  Patient and politically disciplined, she balances competing regional claims.
  She trusts Aldric's practical judgment but worries about his tendency to
  promise results faster than institutions can deliver them.
- **Dowager Queen Helena Maroven:** Their proposed mother, who values restraint,
  continuity and relationships with local custodians. She taught Aldric that
  inherited standing creates obligations rather than personal entitlement.
- **Prince Oren Maroven:** Isolde's proposed son and intended heir. He admires
  his uncle's visible service outside court. Aldric wants to teach him that
  leadership involves listening to people, not merely being admired by them.
  Exact inheritance rules remain open.

A proposed personal foundation is that Aldric grew up beside an elder sibling
destined for the crown and sought a useful identity through regional service.
He wants to be valued for what he accomplishes, not merely his family name.
That can make urgent relief work both a sincere duty and a personal test.
His relationship with Lucan can mirror this tension: a royal-born administrator
and a knight from a wagonwright's household both want achievement to justify
their standing. How Lucan earned the guard appointment remains to be designed.

These proposals do not establish family secrets, a succession crisis or a
hidden motive for the sanctuary excavation.

## Aldric and Serene — approved relief visit and covenant positions

The [regional surge context](REGIONAL_SURGES.md) and
[covenant guide](../../world/SANCTUARY_COVENANT.md) establish the reasons for
their revised MQ03 disagreement. These are approved character motivations;
the runtime dialogue and end-MQ02 arrival now implement that approved context.

Aldric has visited two other affected communities and is helping repair damage
from a small number of extraordinary lightning incidents. Eldenmere's southern
road is another damaged connection. He seeks more quarry stone because the
combined repair needs strain regional supplies. His supervised trial-cut
proposal is sincere and limited under his understanding of the site.

Serene holds protected ground in keeping for the community rather than as
personal property. She shares an old foundation plan and an account of workers
injured by an unexplained discharge. She cannot explain the confluence's full
workings or the buried structures' purpose and does not claim certainty about
a coming disaster. She offers
courtyard repair stone, crew meals, a joint surface inspection and requests for
missing records, while withholding endorsement of excavation. Her inherited
private warning remains a separate chapter-ending beat.

**Faith and site significance, confirmed 2026-11-04:** Serene practices a
[planetary spiritual tradition](../../world/ELDEN_VALE_FAITH.md) that does not
center gods. Elden Vale Sanctuary is a place of remembrance and renewal at a
real natural aether confluence, with a living memorial grove. Its public
importance does not depend on her knowing the purpose of the buried works.
The confluence belongs to the planetary cycle, not the Aethersphere.

Caelan remains a guide, host and witness. He does not adjudicate the covenant,
approve a quarry or inspect the adults' hire agreement.

## Mira's millrace outing

Mira is Caelan's childhood friend and a capable **Engineer**. Her authoritative
[character sheet](characters/MIRA.md) retains her established millrace role,
Rivet, practical competence and relationships, and adds ten dialogue references.

**Combat direction, 2026-11-07:** Engineer supersedes her earlier Machinist
designation. Her [future progression notes](characters/MIRA.md#combat-progression-concept)
cover additional bots, configurable engineered self-augments and a five-turn
piloted-mech ultimate concept. Detailed mechanics and unlocks remain open.

**Approved background revision, 2026-11-03:** Mira is 18 at the opening. She lost
both parents at 12, and Halden took her in. Working with him on the mill gave
her proficiency as a mechanic; she immersed herself in it to bury the loss.
Recognizing her unresolved grief, Halden encouraged her to open her workshop at
17. She now finds purpose in maintaining Eldenmere's infrastructure but still
does not know how to mend the brokenness she feels in her heart.

This describes her relationship to grief, not a declaration that she is
defective. Her parents' identities and death circumstances, formal caregiving
arrangements, and workshop property and funding details remain open.

**Profile approval, 2026-11-03:** Her detailed characterization, emotional
interpretations, writing guidance and ten dialogue references are approved.
Hypothetical scene events, disclosure timing and her exact ending remain open.

The [bot guide](../../MIRA_BOTS.md) remains authoritative for combat rules.
No game dialogue, mechanics or artwork changes accompany this biography revision.

## Caelan's starting gameplay attributes

The approved attribute set is **HP, MP, Str, Mag, Spr, Def, M. Def, Speed, Acc,
Eva and Lck**. Mag increases damaging-spell power; Spr increases healing-spell
power. Caelan's approved job identity is **Spellblade**, beginning as a balanced all-rounder:

| HP | MP | Str | Mag | Spr | Def | M. Def | Speed | Acc | Eva | Lck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 140 | 30 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |

HP and MP are maximum values; current resources start full. The remaining
numbers are ratings, including Acc, Eva and Lck, rather than percentages.
Starting numbers are a tunable gameplay baseline. His Spellblade identity
develops through learned weapon techniques and magicks; these numbers do not
change his established magical limitations. Aether Edge is his only learned
first-weapon starter skill, without permanent elemental coverage. He begins
unarmed. Mira presents his first gear-and-gem blade at the start of MQ02, before
the outing. The first elemental attempt fails and the next discovers Aether
Edge; that remains his only learned starter skill. The
[character ability dictionaries](../../CHARACTER_ABILITIES.md) record his
unlock rules and the other party members' profiles.
The values are visible through **Pause → Characters**, with attribute icons and
hover explanations. Weapon discovery retains its authored story rules. See
[character attributes](../../CHARACTER_ATTRIBUTES.md) for the definitions,
runtime integration and save compatibility.
