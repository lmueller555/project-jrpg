# Game I — approved character facts

**Status:** CANON for the facts listed below. Ages are measured at the beginning
of Game I. The opening household ages were approved on 2026-10-25; the companion
foundations below were approved on 2026-10-30.

| Character | Age at game opening | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caelan | 18 years old | Tomas's older foster brother |
| Tomas | 14 years old | Caelan's younger foster brother |
| Liam | 8 years old | Child of Liam's mother and father |
| Liam's mother | Mid-30s | Liam's mother; Eldenmere's teacher; name unassigned |
| Liam's father | Mid-30s | Liam's father; name unassigned |

Caelan and Tomas are four years apart. These ages guide future dialogue,
characterization and character design. Liam and his parents are a newly
requested NPC family; their surname and quest association are unassigned.
Their home is the new wide cottage in western Eldenmere, directly south of
the sanctuary. His parents remain stationary home residents during ordinary life. MQ04
borrows his mother for the school preparation session and its dialogue. Liam and Daisy share an ambient invitation, chase and nap
routine. Liam also builds with five wooden blocks from a toy box between the
beds, sometimes leaving one out. Daisy can accidentally knock over his creation
and be sent to her rug. Daisy also has indoor wandering, eating, drinking and
play-bone routines. Her single toy can be carried, braced between her paws,
chewed and dropped for a later retrieval. Their front, left, right and back idle sprite designs are approved.

**Daisy** is Liam's family's female dog. She has gray fur with white and black
spots, floppy gray ears and a fluffy tail, and is slightly smaller than Liam.
Her age is unspecified. Her front, left, right and back idle designs are approved.

Earlier narrative drafts spell Caelan's name **Caelen**; both spellings refer
to the same protagonist, whose current game and asset identity is **Caelan**.

Unlisted ages and unapproved biography details remain open. See
[Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md) and the [opening quest](CH01_MQ01.md) for established
relationships and scene context.

## Mira's millrace outing

Mira is Caelan's childhood friend and a capable mechanical problem-solver.
For MQ02 she brings a short travel spear from her workbench. She is a stronger,
more experienced combatant than unarmed Caelan and joins him as a temporary,
player-controlled guest against the Ridgeback Tusker outside Eldenmere. Her
weapon handling and practical guidance establish this competence without
assigning a military background or an age. Their later intake repair preserves
Caelan's useful, modest magical success and their complementary skills.

Liam's mother is Eldenmere's schoolteacher, as established by the author on
2026-10-25. The school is a separate public building northeast of the well square,
with a furnished classroom for six children. Her personal name remains open.

The six generic adult villager appearances form three parent pairs: the sanctuary
gardener (female_02) and male_01 in home_a; female_01 and male_02 in home_b;
female_03 and male_03 in home_c. The adults' personal names and ages remain unassigned. Halden's, Elira's and the Venn family's story homes are retained.

## Caelan's starting gameplay attributes

The approved attribute set is **HP, MP, Str, Mag, Spr, Def, M. Def, Speed, Acc,
Eva and Lck**. Mag increases damaging-spell power; Spr increases healing-spell
power. Caelan begins as a balanced all-rounder:

| HP | MP | Str | Mag | Spr | Def | M. Def | Speed | Acc | Eva | Lck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 140 | 30 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |

HP and MP are maximum values; current resources start full. The remaining
numbers are ratings, including Acc, Eva and Lck, rather than percentages.
Starting numbers are a tunable gameplay baseline. They do not assign Caelan a
job, define a battle format or change his established magical limitations.
The values are visible through **Pause → Characters**, with attribute icons and
hover explanations. The Sphere Grid will be developed separately. See
[character attributes](../../CHARACTER_ATTRIBUTES.md) for the definitions,
runtime integration and save compatibility.
