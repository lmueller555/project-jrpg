# Game I — approved character facts

**Status:** CANON for the facts listed below. Ages approved by the author on
2026-10-25 and measured at the beginning of Game I.

Caelan and Tomas are four years apart. These ages guide future dialogue,
characterization and character design. Liam and his parents are a newly
requested NPC family; their surname and quest association are unassigned.
Their home is the new wide cottage in western Eldenmere, directly south of
the sanctuary. His parents remain stationary residents without dialogue or
action routines. Liam and Daisy share an ambient invitation, chase and nap
routine. Liam also builds with five wooden blocks from a toy box between the
beds, sometimes leaving one out. Daisy can accidentally knock over his creation
and be sent to her rug. Daisy also has indoor wandering, eating, drinking and
play-bone routines. Her single toy can be carried, braced between her paws,
chewed and dropped for a later retrieval. Their front, left, right and back idle sprite designs are approved.

**Daisy** is Liam's family's female dog. She has gray fur with white and black
spots, floppy gray ears and a fluffy tail, and is slightly smaller than Liam.
Her age is unspecified. Her front, left, right and back idle designs are approved.

Earlier narrative drafts spell Caelan's name **Caelen**; both spellings refer
to the same protagonist, whose current game and asset identity is **Caelan**.

Other characters' ages and unapproved biography details remain open. See
[Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md) and the [opening quest](CH01_MQ01.md) for established
relationships and scene context.

Liam's mother is Eldenmere's schoolteacher, as established by the author on
2026-10-25. The school is a separate public building northeast of the well square,
with a furnished classroom for six children. Her personal name remains open.

The six generic adult villager appearances form three parent pairs: the sanctuary
gardener (female_02) and male_01 in home_a; female_01 and male_02 in home_b;
female_03 and male_03 in home_c. Their personal names, ages and children remain
unassigned. Halden's, Elira's and the Venn family's story homes are retained.
