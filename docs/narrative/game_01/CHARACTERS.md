# Game I — Character Foundations

**Status:** Refined working proposals, not final canon. Names, ages, exact histories, and dialogue remain subject to author approval.  
**Version:** 0.1  
**References:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [Act Outline](ACT_OUTLINE.md), [Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md).

## Caelen Veyr (working name, 21)

A sanctuary acolyte and apprentice archivist raised by Serene after the deaths of his parents. He wants to serve Eldenmere and preserve its histories, not pursue a grand destiny. He admires Aldric because, during a harsh winter when Caelen was a child, the regent personally helped deliver relief supplies to the sanctuary.

**Strengths:** Patient, inquisitive, compassionate, competent at simple magicks and practical work. **Flaw:** Gives trusted authorities too many chances to excuse harmful decisions. **Need:** Learn that uncovering truth demands action, including disagreement with people he loves. **Voice:** Careful questions, understated wit with family, precise language under stress. **Agency:** Must make consequential choices rather than merely receive exposition.

## Mother Serene (working name, 52)

The practical, beloved caretaker who raised Caelen and Tomas. Years of treating the sick, feeding neighbors, and mediating disputes shaped a service-centered faith. She respects Aldric's earlier relief efforts even while opposing forbidden excavation.

**Strengths:** Calm judgment, warmth, dry humor. **Flaw:** Confuses protection with withholding uncomfortable information. **Need:** Trust that loved ones deserve an informed choice. **Voice:** Concise, direct, kind without sentimentality. **Knowledge limit:** Inherited warnings about sacred sites, not the Great Sacrifice, atmospheric barrier, or four surviving Ancients.

## Mira (working name, 22)

Caelen's longtime friend and an apprentice engineer from a working household. She has seen poor infrastructure harm ordinary people and dreams of bringing improved machinery back from the capital.

**Strengths:** Energetic, resourceful, analytical. **Flaw:** Assumes technical competence can overcome social and political risks; dismisses unexplained warnings too quickly. **Need:** Understand that who controls technology matters as much as how it functions. **Voice:** Quick, concrete, teasing, not relentlessly sarcastic. She and Caelen cooperate in the mill quest using different strengths. Romance is OPEN, not presumed.

## Tomas (working name, 16)

Serene's foster child and Caelen's younger brother. Curious, humorous, drawn to Mira's inventions, and determined to explore the world beyond Eldenmere.

**Strengths:** Empathy, initiative, inventive thinking. **Flaw:** Takes unnecessary risks to prove he is grown. **Need:** Learn that independence and accepting help are compatible. **Voice:** Playful with occasional surprising seriousness. He needs his own relationships and agency; a possible later disappearance must not reduce him to a plot device. Ultimate fate OPEN.

## Lord Regent Aldric (working name, 57)

A respected ruler whose early trade relief and infrastructure reforms genuinely improved regional life. During a difficult winter he accompanied relief supplies to Eldenmere; he later supported the sanctuary's work with displaced children. He remembers Caelen and treats him warmly.

**Strengths:** Competence, courtesy, listening, commitment to public welfare. **Flaw:** Past successful decisions are becoming a conviction that he alone may decide who bears the price of survival. **Need:** Accept that stewardship does not grant unrestricted authority over others' lives. **Voice:** Calm, persuasive, asks critics for workable alternatives rather than insulting them. His later decisions must remain his own, not mind-control by an Ancient.

## Relationship development

| Pair | Emotional bond | Real disagreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caelen–Serene | Adoptive family and shared faith | Truth versus protective silence |
| Caelen–Mira | Childhood trust, intellectual chemistry | Caution versus rapid progress |
| Caelen–Tomas | Brothers, protective affection | Safety versus independence |
| Caelen–Aldric | Admiration grounded in lived rescue | Whether good intentions justify secrecy |
| Serene–Aldric | Years of productive cooperation | Sanctuaries and regional development |
| Serene–Mira | Respect for each other's care | Traditions versus testable knowledge |
| Mira–Tomas | Encouragement of inventiveness | Enthusiasm versus safe limits |

## Chapter 1 behavioral anchors

Caelen notices small inconsistencies; Serene quietly makes sure others are fed; Mira fixes objects while arguing; Tomas masks a wish to be taken seriously with humor; Aldric remembers specific people he helped. These are prompts, not scripted catchphrases.
