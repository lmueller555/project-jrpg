## Family background and missing parents

**Confirmed revision, 2026-11-04:** Caelan and Tomas are biological brothers
with the same parents. Both were born in Eldenmere and have lived there their
whole lives. They have not seen their parents in years, which is why they are
in Serene's care. This supersedes the earlier description of them as foster brothers.

**Author-only truth:** Their parents are alive at the start of the game, but
missing. The reason for their disappearance and their whereabouts are not yet
chosen.

**The brothers' knowledge:** Neither knows that their parents are alive.
Although they do not talk about it, they probably think their parents are dead
by now. This is their likely private belief, not an established death or a
certainty they have discussed with each other.

**Still open:** The parents' names and occupations, when and why they went
missing, the boys' ages at the time, the exact last sighting or reliable news,
details of the transition into Serene's care, what Serene or others know, and
how and when their survival will be revealed. Do not assume Serene is hiding
the truth or assign a hidden lineage, prophecy or other explanation.

This revision does not introduce early-game dialogue revealing the secret or
replace Caelan's opening desire for magical development and a vocation with
an active parent-search quest.

### Growing up too quickly

**Confirmed, 2026-11-04:** After their parents disappeared, Caelan had to
quickly work out how to be an older brother and care for Tomas while he was
still young and immature himself. This premature responsibility is one source
of his self-doubt.

He feels that he must act composed and have the answers for Tomas, even when
he does not know what the right answers are. His outward composure is therefore
not proof that he feels certain or was ready for that responsibility.

Serene remains their caregiver. Caelan's early caregiving role does not make
him Tomas's sole parent, erase his own immaturity or remove Tomas's agency.
The exact ages and particular childhood incidents remain unassigned.

## Voice and behavior

**Established foundation:** Reserved, thoughtful and self-doubting, but capable
of concrete questions and useful initiative in existing scenes.

**Approved writing guidance:**

- Let him observe a particular thing and decide what to do about it. He should
  not ask questions only to invite another character's lore speech.
- Use clear sentences when he understands the task. Save pauses for a reason:
  privacy, uncertainty, frustration or a difficult request.
- Give him turns long enough to develop a thought, and short replies when he
  has little to add. Do not make every exchange an evenly balanced pair.
- Avoid constant apologies, clever closing lines or perfect summaries of other
  people's feelings. Thoughtfulness is not automatic wisdom.
- Let comfort with familiar people change his delivery. Reserve need not make
  his conversations with his brother and oldest friend formal.
- Around Tomas, distinguish the composure he feels he must show from the
  certainty he actually has. He need not have the right answer just because
  he feels responsible for providing one.
- With Mira, allow familiarity and shared quiet to carry the connection.
  Their shared past need not become an explanation of grief in every exchange.
- Give his own desire consequences: he asks for time, starts practice, admits
  a preference or chooses a next step. He cannot spend the whole game waiting
  for stronger personalities to choose his direction.

These notes follow the [approved dialogue-structure direction](TEMPLATE.md#dialogue-structure).
They do not require new dialogue-choice mechanics or routine animations.

## Show, Don't Tell

All five scenarios are approved portrayal references with hypothetical events.
They do not establish new childhood events, feats, quests, spells or animation
requirements.

### Scenario 1 - Choosing to practice

**Status:** Approved portrayal reference; hypothetical scene.
**Stage:** Opening characterization.
**Setup:** His assigned work is done. A quiet space and his notes are available.

**Observable beats:**

- Caelan starts to put the notes away, then lays them out again.
- He prepares a small practice setup with existing water, checking the space
  before attempting the kind of modest movement he already knows.
- After checking the result against his notes, he changes one part of the
  setup and tries again rather than waiting for someone to give him a task.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He spends his free time on something he wants to learn
without a guarantee of success or an audience offering reassurance.

**Character signal:** His aspiration produces action. Self-doubt does not erase
interest, care or willingness to practice; no new magical power is demonstrated.

### Scenario 2 - An observation left unshared

**Status:** Approved portrayal reference; hypothetical scene.
**Stage:** Opening flaw; its particular scene remains hypothetical.
**Setup:** Others discuss an ordinary local problem. His notebook contains an
observation that may be relevant.

**Observable beats:**

- He opens the page, checks it, and starts to step into the discussion.
- As another speaker continues, he stops and folds the notebook against himself.
- When the group moves on, he opens the same page again instead of putting it away.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not offer the observation, so it cannot be
considered. His uncertainty has a cost rather than functioning as a decorative pause.

**Character signal:** One expression of his weakness is withholding his contribution.
This should contrast with occasions when he does volunteer, not define every scene.

### Scenario 3 - Making room for his brother

**Status:** Approved portrayal reference; hypothetical scene.
**Stage:** Familiar household life.
**Setup:** Tomas's unfinished project occupies the table where Caelan wants to eat.

**Observable beats:**

- Caelan lifts a loose piece to clear space, then notices Tomas reaching for it.
- He returns it to Tomas's side and moves his own bowl to the other end.
- He slides another piece away from the table edge without rearranging the
  project, then settles down beside him.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not take over, put the project away or require
a public acknowledgment of his help. He accepts a little inconvenience.

**Character signal:** Quiet familiarity can contain affection and irritation.
The relationship is between brothers; Tomas is not merely someone to supervise.

### Scenario 4 - Curiosity beyond an errand

**Status:** Approved portrayal reference; hypothetical scene.
**Stage:** Ordinary village life.
**Setup:** While passing an ordinary local inscription, he notices a detail
different from the copy in his notes.

**Observable beats:**

- He walks past, slows, then returns to examine the mark.
- He opens his notebook and compares the two versions, changing his position
  to see the surface more clearly.
- He makes a careful note before rejoining his companion, keeping the page open.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not simply finish the route as quickly as
possible. His own interest takes some of the time.

**Character signal:** Thoughtfulness can be interested and absorbed, not just
worried. The mark is not an approved ancient clue or cosmological revelation.

### Scenario 5 - Offering his judgment

**Status:** Approved portrayal reference; hypothetical scene.
**Stage:** Possible later growth; exact occasion remains open.
**Setup:** A group considers a plan. He has an observation, but cannot guarantee
that it will solve the whole problem.

**Observable beats:**

- He checks the relevant note once and leaves the notebook open instead of
  folding it against himself.
- He puts the page where the others can see it and indicates what he observed,
  waiting while they consider it rather than withdrawing it.
- Once the group has considered the information, he takes up the equipment
  for his part in the next task and begins preparing.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not wait to become certain of everything or
avoid being associated with the outcome.

**Character signal:** Participation becomes a choice he makes. This is not a
claim that he is always right, seizes command or stops having doubts.

## Ten dialogue and expression references

All ten examples are approved voice references as of 2026-11-04. Examples 1, 2
and 7 quote existing game scenes; the others remain hypothetical reference
sketches, not implemented dialogue or established plot events.

### 1. Thoughtful initiative - the millrace

**Context:** After pressure relief, he notices an isolated pocket of water.

> **Caelan:** "I could move that into the drain."
>
> **Mira:** "Just the water in the recess?"
>
> **Caelan:** "Yes. There's nowhere for more to come through now."
>
> **Mira:** "All right. I'll keep clear."

**Acting cue:** The existing scene gives him a useful proposal and performs
the spell afterward, not before the conversation is acknowledged.

**Character signal:** He can reason and act despite self-doubt. Mira's question
clarifies the task and safety rather than setting up a clever reply.
**Status / source:** Existing dialogue, [MQ02 small spell](../CH01_MQ02.md).

### 2. Acknowledging a limit - conditional early setback

**Context:** Only if the player attempted magicks against the Venns' flowing water.

> **Caelan:** "I can't move it. I'll need the tools."

**Acting cue:** The implemented quiet acknowledgment leads into the practical repair.

**Character signal:** A failed attempt can disappoint him without preventing useful action.
**Status / source:** Existing dialogue, [MQ01 failed attempt](../CH01_MQ01.md).

### 3. Familiar irritation - ordinary brotherhood

**Context:** Tomas is using the table for an unfinished project.

> **Caelan:** "Your pieces are all over the table again."
>
> **Tomas:** "I'm not finished."
>
> **Caelan:** "I know. Move them to that end while I eat, then you can spread
> them out again."

**Acting cue:** Makes room for his bowl without packing the project away.

**Character signal:** He can be direct and a little irritated with his brother;
affection does not require constant gentle instruction or jokes.
**Status:** Approved voice reference; hypothetical scene.

### 4. A personal ambition - before the crisis

**Context:** He tries to explain his interest to someone he trusts; no teacher is assigned.

> **Caelan:** "I'm thinking about studying magicks properly. I don't know who
> I'd ask, or what work it would lead to."

*He looks back at his notes.*

> **Caelan:** "I'd still like to try."

**Acting cue:** Lets the wish stand without presenting a finished career plan.

**Character signal:** An uncertain aspiration can still be his own, not a task
another person has given him.
**Status:** Approved voice reference expressing the confirmed desire.

### 5. Frustration without an immediate lesson

**Context:** Something he is trying to learn has not worked as expected.

> **Caelan:** "I thought I had it right."
>
> **Companion:** "Do you want to try again?"
>
> **Caelan:** "Later. Not right now."

**Acting cue:** Puts the notes aside without insisting that he is fine.

**Character signal:** He can be discouraged and need time without explaining
the feeling perfectly or converting it into a joke.
**Status:** Approved voice reference; the task and setback remain unchosen.

### 6. Insisting on being heard - a possible party disagreement

**Context:** Lucan is moving ahead with a route decision before hearing his observation.

> **Lucan:** "We're taking this route."
>
> **Caelan:** "Wait. You haven't heard what I found."
>
> **Lucan:** "We can't spend all morning here."
>
> **Caelan:** "Then give me a minute. I went to look. I need you to listen."

**Acting cue:** Holds his place and keeps the relevant notes visible.

**Character signal:** Reserve does not prevent an objection. His observation
still needs consideration; the scene does not declare him automatically correct.
**Status:** Approved voice reference; not a fixed route or recruitment scene.

### 7. Accepting a correction - Liam's independence

**Context:** He starts to help with Liam's drawing before the child wants assistance.

> **Liam:** "Wait. I was doing that."
>
> **Caelan:** "All right. Tell me when you want a hand."

**Acting cue:** Withdraws his hand as in the existing scene.

**Character signal:** He can misjudge the need for help and adjust without
a speech about respecting independence.
**Status / source:** Existing dialogue, [MQ04 agreed outing](../CH01_MQ04.md).

### 8. Curiosity - a particular detail

**Context:** He compares an ordinary local inscription with a written copy.

> **Caelan:** "These marks don't match the copy. This one has another line beneath it."

*He turns the page back.*

> **Caelan:** "Can I borrow this? I'd like to compare it with my notes."

**Acting cue:** Keeps his attention on the difference rather than awaiting a lore explanation.

**Character signal:** He has interests and questions beyond his assigned errands.
**Status:** Approved voice reference; no hidden historical discovery is established.

### 9. Asking for company - an ordinary friendship

**Context:** He visits Mira near the end of the day.

> **Caelan:** "Are you nearly finished?"
>
> **Mira:** "I've got to put this back together."
>
> **Caelan:** "I'll wait. I wanted to take a walk before it gets dark."

**Acting cue:** Finds somewhere to sit without turning the visit into a repair request.

**Character signal:** A reserved person can seek company plainly. Both have
their own immediate concern; neither needs a witty closing line.
**Status:** Approved voice reference; does not establish romance or an actual visit.

### 10. Choosing a next step - possible later growth

**Context:** The group needs information about a route. Exact circumstances remain open.

> **Caelan:** "I haven't checked the far side. We need to know whether people
> can still get through."

*He checks his equipment and looks toward the road.*

> **Caelan:** "I'll go and look. Mira, will you come with me?"

**Acting cue:** Prepares to act while leaving Mira room to answer.

**Character signal:** He can take responsibility amid incomplete knowledge
without declaring himself certain, issuing a grand vow or taking away another person's choice.
**Status:** Approved voice and growth reference, not a fixed later event.
