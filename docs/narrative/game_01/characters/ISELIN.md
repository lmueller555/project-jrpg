# Regent Iselin

## Approved identity and opening role

Iselin replaces Aldric in current game canon, retaining the regional regent title,
royal role and sincere concern for the people. The
[replacement opening](../CHAPTER_01.md), approved 2026-11-26, supersedes the
former relief-visit, quarry and Sanctuary breach-of-trust spine. This is approved
story direction; its new scenes and playable roles are not implemented by the
documentation revision. She is
[Queen Althea's](../CHARACTERS.md#queen-althea) younger sister and Lucan Rooke's
employer. Althea reigns over **Adamanthra**. The dynasty's name, Iselin's age
and detailed succession rules remain unresolved; older proposed family names
are not promoted to canon.

Her appearance is approved Design A: white hair, blue eyes, royal-blue and silver
travelling clothes, navy mantle on anatomical right and braid forward over
anatomical left. The portrait and four directional idle/walk sets are approved.
Scene-specific full-body drawings follow the global sprite standard.

## Northern home and the journey

Adamanthra lies in northern Merovar in a very cold climate. Mountains enclose
it on the east, west and north; the only approach is from the south. The capital
is the party's eventual destination, but remains unnamed. Merovar is the larger
continent, not a synonym for her kingdom, and the Crown is not established as
ruling all of it. The [world atlas](../../world/WORLD_ATLAS.md#named-kingdoms)
owns the kingdom names, standard adjective forms and naming intentions.

The first half to roughly two-thirds of the intended adventure accompanies
Iselin on a long northward journey from far-southern Elden Vale through
several affected regions. Landscapes, settlements,
intermediate incidents and the precise itinerary still need development.
The opening party's first leg returns to Fire Veinhold; that immediate alliance
does not yet select the later northward departure trigger or itinerary.

### Return and parallel antiwar story

She eventually leaves the company's journey and returns to Adamanthra to try
to prevent the broader war with Carcovia. The opening already includes hostile
Carcovian action; these later efforts cannot presume that no attack has occurred.
Main-party travel continues with occasional
Iselin cutaways. The departure's timing, immediate trigger and diplomatic scenes
remain open; follow the shared chronology rather than inventing a second order.

## Existing ice magick

Growing up in the cold north, Iselin developed proficiency with ice magick and
is exceptionally gifted with it. Before the prologue mission, royal duties had
given her few recent occasions to use that talent. The approved playable
prologue now demonstrates her existing magick: an aether cannon breaks her ice
barrier, killing the ordinary Crown soldier accompanying her and Lucan.
This failed protection begins the opening's protective ice motif; it does not
establish a new acquisition of ice magick or the mechanics of every later ice
prison. Her precise opening command set and progression remain design work.

## Covert investigation — author knowledge

Before the game begins, the Crown has partial knowledge about the origins of
the regional phenomena and suspects an approaching catastrophe. It does not
know the full explanation. It dispatches Iselin primarily to investigate and
gather information. Her desire to help suffering communities remains sincere.

In the approved playable opening, Iselin, Lucan and an unnamed ordinary Crown
soldier infiltrate Fire Veinhold without shutting it down; the precise mission
objective remains open. They lose a scripted encounter against a boss too
powerful for them. In the following cutscene, its aether cannon breaks
Iselin's ice barrier and kills the soldier. Iselin and Lucan survive and retreat
to Eldenmere offscreen. The
[Chapter 1 chronology](../CHAPTER_01.md) owns the subsequent arrival, attack,
village cooperation and return to the damaged facility.

The mission is covert in the world, but the player now witnesses it from the
start. Do not retain the old rule that its existence must remain hidden from
players until the Sanctuary incident. Caelan, Mira, Serene and other characters
learn through the conversations and evidence actually authored; they do not
automatically share the player's prologue knowledge. Exactly what the Crown
already knows, what intelligence is sought or recovered, and what Iselin tells
each person remain open. Participation does not grant either survivor complete
knowledge of the harvesting network or the later supercannon.

The former unauthorized mining preparations and their accountability sequence
are retired as the mandatory opening spine. The natural Sanctuary confluence
remains, with its new narrative purpose undecided. Iselin's future Ice Reaper
identity retains its author/player knowledge boundary; showing her ordinary
ice magick does not disclose that future outcome.

## Eventual Ice Reaper arc — author knowledge

The long shared journey must give the party and player reasons to care about
her before her fall. Her fear and protective intent lead to the kingdom-sized
ice dome in response to Carcovia's cannon-backed ultimatum, followed by accusation
of treachery and exile. The dome also disrupts the Ice Worldvein; her protective
purpose does not exempt such an enormous working from its environmental cost.
The shared sequence and surface
interval are recorded in the [Game I chronology](../STORY_BIBLE.md#approved-later-progression-and-parallel-chronology).
Exile begins the final sequence; her transformation occurs afterward in the cave.
Her exile is unfair. The author-approved general direction now establishes her
sister's broken authorization and refusal of acknowledgment; the remaining
political procedure and enforcement circumstances still need development.

### Her sister's promise and betrayal

**General direction approved, 2026-11-24; not implemented:** Her sister Althea
privately authorizes the protective intervention and promises to support her,
then permits a false public account that Iselin acted against Crown orders.
The [Story Bible](../STORY_BIBLE.md#royal-authorization-and-betrayal) owns the
full decision sequence and the distinction between approved direction and
illustrative military logistics.

The relationship must include genuine affection, trust, disagreement and prior
support, so the betrayal breaks an experienced bond. Earlier correspondence
is one possible presentation, not an approved set of letters or invented
childhood events. The Crown's covert assignment remains part of their history
without making Iselin's sincere concern for the people fraudulent.

Althea regards commuting execution to exile as protecting her sister.
Iselin seeks public acknowledgment of what she was entrusted to do, and her
sister refuses. That refusal supplies a personal wound beyond loss of rank or
public approval. Exact hearing scenes and dialogue remain unwritten.

During confinement she can return to the promise, authorization, people saved,
public accusation and refusal. Her resentment eventually extends from those
responsible to the population collectively. The injustice is real; it does
not make that expansion of blame or her later murders justified. Real losses
caused by the dome also remain part of the history.

### Recurring stasis and dome symbolism

**Established direction, 2026-11-24:** Iselin repeatedly uses ice magick to trap
dangerous enemies in stasis during the story. This recurring action prepares
the symbolism of the enclosing dome and her eventual self-imprisonment. Her
attempts to hold threats and loss at bay culminate in preserving her own body
and restricting her ability to harm the kingdom. Specific earlier encounters,
release rules, and whether all other stasis subjects remain conscious are open.

### Playable exile and conscious confinement

**Established direction, 2026-11-24; not implemented:** After her exile, control
passes to Iselin for a substantial emotional roleplaying sequence.

1. Royal troops pursue her out of the kingdom. Across successive battle
   backgrounds, the kingdom recedes into the distance, making her separation
   visible. The troops' orders and precise route remain to be written.
2. In the mountain foothills she encounters local wildlife and kills animals
   to survive as her desperation increases.
3. During the ascent she encounters hikers who ridicule her as a branded
   traitor. The player can attempt lethal actions, but Iselin initially refuses.
   Several encounters develop this boundary until she loses her resolve and
   no longer refuses to kill them. Exact commands, nonlethal progress,
   encounter scripts, and handling players who never attempt killing remain
   design work; no additional moral branch is promised by this outline.
4. Near the summit she finds an ice-aspected cave and defeats its resident
   yeti, exhausting her strength. Desperate and starving, she encases herself
   in ice to preserve her life and keep the kingdom safe from her wrath.

Her body is preserved, but she remains conscious throughout the approximate
three-year confinement fixed by the shared chronology. She broods over the
exile and develops resentment and hatred. During confinement she absorbs
accumulating ice-aspected aether from the barrier, transforming into the Ice
Reaper while she is encased. Her absorption also
weakens the barrier and causes its existing failure. The cave remains naturally
ice-aspected; the exact Worldvein route connecting it to the barrier is open.
Upon emerging,
she is resolved to bury those she once swore to save, the intention reflected
in her battle theme. The barrier supplies the accumulating excess; no new pact
or relic is established. Physical transformation and her developing
intentions remain distinct parts of the arc.

The precise presentation, duration as she perceives it, physiological rules,
spell costs, quantities of absorbed aether, trigger for leaving self-stasis and remaining
events after emergence remain open. The
[aether foundation](../../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md#ice-dome-absorption-and-restoration)
establishes that defeating her is necessary to release the excess aether and
restore the Ice Worldvein. Her death is not established by that requirement.
These approvals do not implement the boss or settle her final fate and the
safe handling of the remaining physical dome. Her initial decision to restrain
herself remains part of her history after transformation.

### The kingdom's appeal and deliberate collapse

**Author-established direction, 2026-11-24; not implemented:** As the dome fails,
the kingdom turns to the woman it unfairly exiled as its only hope of rescue.
[Rhea leads the expedition](RHEA.md#expedition-leadership-and-captivity) sent to
find her. Iselin kills its other members and imprisons Rhea in ice. The
[Story Bible](../STORY_BIBLE.md#post-timeskip-aftermath-and-the-collapsing-dome)
owns the surrounding collapse, search and approach chronology.

Her resolve to bury those she once swore to save now has a concrete method:
deliberately collapse the dome onto the entire kingdom. She rejects Rhea's
attempt to reason with her. Their substantial
[confrontation](../../scenes/ICE_REAPER_PRE_BOSS.md#planned-confrontation-and-imprisonment)
reveals to the player how conscious confinement and brooding have developed
into bitterness, betrayal and revenge. The injustice of exile and her
responsibility for subsequent killings and collective punishment remain
separate facts; neither erases the other.

The ice imagery consequently develops from attempted protection and restraining
dangerous enemies to
enclosing a population for protection, confining herself, and finally turning
that protection into the means of burial. Existing structural failure is not
automatically a deliberate attack by her: absorption weakens the barrier during
confinement, while her later intention to collapse it is conscious revenge.
Why Rhea survives,
how she is captured, the exact exchange, and her final fate and aftermath remain open. No new
possession, magical command to hate, or literal internal disease is established
by this account of her emotional deterioration.

## Relationships and continuation

Lucan remains her personal guard and participates in the prologue mission.
Their shared survival and the soldier's death are established; their precise
responses and follow-up remain to be written. He is no longer introduced as
ignorant of the mission he has just taken part in. His later permanent party
recruitment retains its existing future scope.

Caelan and Mira cooperate with Iselin and Lucan during the ground assault and
the first journey back to Fire Veinhold. This establishes an immediate alliance,
not the final form of every party commitment or automatic trust. Serene remains
Caelan and Tomas's caregiver and the Sanctuary's keeper. Her new involvement and any
disagreement with Iselin require authoring; the removed concealed-crew dispute
does not remain an event in the replacement opening.

## Ruling-family development — proposals, not canon

Queen Althea and Adamanthra are approved names; earlier queen-name candidates
are superseded. Althea's brief supporting-character entry belongs to the
[cast index](../CHARACTERS.md#queen-althea).

The previously discussed House Maroven, Dowager Helena and Prince
Oren remain unapproved proposals, now associated with Iselin rather than her
predecessor. If developed, her relationship to the prince would be aunt rather
than uncle. No dynasty name, heir or inheritance rule is established by the
approved names. Proposed themes of service, responsibility and
wanting achievement to justify royal standing remain usable development notes.
Her covert Crown investigation is approved separately; these older family
proposals do not limit that mission or revive retired explanations of the crisis.

## Opening responsibilities and remaining decisions

The [replacement opening](../CHAPTER_01.md) owns Iselin's mission and first
alliance with the villagers. Carcovia tracks the survivors to Eldenmere; its
overcharged Fire weapon fires a fireball at the village, misses and destroys
the bridge, then self-destructs and damages much of Fire Veinhold. The return
objective is still open; a deadline to prevent that weapon's second shot is not
established after its destruction.

The exact facility location, intelligence, relationship between the guns,
military roles, repair plan, departure arrangements and reason for the longer
northward journey remain open. A cave bypass is not approved. Neither the
prologue nor the village emergency establishes Adamanthran rule over all of
Merovar or gives Caelan Crown policy authority. The former relief itinerary and
festival ending no longer determine her opening schedule.
