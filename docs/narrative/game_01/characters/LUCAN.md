## Combat progression concept

### Implemented Sanctuary guest appearance

Lucan assists in the cave as a **level-five guest** and earns no combat EXP.
Players command his Attack, Skills, Items, Guard and permitted Flee actions,
but his full Armsmaster mechanics and character interactable wait for permanent
recruitment later in the story. His guest skills establish his protective role:

- **Inspire:** 5 MP; all living active allies gain 50% Attack for five of their own turns.
- **Bolster:** 5 MP; all living active allies gain 50% Defense for five of their own turns.
- **Provoke:** 8 MP; normal enemies direct single-target hostile actions toward
  him for five of their own turns. Minibosses and bosses are immune.

Inspire and Bolster can coexist; recasting refreshes duration without stacking
the bonus. His newly granted self-buff does not lose a turn to its casting
action. Provoke preserves telegraphed-attack response opportunities and ends
if Lucan is incapacitated. He accompanies early healing trips, then leaves
after the cave boss is defeated and the party exits the cave. This departure
does not decide his later permanent recruitment or ultimate ability.

**Current author direction, 2026-11-07:** Lucan's combat identity is
**Armsmaster**, replacing Knight as his intended job. His employment as Aldric's
personal guard and his protective characterization remain established.
The arsenal and ultimate directions below remain future combat concepts; the
implemented guest skills above are a separate limited appearance.

### A growing arsenal

- Lucan wields a huge arsenal of **weapons and shields**, with different combat
  effects associated with different equipment.
- He unlocks more weapons and their effects as the game progresses.
- The existing sword-and-shield and two-handed-sword directions are examples
  within that larger arsenal, rather than his only eventual configurations.

The equipment list, individual effects, unlock methods and timing, and switching
or loadout rules remain open. No progression framework is chosen for these
unlocks.

### Ultimate possibility: expanded wielding

**Tentative:** Lucan's ultimate is not yet chosen. A possible direction is a
**five-turn** state that lets him wield more than one weapon at a time, or
hold a normally two-handed weapon in one hand so that he can use another weapon
or a shield in his offhand.

This is an idea to revisit, not a finalized ultimate or an established ordinary
wielding rule. The exact configuration, effects, duration counter, activation
requirements and name remain undecided.

## Voice and behavior

**Established:** Speaks decisively and often assumes he is in charge. Pride,
practical instruction and willingness to enter danger should all be audible.
His courage and skill must appear in actions, not only in boasts.

**Proposed writing guidance:**

- Use clear, deliberate sentences and concrete advice rather than constant courtly flourishes.
- Under immediate danger, shorten the speech and let useful action precede self-promotion.
- His humor can protect his dignity, but should not make every exchange a boast.
- Retain occasional deliberate wit while giving ordinary duties, concerns and guarded answers their own space.
- Show affection through attention and practical help; leave romantic preferences open.
- Let a difficult apology become specific and accountable, not another claim of superiority.
- Let practical questions and cooperation show care or respect without an announcement of the lesson he has learned.
- In later growth, preserve confidence while changing orders into genuine invitations.

**Avoid:** A cowardly fraud, a caricature who despises ordinary people, instant
perfect humility, or repeated catchphrases in place of emotional range.
Detailed mannerisms and behavior when safe remain proposals, not approved animation requirements.

## Show, Don't Tell

These five hypothetical scenarios express Lucan without spoken lines, internal
monologues or explanatory emotion captions. They illustrate established
competence, pride, overreach, loyalty and intended growth; the particular
events and actions are proposals for review, not approved quests or animations.

### Scenario 1 - Protection before performance

**Status:** Proposed for review.
**Stage:** Opening characterization.
**Setup:** On a narrow rocky path, loose stones begin rolling toward an
inexperienced traveler beside the party.

**Observable beats:**

- Lucan immediately turns his shield toward the falling stones and moves
  into the exposed side of the path.
- He gestures the traveler behind the shield, keeping his own footing
  between them and the hazard.
- He holds the protective position until the others are clear, then checks
  behind him rather than posing for their gratitude.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not wait for recognition or keep the safer
position for himself. His useful action precedes any display of importance.

**Character signal:** His confidence has a foundation in courage and practical
protection. The hazard should stay believable; the scene does not establish
invulnerability or a legendary feat.

### Scenario 2 - Taking the decision away

**Status:** Proposed for review.
**Stage:** Opening flaw, before the intended growth.
**Setup:** At a route-planning stop, Caelan points to an alternative on the
shared map while other party members are still considering it.

**Observable beats:**

- Lucan briefly raises a flat palm to stop the contributions, then folds
  the map before everyone has finished indicating their choices.
- He points out positions for the group and starts down his selected path,
  assuming they will follow.
- When the others remain at the map, he looks back impatiently instead
  of returning to make room for their judgment.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not wait for agreement. A potentially useful
discussion ends early, at a cost to trust and shared decision-making.

**Character signal:** He confuses protective responsibility with authority.
His route may be sensible; the flaw is treating it as his decision alone.
This is a scene proposal, not permission to force the player's movement.

### Scenario 3 - Enjoying being seen

**Status:** Proposed for review.
**Stage:** Opening characterization.
**Setup:** After a useful task is complete, nearby villagers acknowledge
Lucan with appreciative gestures and applause.

**Observable beats:**

- He straightens immediately and smooths his clothing before returning
  their acknowledgment.
- He gives his polished armor a quick glance and turns toward the people
  watching, visibly enjoying the attention.
- When someone still needs help with the last load, he notices and goes
  back to work, retaining the pleased expression.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He allows himself a moment of display rather than
pretending praise means nothing, but does not abandon the remaining work.

**Character signal:** Pride and sincere service coexist. He need not be a
fraud or a selfless saint for his appetite for recognition to be visible.

### Scenario 4 - Duty over applause

**Status:** Proposed for review.
**Stage:** Service as Aldric's personal guard.
**Setup:** During a public gathering, an appreciative group beckons Lucan
closer while Aldric moves to another part of the crowd.

**Observable beats:**

- Lucan starts to answer the beckoning gesture, then tracks Aldric's movement.
- He pauses, lowers the hand he had raised toward the group and shifts
  back into a position from which he can watch Aldric and the approach.
- He stays there while the others enjoy the gathering, attentive rather
  than attempting to reclaim its center.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He gives up recognition he genuinely wants instead
of leaving his protective assignment to pursue it.

**Character signal:** Loyalty to Aldric can outweigh his appetite for applause.
The setting does not establish an assassination threat, court secret or
particular political event.

### Scenario 5 - Respect made visible by waiting

**Status:** Proposed for review.
**Stage:** Possible later growth; not a fixed recruitment or late-story scene.
**Setup:** The party considers another route. Caelan studies the map while
the others indicate their preferred approaches.

**Observable beats:**

- Lucan begins to reach toward the map, then withdraws his hand and shifts
  aside so the others can finish.
- He studies the indicated route without folding the map or turning the
  group's pause into an impatient departure.
- Once Caelan starts leading, Lucan takes a protective position behind the
  group rather than automatically reclaiming the front.

**Inaction / trade-off:** He does not seize the decision or the visible place
of command. He accepts a useful role that is not the leading one.

**Character signal:** His competence remains, but protection now leaves room
for others' agency. Growth is not passive obedience; he can still contribute
judgment and act when there is a real danger.

## Ten dialogue and expression references

Example 1 preserves the existing approved voice reference. Examples 2-10,
all contexts and acting cues, and the proposed vulnerability explored in
example 5 are development proposals. None establishes an actual quest event,
a performed game line or a fixed recruitment script.
Dialogue structures were revised on 2026-11-04 following the approved
[template direction](TEMPLATE.md#dialogue-structure). Existing proposal statuses
for scene events and unapproved character interpretations remain in place.

### 1. Signature confidence - opening characterization

**Context:** A companion objects to his commanding manner during a dangerous task.

> "You may dislike my manners. I'd appreciate you surviving long enough to
> complain about them."

**Acting cue:** He moves into the exposed position himself rather than waiting for compliance.

**Character signal:** Irritating confidence backed by protective action.
**Dialogue status:** Existing voice reference retained from the approved companion foundations.

### 2. Reassurance under danger - opening characterization

**Context:** A frightened companion freezes before crossing an exposed stretch.

> "Look at me. You needn't be brave for the whole crossing. Just take the next
> step. I'll take it with you. There. Now the next."

**Acting cue:** His voice loses its theatrical polish; he matches their pace.

**Character signal:** Competence includes helping frightened people, not merely issuing orders.
**Status:** Proposed.

### 3. Pride after success - opening characterization

**Context:** A difficult maneuver works and someone offers him sincere praise.

> **Lucan:** "Thank you. That took a fair amount of practice."

*He accepts the praise openly before looking back toward the others.*

> **Lucan:** "Is everyone ready to move?"

**Acting cue:** Takes a moment to enjoy the acknowledgment, then checks the
group rather than turning the compliment into another joke.

**Character signal:** Pride appears in how he receives recognition; a practical
question returns attention to his responsibility for the others.
**Status:** Proposed.

### 4. Anger and overreach - opening flaw

**Context:** Caelan rejects Lucan's plan while people may be at risk.

> "If this goes wrong, I'll be the one trying to get you out. So yes, I expect
> you to listen when I tell you how to avoid it."

**Acting cue:** He steps too close and speaks as if the discussion has ended.
The scene should allow Caelan to challenge him.

**Character signal:** His concern is real; his assumption of authority is the flaw, not the scene's moral answer.
**Status:** Proposed.

### 5. Fear beneath ambition - proposed private vulnerability

**Context:** In a quiet, hypothetical conversation, someone asks why recognition matters so much.

> **Lucan:** "I'd like a command. Something I can build on. I'd like to make
> plans for my family."

*He looks down at his armor before continuing.*

> **Lucan:** "I worked hard to get this posting. I'm not keen to start again."

**Acting cue:** Leaves the answer there instead of explaining what recognition
would settle inside him. The pause interrupts his usual assured delivery.

**Character signal:** Practical ambitions and defensiveness suggest the
proposed insecurity without an interviewer prompting a complete self-analysis
or an invented past humiliation.
**Status:** Proposed, including the underlying fear.

### 6. Failure and apology - possible growth beat

**Context:** His insistence on his own plan has caused a setback; the specific event remains open.

> **Lucan:** "You told me, and I ignored you. I'm sorry."

*He takes a step back and waits.*

> **Lucan:** "What still needs doing?"

**Acting cue:** Does not resume directing the response or demand forgiveness;
he waits to learn where his help is wanted.

**Character signal:** Owning a specific action and offering practical help
shows accountability without a polished summary of his central flaw.
**Status:** Proposed; neither the failure nor this stage of growth is fixed.

### 7. Affection through practical care - developing friendship

**Context:** The party is arranging night watches. Caelan is exhausted, but
also concerned about Lucan taking another watch.

> **Lucan:** "I can take the watch tonight."
>
> **Caelan:** "You were on last night."
>
> **Lucan:** "I slept this afternoon. I don't mind."

**Acting cue:** Waits for Caelan's answer instead of assigning the watches himself.

**Character signal:** Both have practical concerns for the other. Lucan offers
care and leaves a choice without commenting on his own development.
**Status:** Proposed.

### 8. Humor when challenged - party banter

**Context:** Rhea jokes that he has checked his polished armor yet again.

> "Yes, Rhea, it's still armor. If looking after it has become unfashionable,
> you'll have to forgive me. I intend to be insufferable for many years yet."

**Acting cue:** Finishes the check, then gives her a look that invites the next joke.

**Character signal:** Pride can become shared humor rather than hostility.
**Status:** Proposed.

### 9. Loyalty and a new assignment - possible recruitment expression

**Context:** Aldric entrusts him to the party; the actual scene and wording remain open.

> **Lucan:** "Who will be guarding you?"
>
> **Aldric:** "I've made arrangements. You won't be leaving me unprotected."

*Lucan looks toward the party.*

> **Lucan:** "Then I'll need to speak with Caelan about the journey."

**Acting cue:** Turns toward the practical preparation after his concern has
been addressed. No replacement guard or recruitment checkpoint is assigned.

**Character signal:** Loyalty appears in the question he needs answered and
the responsibility he takes next, not a clever rephrasing of Aldric's request.
**Status:** Proposed voice sketch, not an approved recruitment script.

### 10. Respect without surrendering confidence - later growth

**Context:** Caelan and Lucan have different route preferences after rain.
This is a hypothetical later-growth situation, not a fixed story event.

> **Caelan:** "I'm taking the lower path."
>
> **Lucan:** "The footing will be worse after the rain."
>
> **Caelan:** "I know. I still think it's safer."

*Lucan checks the map again.*

> **Lucan:** "Give me a moment to move the supplies."

**Acting cue:** Follows through with the preparation instead of requiring
Caelan to agree with his preference or praise his willingness to cooperate.

**Character signal:** Respect can coexist with disagreement. His practical
cooperation shows the changed relationship to authority without a speech about it.
**Status:** Proposed illustration of the established growth direction.
