## The southern road

Before the opening morning, a sudden, extraordinarily powerful surge of
lightning destroyed the road connecting Eldenmere with the next town to its
south. This is the southward connection in the world-map geography, continuing
toward the Leth settlements. The neighbouring town's name, exact distance and
playable layout remain open. River Leth and Lower Leth are geographic names,
not a new name for that town.

The damage gives the road project a specific purpose: restore the connection
used by people and deliveries between these communities. Its destruction is
not ordinary wear or a general modernization project. The first playable
overworld now establishes a destroyed stone bridge across the western boundary
river, with road damage on both banks. Eldenmere's southern road bends west to
this crossing; the far-bank route continues toward the unnamed town south.
Guarded land gates are separate from the ruined crossing. Detours, repair
quantities and eventual restoration still need later quest design. The
classroom world-map artwork remains unchanged.

MQ02's stopped mill remains a separate local problem. The ridgeback tusker
felled the tree whose broken limb obstructs the intake. Fixing the mill does
not repair the southern road or explain the lightning.

## Scale and chronology of the incidents

Only a handful of similar events have occurred in different places by this
point in the story. Each can cause serious local damage, but their frequency
and extent have not become a widespread cataclysm. Eldenmere's ordinary work,
relationships and Festival of Light preparations continue alongside recovery.

The other incidents and their exact locations, dates and individual damage
remain unspecified. Do not invent an exact regional count or portray every
storm, monster or broken mechanism as another surge.

These recent lightning incidents precede Aldric's proposed excavation. They
are not punishment for his later decision, and the quarry request is not
their original cause. The small historic discharge recorded at the ridge
sanctuary is a separate older incident, not one of these recent road strikes.

## Aldric's purpose and resource pressure

Aldric has already travelled to a couple of the affected communities to help
organize aid and repairs. He reaches Eldenmere with firsthand knowledge of
their damage and outstanding needs. His concern and relief work are sincere.

Repairing several damaged places is straining the available materials. A
nearer stone seam offers a possible additional supply for regional recovery.
Long hauling distances and cart availability still matter, but saving money
on a routine road project is no longer the main explanation for his request.

His visit was arranged in advance; Serene already has the letter describing
his expected party. MQ02's completion controls the player's encounter with
that arrival, not Aldric's decision to travel. The repaired mill is useful
news Caelan can report, not the reason Aldric undertook the visit.

The Festival of Light provides a human and social setting for the visit, and
Aldric intends to see the villagers there. His immediate business is the
relief effort and a discussion with Serene about the protected ridge site.

## The protected seam and the unresolved request

The seam lies inside the marked reserve around an old ridge sanctuary near
Eldenmere, separate from the village sanctuary where Caelan lives. The
[covenant guide](../../world/SANCTUARY_COVENANT.md) defines the protected land,
ordinary permitted use, restricted excavation and shared custodial duties.

Serene openly shares an old plan and a maintenance account of workers injured
by an unexplained discharge while opening a passage. The plan does not
reliably establish where the buried foundations end. Neither record proves
that a new trial cut will cause a disaster.

She offers the stone set aside for courtyard repairs, meals for the road crew,
a joint surface inspection with Aldric's engineer and work to obtain missing
records. Aldric accepts the help, but it cannot supply all the damaged roads.
His supervised shallow trial-cut proposal remains disputed. MQ03 ends with
limited practical agreements and no endorsement or authorization of excavation.

## Approved arrival-cutscene goal

At the end of MQ02, show Aldric's caravan arriving in Eldenmere so the player
witnesses its arrival before finding the visitors already waiting in town.
The scene should follow the mill quest's acknowledged conclusion and preserve
the completed repair and rewards.

The arrival should introduce an expected relief visit: a capable, tired
administrator arriving after helping other communities. It can establish the
damaged southern connection and his intended discussion with Serene without
delivering the full covenant debate in the street.

The subsequent MQ03 preparations remain inside the sanctuary dining room,
with Serene preparing hospitality and Tomas helping. Caelan's role remains
welcoming and guiding the visitors, witnessing the cart dispute and helping
host them. He gains no legal or survey authority from the new context.

The [arrival guide](../../REGENT_ARRIVAL.md) documents the cinematic's dialogue,
shots, input ownership and saved handoff. The scene grants no new repair,
checkpoint-release or excavation facts.

## Knowledge and longer-term continuity

The lightning incidents subtly foreshadow a problem with the flow of aether.
They give the opening cinematic's reference to a potentially valley-destroying
surge a smaller early counterpart. This connection does not establish the
future scene's exact cause or explain the physical mechanism of the strikes.

Villagers, Aldric and Serene can know the damage and reports from other places
without knowing why the incidents occur. Observation, religious interpretation
and author knowledge remain distinct. Serene's public maintenance evidence is
separate from the inherited warning she privately examines after the festival.

The later Chapter 1 authorization and private-warning beat remain in place;
the excavation catastrophe belongs to Act I, Chapter 3. The hidden ancient
history, the atmosphere's nature and the larger cosmic cause remain subject
to the established disclosure limits. These revisions approve no new ancient
mechanism, divine punishment or identification of the original sacred temple.
