# Game I — CH01_MQ01: A Day Like Any Other

**Status:** CANONICAL QUEST NARRATIVE DESIGN v1.0 (approved sequence, three households, Tomas accompanies only part of the quest).  
**Scope:** Opening playable quest of Chapter 1, *Life in Eldenmere*. Dialogue below is the **approved first implementation script**; copyediting that preserves intent may occur during implementation. Mechanical numbers, art and map coordinates remain implementation decisions.  
**Continuity:** [Chapter 1](CHAPTER_01.md), [Act Outline](ACT_OUTLINE.md), [Story Bible](STORY_BIBLE.md), [Approved Character Facts](CHARACTERS.md). Character relationships are approved by the author. At the game opening, Caelan (spelled Caelen in this script) is **18** and Tomas is **14**; these ages are approved canon. Other ages, surnames, exact personal histories and wider character arcs remain working details pending separate approval.

## Narrative contract

The player starts in Caelen's sanctuary bedroom in Eldenmere on an ordinary festival-preparation morning. No lore prologue, ominous catastrophe, prophecy or supernatural omen. Through Serene's request and three households, establish ordinary generosity, modest magicks, grief and memory, working-class concerns, and local skepticism of institutional authority. **Tomas joins only for the sanctuary opening and first household visit, then leaves to fulfill his own preparations.** Remaining two visits can occur in either order. Player returns to Serene, then the failing mill sets up MQ02.

**Confirmed:** Exactly three households, one introductory companion segment, a meaningful task at each home, a warm resolution, no combat, and no mandatory side content. The impending catastrophe occurs in Act I Chapter 3, not here.

## Cast and interactables

- **Caelen (Caelan), 18:** Playable sanctuary acolyte, practical and inquisitive; Tomas's older foster brother.
- **Tomas, 14:** Caelan's younger foster brother, temporary following companion; leaves after the first household. *No controllable party system is required by this script.*
- **Mother Serene:** Guardian and sanctuary caretaker; gives and concludes quest.
- **Master Halden:** Elder miller, proud and sore-jointed; later brings MQ02 hook.
- **Elira:** Widow near bridge, warm humor and remembrance.
- **Venn household:** Farming parents and a recovering child on eastern terrace. Names of parents/child intentionally OPEN.
- **Sanctuary:** Caelen's room, hall, dining area, courtyard.
- **Home A:** Mill-side cottage/storehouse; heavy sacks.
- **Home B:** Bridge cottage; memorial lantern on high shelf.
- **Home C:** Eastern terrace home; irrigation gate.

Keep setting descriptions local; in-world beliefs about life returning to the gods are **belief**, not proof of the real cosmology.

## Critical-path scene order

`S01 bedroom → S02 breakfast → S03 Serene's request → S04 Halden (Tomas present, then leaves) → S05 Elira / S06 Venn (either order) → S07 return to Serene → S08 Halden arrives, MQ02 unlocked.`

Because Tomas's departure must occur after Halden, **Halden is the mandatory first stop**. The subsequent Elira/Venn order is player-selected. Players can explore or speak with optional NPCs between scenes.

### S01 — Morning bells, sanctuary bedroom

**Trigger:** New game / first playable chapter entry. Fade from black; distant bell, birds and stream. Show Caelen asleep, brief knock. Transfer control immediately following exchange.

**Dialogue:**

- **Tomas (through door):** "Caelen? You're awake, aren't you?"
- **Tomas:** "Mother Serene says if you're still in bed when the last bell rings, you're helping me clean the courtyard."
- **Caelen:** "She didn't say that."
- **Tomas:** "No. But she might, once I suggest it."
- **Caelen:** "I'm getting up."
- **Tomas:** "Breakfast won't wait forever!"

**Gameplay:** Player moves, opens door, may inspect room. No forced checklist.

**Optional inspect lines:**
- Bookshelf — Caelen: "Two chapters left. I keep telling myself I'll finish them before festival week."
- Desk notes — Caelen: "Copies of the old local inscriptions. Serene says they're easier to read once I stop adding my own guesses."
- Childhood family drawing — Caelen: "Tomas insisted that's me. I'm still not sure why he gave me such enormous ears."
- Window — Caelen: "Eldenmere's already awake. It smells like somebody started baking before sunrise."
- Wardrobe — Caelen: "Clean robes. Mostly clean, anyway."
- Bed after waking — Caelen: "Tempting. Serene would notice."

**Exit:** Player walks to communal dining area; no timed fail state.

### S02 — Breakfast and living family dynamic

**Trigger:** First entrance dining hall. Serene organizes provisions while Tomas eats.

**Core dialogue:**
- **Tomas:** "There he is. I was beginning to think we'd have to send a search party."
- **Caelen:** "You'd have eaten my breakfast before they found me."
- **Tomas:** "I considered it. But Mother Serene has eyes everywhere."
- **Serene (without looking up):** "Only where they're needed."
- *(Tomas pushes a plate toward Caelen.)*
- **Serene:** "Sit and eat. There'll be plenty to do before the festival."
- **Caelen:** "You said that yesterday."
- **Serene:** "And it was true yesterday."

**Interaction:** Player speaks to Serene to accept task; Tomas offers nonessential remarks if approached. No inventory/menu exposition sequence.

### S03 — Serene assigns three visits

**Trigger:** Interact with Serene after breakfast exchange.

- **Serene:** "I promised to look in on three households, but there's more here than I can leave unattended."
- **Caelen:** "Who needs us?"
- **Serene:** "Halden by the mill needs medicine. Elira needs food and lamp oil. And the Venn family could use supplies while their youngest recovers."
- **Caelen:** "I'll see to them."
- **Serene:** "Thank you. Take Tomas to Halden first—he's been asking to help."
- **Tomas:** "I have been asking to do something interesting."
- **Serene:** "Then start by helping someone who needs you."
- **Serene:** "And Caelen? Eat something before you go."
- **Caelen:** "I did."
- **Serene:** "Half a slice of bread is not a meal."

**Systems:** Add three deliveries to journal; mark Halden as first destination, Elira and Venn initially listed as queued. Player receives supply bundle as quest abstraction; do not require inventory micromanagement. Tomas follows Caelen until Halden visit finishes.

**Journal text:** "Serene has asked me to check on Halden, Elira, and the Venn family. Tomas is coming along to Halden's place first."

### S04 — Master Halden, first household, Tomas departs

**Trigger:** Approach Halden's property with Tomas present. Medicine delivery starts short practical task.

- **Halden:** "Serene sent you? I'm not so frail that I need someone counting my aches."
- **Caelen:** "She sent the medicine you asked about last week."
- **Halden:** "I said I'd think about asking."
- **Tomas:** "That's practically a yes, for you."
- **Halden:** "And which one of you came to do actual work?"

**Action:** Player shifts three manageable sacks from exposed entry to storage; Tomas carries or steadies one in a short animation. Teach context interaction with no failures or timers. Keep Halden's difficulty credible; don't force Caelen to shame him.

**Completion exchange:**
- **Halden:** "I spent forty years carrying these myself."
- **Caelen:** "Then perhaps you've earned the right to let someone else carry a few."
- **Halden:** "Hmph. Tell Serene I'll take the medicine."
- **Halden (looking at mill):** "And if she asks, the wheel's been making a dreadful racket. I'll have it looked at."
- **Tomas:** "Mira would know what to do."
- **Caelen:** "Maybe. If Halden asks for help."
- **Halden:** "I heard that."

**Tomas exit:** He says Serene expects him back to help prepare for the festival, and leaves along the village path. This is the **end of his escort segment**; he does not follow to Elira or Venn.

- **Tomas:** "I'd better get back. Serene said she'd need another pair of hands."
- **Caelen:** "Go on. And try not to turn the courtyard into a workshop."
- **Tomas:** "No promises."

**Journal update:** "Halden has his medicine. Tomas headed back to the sanctuary. Elira and the Venn family still need visits." Unlock either remaining household in player-chosen order.

### S05 — Elira, bridge cottage (order-flexible)

**Trigger:** Deliver food/lamp oil to Elira. Fetch a festival lantern from high shelf using nearby step stool or reachable interaction. No supernatural spell is necessary.

- **Elira:** "A delivery from Serene? I swear she thinks I live on bread and stubbornness."
- **Caelen:** "She mentioned lamp oil, too."
- **Elira:** "Good. There's a lantern up there, if you'd be kind enough."
- *(Player retrieves lantern.)*
- **Caelen:** "Is this the one for your husband?"
- **Elira:** "It is. He used to say he'd only come to hear me sing. The old liar couldn't carry a tune himself."
- **Caelen:** "Does preparing it make you sad?"
- **Elira:** "Some days. But remembering him isn't the same as losing him again."
- **Elira:** "You will come to the festival, won't you?"
- **Caelen:** "I wouldn't miss it."
- **Elira:** "Good. Tell Serene I'll bring the lantern myself."

**Interaction:** Player handles lantern respectfully; optional inspect may show hand-painted initials, no secret clue. The festival is presented as a community expression of remembrance, not as a lecture on souls.

**Journal update:** "Elira has the food and oil and will bring her lantern to the festival."

### S06 — Venn family, eastern terrace (order-flexible)

**Trigger:** Deliver recovery supplies. A small irrigation gate is jammed or misaligned; water is failing to reach the family's plot. No dangerous magic surge.

**Dialogue:**
- **Venn parent A:** "Please thank Serene. The little one's finally keeping food down."
- **Venn parent B:** "Now if only the channel would cooperate."
- **Caelen:** "May I take a look?"
- **Venn parent A:** "Please do. Our tools aren't much, but they're yours to borrow."

**Player task:** Inspect two components (gate pin, water channel) and choose a valid repair method. Both choices work, teach agency, and **do not** affect major plot outcomes:
- **Practical approach:** Set pin with borrowed tools and flush debris.
- **Modest magicks:** Shift a small flow of existing water to relieve pressure, then set pin by hand.

**Resolution dialogue:**
- **Venn parent B:** "They've got pumps in the capital that could fill these channels in half the time."
- **Venn parent A:** "And payments that would take twice as long to finish."
- **Caelen:** "For today, at least, we've got the water moving again."
- **Venn parent A:** "You've done us a kindness. Let Serene know we'll be all right."

**Continuity:** This does not create water or aether; if magicks are selected, they manipulate the local existing water flow using accessible aether. No claims of unlimited energy.

**Journal update:** "The Venn family has the supplies they needed. The irrigation channel works again."

### S07 — Report to Serene

**Trigger:** Both S05 and S06 complete; return and speak with Serene at sanctuary.

- **Serene:** "There you are. How is everyone?"
- **Caelen:** "Halden finally accepted the medicine. Elira's bringing her lantern. The Venns have their irrigation running again."
- **Serene:** "And how are they, aside from what you fixed?"
- **Caelen:** "Halden's doing more than his joints can bear. Elira misses her husband. And the Venns worry what next season will cost them."
- **Serene:** "It's easy to think our work is finished when the baskets are empty."
- **Caelen:** "You don't think we helped?"
- **Serene:** "Of course we did. But tomorrow they'll still be carrying the things we couldn't take from them today."
- **Caelen:** "Then I suppose we keep showing up."
- **Serene:** "That's a good place to begin."

**Narrative completion:** Relief is meaningful but not a magic fix for structural problems. No currency/experience required by narrative specification.

### S08 — Mill hook and quest transition

**Trigger:** Immediately after S07 conclusion, Halden enters or calls from courtyard.

- **Halden:** "Caelen! That dreadful wheel's finally stopped altogether."
- **Caelen:** "You said it was making a noise."
- **Halden:** "And now it isn't making *any* noise. That's worse!"
- **Serene:** "Did you ask Mira to look at it?"
- **Halden:** "That's why I'm here. Someone fetch her before festival flour becomes festival dust."
- **Caelen:** "I'll go with her."
- **Serene:** "Then be careful along the channel."

**Result:** Mark MQ01 complete, unlock **CH01_MQ02 — A Little Trouble at the Mill**. The player remains free to explore, and a natural objective points toward Mira.

## State and quest flags

| Flag | Set when | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CH01_MQ01_STARTED` | S03 | Delivery task accepted |
| `CH01_MQ01_HALDEN_DONE` | S04 | Halden visited; practical task and medicine done |
| `CH01_MQ01_TOMAS_DEPARTED` | S04 exit | Tomas no longer following |
| `CH01_MQ01_ELIRA_DONE` | S05 | Lantern retrieved, delivery completed |
| `CH01_MQ01_VENN_DONE` | S06 | Gate repaired, delivery completed |
| `CH01_MQ01_REPORTED` | S07 | Caelen reports all visits to Serene |
| `CH01_MQ01_COMPLETE` | S08 | MQ01 completed and MQ02 unlocked |

**Gate:** S07 available iff HALDEN_DONE, ELIRA_DONE, VENN_DONE, TOMAS_DEPARTED. S05 and S06 may complete in either order. Repair choice may be stored as `CH01_MQ01_VENN_METHOD` with values `tools` or `magicks` for dialogue flavor only.

**Idempotence:** Household flags prevent duplicate supplies, repeated repair tasks, or Tomas rejoining on revisit. If player leaves during a task, preserve local task progress. If a story interaction is skipped, any necessary tutorial hint may be repeated without resetting flags.

## Optional conversations and exploration

- In the sanctuary, other acolytes can comment on preparation workload or Caelen's dedication; no mandatory lore speech.
- Around the village, villagers may praise sanctuary care or question higher religious authorities' influence without turning into ideological mouthpieces.
- Children may play at being travelers, making Tomas's own ambitions feel ordinary.
- After visiting Elira, Caelen can notice festival lanterns being set along the bridge.
- If optional characters return in later chapters, acknowledge previous interactions where feasible without locking essential survival outcomes behind side quests.

## Player experience and acceptance criteria

1. Player control follows the opening exchange promptly; no required prologue.
2. The **three household** structure is clear, and each has a unique emotional and interactive purpose.
3. Halden is first because Tomas participates there; **Tomas accompanies only the early segment** and exits before Elira and Venn.
4. Elira and Venn can be completed in either order; journal and flags handle both.
5. No combat or cosmic revelation in MQ01.
6. Minor player agency exists at Venn gate without fake high-stakes branching.
7. Serene, Tomas, Halden, Elira, and Venn family act like people with lives beyond Caelen.
8. The sanctuary's benefits are apparent without requiring everyone to agree with its wider hierarchy.
9. Magic never creates aether or water from nothing.
10. The conclusion has narrative payoff, then naturally opens the mill adventure.
