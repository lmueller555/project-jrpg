**Status:** Current implementation script for the approved opening dialogue and worldbuilding pass; three households, with Tomas accompanying only the early segment.  
**Scope:** Opening playable quest of Chapter 1, *Life in Eldenmere*. Speaker-attributed dialogue below matches the current runtime JSON, including the user-requested asterisk-wrapped emotes. Plain dialogue and emotes share the existing text renderer; emotes describe tone or gestures without adding animation requirements.

## Narrative contract

After the separate [late-journey opening cinematic](../../OPENING_CINEMATIC.md),
the playable present begins in Caelan's sanctuary bedroom in Eldenmere on an
ordinary festival-preparation morning. The flashforward introduces no prophecy,
cosmological explanation or catastrophe in Chapter 1. Through Serene's request
and three households, establish ordinary generosity, modest magicks, grief and
memory, working-class concerns, and local skepticism of institutional authority.
**Tomas joins only for the sanctuary opening and first household visit, then
leaves to fulfill his own preparations.** Remaining two visits can occur in
either order. Player returns to Serene, then the failing mill sets up MQ02.

**Confirmed:** Exactly three households, one introductory companion segment, a meaningful task at each home, a warm resolution, no combat, and no mandatory side content. The impending catastrophe occurs in Act I Chapter 3, not here.

## Cast and interactables

- **Caelan, 18:** Playable sanctuary helper, practical and inquisitive; Tomas's older foster brother.
- **Tomas, 14:** Caelan's younger foster brother, temporary following companion; leaves after the first household. *No controllable party system is required by this script.*
- **Mother Serene:** Guardian and sanctuary caretaker; gives and concludes quest.
- **Master Halden:** Elder miller, proud and sore-jointed; later brings MQ02 hook.
- **Elira:** Widow near bridge, warm humor and remembrance.
- **Venn household:** Farming parents and a recovering child on eastern terrace. Names of parents/child intentionally OPEN.
- **Sanctuary:** Caelan's room, hall, dining area, courtyard.
- **Home A:** Mill-side cottage/storehouse; heavy sacks.
- **Home B:** Bridge cottage; memorial lantern on high shelf.
- **Home C:** Eastern terrace home; irrigation gate.

Keep setting descriptions local; in-world beliefs about life returning to the gods are **belief**, not proof of the real cosmology.

## Critical-path scene order

`New Game flashforward → S01 bedroom → S02 breakfast → S03 Serene's request → S04 Halden (Tomas present, then leaves) → S05 Elira / S06 Venn (either order) → S07 return to Serene → S08 Halden arrives, MQ02 unlocked.`

Because Tomas's departure must occur after Halden, **Halden is the mandatory first stop**. The subsequent Elira/Venn order is player-selected. Players can explore or speak with optional NPCs between scenes.

The Festival of Light is tomorrow. Serene names every recipient, gives a recognizable landmark and explains an immediate need. Flour, bread, lamp oil, lanterns and preparation work connect the visits to the coming celebration. These essentials belong to the main path; optional exploration can add perspectives without supplying missing objectives.

### S04 — Master Halden, first household

**Trigger:** Visit Halden at his cottage south of the mill, across the upper bridge. The medicine handoff begins the existing practical task. Flour for tomorrow’s baking establishes his work before the mill quest.

- **Halden:** “*grumbles* Serene sent you? I’m not so frail that I need someone counting my aches.”

- **Tomas:** “*grins* That’s practically a yes, for you.”
- **Halden:** “Hmph. And which one of you came to do actual work?”
- **Caelan:** “What needs moving?”
- **Halden:** “Those three sacks of flour by the door. Onto the dry pallet. I’ve more coming through the mill and nowhere sensible to put it.”
- **Tomas:** “All this for tomorrow?”

- **Caelan:** “We’ll make some room.”
- **Halden:** “One sack at a time. They’re heavier than they look.”
- **Tomas:** “I’ll take the middle one.”
- **Halden:** “You’ll wait until your brother’s ready.”

**Action:** Move the three manageable flour sacks to the dry pallet. Tomas handles his existing share only when Caelan is ready. Keep the original carry poses, contacts, collision and untimed interaction.

**Trigger:** Deliver Elira’s bread and lamp oil. This visit may follow or precede the Venns once Halden and Tomas’s departure are complete.

- **Elira:** “*with a wry smile* A delivery from Serene? I swear she thinks I live on bread and stubbornness.”

- **Elira:** “Sensible woman. I was nearly out. Come in, Caelan.”
- **Caelan:** “Getting ready for tomorrow?”

- **Caelan:** “Of course.”

**After safe placement — `resolution`:**

Elira’s humor and independent plans precede the quieter remembrance. Her lantern belongs to the same ongoing festival preparation later revisited in MQ04. Her occupation and additional biography remain unassigned.

- **Elira:** “*looks over the lantern* Thank you. I can clean it here.”
- **Caelan:** “You’re carrying this one for your husband again?”

- **Caelan:** “Did he?”

### S06 — Venn family, eastern terrace

**Trigger:** Deliver the existing recovery supplies. The parents’ concerns introduce their farming plot and child’s recovery without assigning the child a name, gender or age.

- **Venn mother:** “Caelan. Is that from the sanctuary?”

- **Venn mother:** “What’s happened now?”

- **Caelan:** “May I take a look?”
- **Venn father:** “Please. It’s east of the cottage. Look at the pin, then the channel below it.”
- **Venn mother:** “Borrow what you need from the tool box.”
- **Caelan:** “I’ll see what I can do.”

**Inspect the gate pin — `pin`:**

Approach the actual pin east of the cottage.

- **Caelan:** “The pin’s caught at an angle. I’ll need to take the pressure off before I set it straight.”

**Inspect the channel — `channel`:**

Inspect the channel below the gate; the obstruction remains ordinary and visible.

- **Caelan:** “Twigs have collected below the gate. I’ll need to clear those, too.”

**Choose an approach — `choice`:**

The existing choices remain **Use borrowed tools** and **Try magicks**.

- **Caelan:** “I could use their tools. Or perhaps I can push the water back long enough to free the pin.”

**Practical approach:** Use the borrowed mallet and hook to set the pin, clear debris and raise the gate.

**Magick attempt:** A trace of blue energy gathers and fades. The flowing water, pin, gate and debris do not move. Caelan keeps the approved standing silhouette and normal arm, hip, leg and boot registration; there is no collapse or dangerous surge.

**After the failed attempt — `magicks_failed`:**

Only players who chose magicks hear this line. Its acknowledgment starts the practical repair.

- **Caelan:** “*quietly* I can’t move it. I’ll need the tools.”

The attempt is deterministic and costs no resource or retry roll. Walking away or reloading preserves the failed-attempt milestone and the need for tools. Choosing tools directly omits this attempt. Caelan already knows some magicks; the demonstrated limitation is insufficient force against this flowing load, not general ignorance or an empty spell repertoire.

**After the tool repair — `resolution`:**

Return to the parents for the completed exchange. Their view of capital machinery introduces useful technology and financial pressure through an immediate household concern; festival attendance remains dependent on the child’s condition.

- **Venn father:** “There it goes. That should reach the far end now.”
- **Venn mother:** “One less thing to worry about.”
- **Caelan:** “Has it been catching often?”
- **Venn father:** “Often enough. They’ve got pumps in the capital that could fill these channels in half the time.”
- **Venn mother:** “And payments that would take twice as long to finish.”
- **Venn father:** “*defensively* I know. I’m only saying it would be useful.”
- **Caelan:** “For today, at least, you’ve got the water moving again.”
- **Venn mother:** “You’ve done us a kindness. Let Serene know we’ll be all right.”
- **Caelan:** “I will. I hope we see you at the festival.”
- **Venn mother:** “We’ll see how the little one feels. I’d like that.”
- **Venn father:** “And if we do get there, I intend to sit down for a while.”
- **Venn mother:** “*with a small laugh* You say that now.”
- **Caelan:** “I’ll leave you to it. Take care.”

**Continuity:** The attempt creates neither water nor aether. Only the physical repair restores the channel. Existing completed repairs remain completed; the two initial choices do not change the final household outcome.

### S07 — Report to Serene

**Trigger:** Finish both remaining households and return to Serene. The report recognizes wellbeing, work and unresolved concerns rather than treating people as completed delivery tasks.

- **Serene:** “There you are. How is everyone?”
- **Caelan:** “Halden finally accepted the medicine. We moved his flour sacks into the dry.”
- **Serene:** “Did he let you help without an argument?”
- **Caelan:** “Not entirely.”
- **Serene:** “*smiles* I’d have worried if he had.”
- **Caelan:** “Elira has the bread and oil. She’s bringing her husband’s lantern tomorrow.”
- **Serene:** “Good. I’m glad she wants to come.”
- **Caelan:** “And the Venns have their supplies. The little one’s keeping food down. Their irrigation gate was caught, so I got the channel running again.”
- **Serene:** “You’ve had a busy morning.”
- **Caelan:** “A useful one, I think.”
- **Serene:** “You have. And how were they, aside from what needed fixing?”
- **Caelan:** “Halden’s doing more than his joints can bear. Elira misses her husband. The Venns are tired—and worried about what next season will cost them.”
- **Serene:** “I’m glad you stayed long enough to hear that.”
- **Caelan:** “You knew I’d find more than deliveries.”
- **Serene:** “I hoped you’d have time for people. It’s easy to think our work is finished when the baskets are empty.”
- **Caelan:** “Then I suppose we keep showing up.”
- **Serene:** “*warmly* That’s a good place to begin.”

**Narrative result:** The sanctuary’s practical care is visible through specific people. Helping matters without removing every difficulty. The conversation requires no currency or experience reward to make its narrative point.

**Result:** Complete MQ01 and unlock **CH01_MQ02 — A Little Trouble at the Mill** after the acknowledged handoff. Mira remains at her existing outdoor workbench. The player remains free to explore; MQ02 is not accepted until its reunion is finished.

## Player experience and acceptance criteria

1. Player control follows the opening exchange promptly; no required prologue.
2. The **three household** structure is clear, and each has a unique emotional and interactive purpose.
3. Halden is first because Tomas participates there; **Tomas accompanies only the early segment** and exits before Elira and Venn.
4. Elira and Venn can be completed in either order; journal and flags handle both.
5. No combat or cosmic revelation in MQ01.
6. Minor player agency exists at Venn gate without fake high-stakes branching.
7. Serene, Tomas, Halden, Elira, and Venn family act like people with lives beyond Caelan.
8. The sanctuary's benefits are apparent without requiring everyone to agree with its wider hierarchy.
9. Magic never creates aether or water from nothing.
10. The conclusion has narrative payoff, then naturally opens the mill adventure.

**Implementation boundary:** Existing controllers remain authoritative for map positions, collision, animation contacts, inventory handoffs and save validation. This pass changes the dialogue and its delivery, not quest outcomes, optional-character biographies or the approved magick limitation. Optional exploration ideas above do not become implemented content solely by appearing in this script.
