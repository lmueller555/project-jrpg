## The land under protection

**Elden Vale Sanctuary** stands on the wooded ridge northeast of Eldenmere.
It is separate from **Serene's home** in the village, where Serene, Caelan and
Tomas live and offer community care. Its location and reserve are retained
regional background; they do not identify the site of the Fire Veinhold.

Marked boundaries enclose sanctuary buildings, a wooded hill and buried
foundations extending beyond the visible walls. Protection applies beneath
the reserve as well as on its surface. The complete construction history and
buried layout remain open. Their existence does not establish a mechanism
controlling planetary circulation.

The former story's protected stone seam, proposed quarry and concealed Crown
deployment no longer define an approved future conflict here. They must not
be used to reconstruct the retired plot around the revised opening.

## Why this reserve matters

Serene practices a planetary spiritual tradition centered on care,
remembrance and responsibility rather than worship of gods. The Sanctuary
was deliberately constructed near a **real natural aether confluence** to
guide the region's dead back to the planet and remember them. Its living
memorial grove forms part of that continuing service.

The confluence belongs to the natural Worldvein circulation. Its exact
behavior, the practices of guidance and its relationship to the buried works
remain open. It is not an unlimited energy source or a confirmed artificial
planetary mechanism. The trees are living memorials, not confirmed vessels
for particular souls.

## Sacred keeping and public responsibility

The sanctuary ground is held in keeping across generations, rather than
treated as a resource belonging solely to its present custodian. Civil
authorities recognize the reserve and the custodians' duties toward it.

Civil authorities, sanctuary keepers and the surrounding community share
responsibility for that keeping. The covenant is a custodial trust, not the
order's unrestricted ownership. Serene cannot personally surrender the
reserve's protection or move its boundaries.

Custody also carries service obligations. Preserving the site and its records
belongs alongside care, food, practical assistance and responsibility toward
the surrounding community. These duties remain useful setting foundations
without committing the story to a particular dispute.

The covenant's exact founding date, original participants, formal name and
complete institutional history remain open. No new religious hierarchy or
detailed civil constitution is established here.

The reserve is usable land. Its protection does not prohibit every footstep,
tool or ordinary act of maintenance.

## The public evidence behind Serene's caution

Two older public records can remain local background:

- A plan showing foundations continuing beneath the wooded hill beyond the
  standing walls.
- An account of workers injured by an unexplained discharge when they opened
  a passage, which was closed afterward.

The injury account describes a small historical event. It is not the new
bridge attack, proof of a shared cause or an explanation of the Fire Veinhold.
The records justify caution without proving that every shallow cut is
dangerous or that excavation must cause a catastrophe. They confirm neither
a curse nor divine punishment, and prescribe no required on-screen discovery.
