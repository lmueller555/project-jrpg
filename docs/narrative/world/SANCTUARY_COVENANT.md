# Elden Vale Sanctuary — Covenant

**Status:** Approved setting and narrative canon. The guidance-of-the-dead
and MQ03 emergency revision supersedes the earlier account of a mutually
honest negotiation ending in limited inspection agreements. The custodial
terms remain in place.

**Quest continuity:** [MQ03](../game_01/CH01_MQ03.md) owns the implemented
emergency, rescue and return progression. This guide owns the custodial terms
and public evidence used by that story.

**Scope:** Elden Vale Sanctuary near Eldenmere, its public covenant, its
service to the dead and the breach of trust exposed in Chapter 1.

This guide records the approved local facts for reuse in later scenes. It
expands the [Elden Vale regional setting](regions/ELDEN_VALE.md) and informs
[MQ03 — The Regent's Road](../game_01/CH01_MQ03.md) and the
[Chapter 1 closing authorization](../game_01/CHAPTER_01.md).
The [Foundational Lore](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md) and
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md) retain authority over
the natural aether system and Game I's disclosure limits.

## The land under protection

The proposed quarry is near **Elden Vale Sanctuary**, on the wooded ridge
northeast of Eldenmere. This is a separate site from **Serene's home** in the
village, where Serene, Caelan and Tomas live and offer community care. The
dispute does not concern quarrying underneath their home.

Marked boundaries enclose the old sanctuary's reserve. The reserve includes
the sanctuary buildings, a wooded hill and buried foundations extending beyond
the visible walls. Protection applies to the ground beneath the reserve as
well as its surface.

Iselin's proposed stone seam lies inside that boundary, although the proposed
work is clear of the standing walls. Neither party disputes that location.
Serene opposes disrupting the caretakers' duty to guide the region's dead.
Iselin has secretly deployed men to establish Crown control and prepare mining
before securing her agreement. The exposed deployment makes the conflict a
breach of trust as well as a dispute over the use of protected land.

The old sanctuary's complete construction history and buried layout remain
unknown to the people involved. Its natural confluence does not establish that
the buried works control planetary circulation.

## Why this reserve is sacred

Serene practices a [planetary spiritual tradition](ELDEN_VALE_FAITH.md) that
does not center gods. Elden Vale Sanctuary was deliberately constructed near
a **real natural aether confluence** to guide the region's dead back to the
planet and remember them. Its living memorial grove forms part of that
continuing care. Mining would disrupt the Sanctuary's central service.

The confluence belongs to the natural planetary circulation described by the
[Worldvein foundations](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md).
Its detailed workings and its relationship to the buried foundations remain
imperfectly understood. It is not an unlimited aether source or an established
artificial planetary mechanism.

The community's shared remembrance, care for the grove and long keeping of
this natural place give the whole reserve significance beyond its standing
walls. Neither those practices nor the confluence establishes that individual
souls persist in trees or that any excavation must cause a catastrophe.

## Sacred keeping and public responsibility

Life and nature consist of aether. When living beings die, their aether
returns to the planet's life cycle. Helping the region's dead make that return
is an actual responsibility understood by Serene and the Sanctuary keepers.
The exact practices and what happens to personal consciousness after return
remain open.

The sanctuary ground is held in keeping across generations, rather than
treated as a resource belonging solely to its present custodian. This
custodial duty is held sincerely by the community; it does not depend on
knowing every detail of the site's construction or physical workings.

The covenant gives that keeping a recognized public form. Civil authorities
recognize the reserve and the custodians' duties toward it. Its protection is
therefore more than a personal objection by Serene or a claim that the order
may do whatever it wishes with its land.

Civil authorities, sanctuary keepers and the surrounding community share
responsibility for that keeping. The covenant is a custodial trust, not the
order's unrestricted ownership of the reserve.

Custody also carries service obligations. Preserving the site and its records
belongs alongside care, food, practical assistance and responsibility toward
the surrounding community. The people needing the road are among those the
custodians serve. Protection does not release Serene from helping them.

The covenant's exact founding date, the people who first agreed it, its formal
name and its complete institutional history remain open. No new religious
hierarchy or detailed civil constitution is established here.

## Uses, limits and permission

The reserve remains usable land. Its protected status does not forbid every
footstep, tool or ordinary act of maintenance.

| Activity | Established position |
| --- | --- |
| Existing paths and ordinary passage | Permitted within the reserve's established limits |
| Grazing and woodland care | Permitted within those limits |
| Ordinary repairs | Permitted within established maintenance limits; not a general license to open buried works |
| Surface inspection and surveying | Permitted without opening the ground or disturbing buried construction |
| Opening a quarry face or driving a shaft | Restricted; requires joint review and exceptional authorization |
| Opening or disturbing buried works | Restricted; requires joint review and exceptional authorization |
| Moving or surrendering the protected boundary | Beyond the local custodian's authority |

Serene can cooperate with surface inspection and ordinary work within her
custodial responsibilities. She cannot personally sell away the protection,
redraw the boundary or turn a new quarry into ordinary maintenance.

Joint review brings the custodians' records and knowledge together with the
surveyors' and engineers' assessment. It is a substantive examination of the
proposed work, rather than a requirement that the government accept a claim
without being allowed to investigate it.

The chapter-ending formal authorization remains a later escalation. This
guide does not invent an emergency override clause, establish the full permit
procedure or settle every legal question about that authorization. Iselin's
secret advance deployment is not a new permission granted by Serene. Current
quest progress and completion are recorded in MQ03 rather than this covenant.

## The public evidence behind Serene's caution

Serene's principal objection concerns the Sanctuary's known service to the
dead. She also has concrete safety concerns: caretakers have heard growling
from the cave since the bridge incident and have kept people away.

The growling report does not identify a creature or prove what caused it.
Older public records remain separate background evidence:

- An old plan showing foundations continuing beneath the wooded hill, beyond
  the standing sanctuary walls.
- An old account of workers injured by an unexplained discharge when they
  opened a passage. The passage was closed afterward.

The account concerns a **small, local historical event**. It is distinct from
the recent lightning surges affecting the region. Its existence does not
prove that those events share a cause.

The plan and account are public records, not Serene's inherited private
warning. They describe buried work whose extent and purpose are incompletely
understood and a historical injury associated with opening a passage. The
revised emergency sequence does not require repeating the older debate about
a supervised trial cut or assigning every record an on-screen reading.

Those records justify investigation and caution. They do not establish that
every shallow cut is dangerous, explain the discharge's mechanism or prove
that a new quarry would cause a catastrophe. No curse or divine punishment is
confirmed. The exact extent of the buried works remains uncertain.

## Application in MQ03

The [regional surges guide](../game_01/REGIONAL_SURGES.md) owns the damaged
southern road, limited recent lightning incidents and regional relief pressure.
More distant stone remains a real alternative: its longer haul competes for
carts, money and time needed by other repairs. The conflict does not require
every other quarry or route to be unusable.

[MQ03](../game_01/CH01_MQ03.md) owns Iselin's concealed advance deployment,
the survivor's report, Lucan's surprise and the rescue/return sequence. These
are not the supervised joint inspection of the superseded negotiation. The
page's prior knowledge remains unassigned. The extent of work before the attack
is not established by this covenant.

Neither the deployment nor Serene's emergency care grants mining permission or
settles the covenant's legal questions. Rescue addresses the immediate danger;
the Sanctuary's duties to the living and the dead continue. Earlier proposals
for courtyard stone, crew meals and joint inspection are superseded as MQ03's
negotiated outcome, and must not be reported as agreements reached before the
interruption. The observed chronology does not prove that mining caused the
growling or that the bridge incident created the creature; deeper causes remain
open.

## Knowledge and disclosure boundaries

The public plan and injury account are separate from Serene's **inherited
warning**, which she privately consults after the chapter-ending authorization.
The player does not read that warning in full. It must not become a complete
answer that Serene could have disclosed to settle the MQ03 disagreement.

Serene's faith and the public records do not establish the buried works' full
mechanism, the Crown's covert mission or the source of the recent lightning.
Do not reveal a buried machine or a complete causal explanation during MQ03.
The warning's unwritten content is not permission to restore the retired
cosmic or Ancient-race explanation.

The broader mechanism, the old site's full history, precise civil procedures,
and later discoveries remain open or subject to the existing disclosure
boundaries. This approval establishes the local covenant and conflict; it does
not turn unwritten details into public knowledge or an established secret.
