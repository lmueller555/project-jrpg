# Auratia World Atlas

**Status: Approved world name, broad geography, named kingdoms, regional
cultural foundations and opening regional context.**

**Opening revision, 2026-11-26:** The approved Fire Veinhold opening replaces
the flashforward, Festival of Light and quarry/excavation Act I. The
[Chapter 1 chronology](../game_01/CHAPTER_01.md) owns the revised sequence.
This is future narrative direction, not implemented gameplay or dialogue.
The current overworld still starts with a ruined western crossing; the old
arrival and MQ03/MQ04 runtime are removed. This retained physical state and
historical captures do not establish the revised opening chronology. The canonical
world-map artwork remains authoritative for geography, separately from local
playable states.

**Auratia** contains four major continents, a significant central island chain
and a northern circumpolar ocean region.
This atlas places Eldenmere within that wider landscape and gives future
stories places, livelihoods and cultural relationships to draw upon. Exact
borders and distances remain open; illustrative encounters are story seeds,
not committed quests.

Compatible village, character and local-geography foundations remain.
[Foundational Lore](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md) governs the
world's aether, Worldveins and the effects of interrupted circulation. The
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md) governs what characters
and players may learn. The atlas is a writer's reference; a character's own
map or understanding can be narrower.

## The world's name

**Auratia** is the world's name. Its language of origin, etymology,
historical forms and regional pronunciations remain to be developed. The name
does not by itself establish a particular historical origin.

**Authorial naming intent:** Adamanthra evokes adamant endurance, mountain
strength and jewel-like beauty. Carcovia evokes the invasive expansion of
cancer. These are creative associations, not established in-world etymologies,
magical geology, actual disease or aether pathology. They do not define every
inhabitant's character or require residents to recognize the associations.

## Approved world map

**Approved geography revision, 2026-11-25:** The
[canonical Auratia map](../../../assets/world/auratia/world_map.png) establishes
Merovar as a continuous western continent extending from the far north to the
far south of the depicted world. An **unnamed east–west river** crosses its
middle and divides its northern and southern halves geographically; it is a
river within one continent, not an ocean strait. This geographical division
does not assign a political border.

**Elden Vale and Eldenmere lie in far-southern Merovar**, between two unnamed
north–south rivers. They remain temperate and inland. **Adamanthra lies in the
far north**, enclosed by mountains on the west, north and east, with a land
approach from the south. No water channel separates that northern land from
the rest of Merovar. The Rimeward coast lies beyond the northern mountain
enclosure; depicting that coast does not create another entrance into the
kingdom.

The image and this atlas now supersede the earlier continental outline and
Elden Vale's former placement in the upper Leth watershed. The Vale belongs
to a separate southern watershed. Local village orientation, mill operation,
the western crossing's location and the Sanctuary's northeast location remain
unchanged. [Merovar](continents/MEROVAR.md) and
[Elden Vale](regions/ELDEN_VALE.md) own the detailed relationships.

Castle motifs do not assign a capital name or exact coordinates. The capital
of Adamanthra remains unnamed. Distances, travel times, exact latitudes,
political boundaries, the central river's name and detailed tributary junctions
remain open. The picture is not a latitude grid: far-southern Elden Vale does
not acquire a polar climate from its proximity to the frame or Istrava's art.
See the [migration review](../../reviews/merovar_world_map/README.md) for the
approved asset and integration scope; historical captures retain their status.

## The broad arrangement

Merovar, Serevin and Othara surround the **Amber Sea**, a warm central sea with
major connections to the wider oceans. The **Rillan Isles** lie along several
routes across it. Farther south, the **Southern Ocean** separates Othara from
the polar continent **Istrava**. In the north, the **Nivren Crown** includes
the polar ocean and the far northern shores of Merovar and Serevin; it is not
another continent. This arrangement makes contact between societies plausible
while leaving voyages expensive, seasonal and consequential.

| Land | Relative position | Geographic range | Initial story use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Merovar** | Continuous western continent, west of the Amber Sea and extending far south | Temperate valleys, wooded uplands, alpine country, river plains, marshes, southern coasts and a drier eastern interior; colder forests and tundra farther north | Eldenmere and the first game's regional setting |
| **Serevin** | East and northeast of the Amber Sea | Northern continental steppe, large river basins, seasonal monsoon country, forested uplands and maritime lowlands | Carcovia's continental setting; trading connections, visitors and possible later journeys |
| **Othara** | South of the Amber Sea | A wet eastern coast, cool elevated plateaus, savanna transitions and an arid leeward interior | Trading relationships and substantial space for future games |
| **Rillan Isles** | Island chain within the central sea routes | Sheltered harbours, wet volcanic highlands, cultivated slopes, reefs and exposed outer islands | Maritime communities with connections to the three surrounding continents |
| **Nivren Crown** | Northern polar ocean and the far northern margins of Merovar and Serevin | Central pack ice, fjords, tundra, coastal islands and rocky offshore skerries | Coastal households, seasonal herding, cold-water trade and rescue obligations |
| **Istrava** | Southern polar continent across the Southern Ocean from Othara | Interior ice plateau, glacier coasts and a subpolar volcanic island fringe | Small island communities and rotating shore stations; substantial room for later stories |

This is a schematic of relative positions, not a coastline, route or distance map:

The atlas does not settle the planet's total land area, exact distances,
coastline shapes or the existence of additional landmasses beyond those named
here. Large interiors, remote coasts and the detailed extent of polar surveys
remain available for later development. Specific Worldvein routes, Veinhold
sites and the cannon's location have not been assigned on this map. This
includes the opening's Fire Veinhold: its exact position and approach remain
open, and the opening weapon is not yet identified as the later cannon.

## Where Eldenmere belongs

Eldenmere's geographic hierarchy is:

**Auratia → southern Merovar → Elden Vale → Eldenmere.**

Elden Vale occupies a temperate inland valley among the southern outliers of
the **Greyfold Mountains**, between its two bounding rivers. Eldenmere is a
village within it. The existing local map has northern headwater woodland,
terraced ground, a descending
north–south brook, Serene's home on a raised terrace and a working mill. It
remains a brook-side settlement; the name does not establish a lake or coastal location.

At the larger scale, the village brook belongs to the Vale's separate southern
watershed; its junction with the bounding rivers remains unassigned. The
**River Leth**, **Lower Leth** and **Rookfen** lie in central Merovar, north of
the Vale. The Leth joins the unnamed central river. Rookfen comprises wetlands
near that confluence; its exact estuarine extent is open. The old account of
Eldenmere as an upper-Leth settlement with the delta farther south is replaced.

The village's controlled millrace and irrigation supplies remain distinct
from the main brook. The short wheel-feeding branch runs east–west locally.
The blockage in MQ02 obstructs the mill supply; it does not stop the whole
river or watershed. See the approved
[Eldenmere elevation and water contract](../../world/eldenmere/blockout/README.md).

Regent Iselin's established authority is regional. Her
administration's concerns include Elden Vale and neighbouring southern
communities. This does not retain the retired opening quarry dispute.
She is the younger sister of Queen Althea of
Adamanthra. This does not establish Adamanthran sovereignty over the entire
continent. Her precise constitutional powers and exact political boundaries
remain open. The [royal character foundations](../game_01/CHARACTERS.md#iselin--approved-royal-background)
record this approved relationship separately from proposed family details.
A geographic region in the atlas is not automatically an administrative province.

**Elden Vale Sanctuary**, northeast of Eldenmere, remains separate from

## Merovar and the first game's neighbourhood

The Greyfold range creates a useful geographic contrast: moist western
slopes support woodland and pasture, while the eastern Redstone rain-shadow
country becomes drier. Elden Vale occupies sheltered southern outliers rather
than the former upper-Leth position. Elevation provides conifer slopes,
moorland and alpine meadows within a regional journey. The central Leth
riverlands and Rookfen wetlands have different livelihoods from the uplands.

| Region | Landscape | Everyday cultural foundations | A material source of tension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Elden Vale** | Temperate woods, streams, terraces and pasture | Sanctuary care, household crafts, milling and shared maintenance work | Useful improvements can conflict with protected ground and local obligations |
| **Greyfold Uplands** | Conifer slopes, moorland, alpine meadows and mountain passes | Herding, quarrying, pass maintenance and hospitality along difficult routes | Quarry access, grazing rights and maintaining safe passes |
| **Lower Leth** | Fertile river plains and seasonal floodlands | Grain markets, water-sharing agreements, tenant farms and market towns | Upstream and downstream interests diverge over water and flood protection |
| **Rookfen** | Central confluence wetlands and branching channels; exact estuarine extent open | Reed work, fishing, ferry services and homes adapted to wet ground | Draining land creates farms while threatening existing livelihoods |
| **Marris Coast** | Central-eastern cliffs, estuaries and sheltered Amber Sea harbours | Ship repair, salt production, migrant neighbourhoods and merchant institutions | Transit fees, access to waterfront work and the price of imported necessities |
| **Redstone March** | Drier grassland and scrub east of the Greyfold range | Pastoral routes, quarry settlements, market centres and well maintenance | Town boundaries and private holdings can interrupt shared access to water |

Game I's home region remains far-southern Elden Vale. The Fire Veinhold
prologue and first party expedition are owned by Chapter 1; the facility's
exact geographic placement remains open. The longer journey develops the
approved northward direction described below. The regions in
this table offer choices for planning that route; they do not require visits
to every biome, the coast or every central river district. Intermediate
destinations remain open.

Read the continental detail in [Merovar](continents/MEROVAR.md).

## Other continents, the island chain and the poles

**Serevin**, the continent containing Carcovia, gives us permanent river cities, mobile and settled communities
in the northern steppe, upland settlements and busy coastal ports. Its
regions are **Talven Steppe**, **Vashen Basin** and **Namar Coast**.
Seasonal water and long travel routes matter in different ways to farmers,
herders, public officials, artisans and traders. Their interests can conflict
within the same community. See [Serevin](continents/SEREVIN.md).

**Othara** has a strong contrast between the **Velin Raincoast**, the elevated
**Ossan Plateau** and the leeward **Damar Drylands**. Forest and coastal
settlements, plateau towns, farming districts and dryland routes depend upon
one another. Differences in water access, land tenure and transport create
cultural histories worth developing. See [Othara](continents/OTHARA.md).

**Rillan** is an archipelago, not a continent or a single island nation.
The **Harbor Chain**, **Rainward Isles** and **Outer Reaches** have different
relationships with trade and the sea. Port councils, farming communities,
boatbuilding families and lighthouse crews do not benefit equally from the
same shipping routes. See [Rillan Isles](continents/RILLAN_ISLES.md).

**Nivren Crown** encompasses the **Pale Sea**, the **Rimeward Shores** and the
**Skeld Isles**. The North Pole lies over water beneath the Pale Sea's drifting
pack ice. Long-established coastal settlements and mobile herding households
occupy the region's continental margins and islands; their lives include
fishing, boatbuilding, trade, teaching and the maintenance of rescue stores.

**Istrava** contains the **White Expanse**, **Bluewall Coast** and **Cinder
Isles**. The Cinder Isles also lie offshore from southeastern Merovar and
host the opening Fire Veinhold; their approved viewing geography is recorded
in [Polar Regions](POLAR_REGIONS.md#cinder-isles). The South Pole lies on its interior ice plateau. Small island
communities, including families with generations of residence, differ from
the rotating crews at mainland shore stations. Fisheries, imported supplies
and local geothermal sites support limited settlement; the deep interior is
sparsely surveyed. See [Polar Regions](POLAR_REGIONS.md) for both poles.

These areas can first become familiar through people, goods and stories:
an imported tool, a visiting performer, a sailor's household, a healer trained
elsewhere or a disagreement over a familiar religious phrase. An early
encounter need not promise a later visit.

## Connections that make the world feel inhabited

| Connection | Goods and work | People and ideas travelling with them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elden Vale and neighbouring southern valleys | Timber, wool, milled flour, stone, tools and repair work | Sanctuary workers, herders, seasonal labourers and road crews |
| Lower Leth, Rookfen and Marris | Grain, river transport, reeds, fish, salt and coastal shipping | Bargemen, tenant families, apprentices and merchants |
| Merovar and Rillan | Merovar timber, wool and grain; island produce, navigation and ship services | Migrant households, crews, travelling teachers and performers |
| Serevin and the Amber Sea ports | Basin cloth, paper and preserved produce; steppe wool and livestock; imported metals, salt and timber | Clerks, scholars, transport workers, traders and mixed-language families |
| Othara and the island routes | Plateau ores and woven goods, dryland salts, coastal crops and forest products | Metalworkers, water engineers, healers, students and migrant crews |
| Nivren Crown and the northern continental coasts | Fish, boat work and coastal passage knowledge; imported grain, tools, cloth and timber where needed | Fishing households, herders, teachers, traders and rescue crews |
| Othara and Istrava across the Southern Ocean | Fisheries and repair work; imported grain, timber, fuel and equipment | Island families, navigators, healers, surveyors and rotating station crews |

These are broad foundations, not fixed inventories or a complete trade
simulation. Each region needs food producers and household work as well as its
most visible exports. Ports should contain settled families and ordinary
services; upland communities should contain skilled crafts and intellectual
life. Travel permits, tolls, refuge and access to work are possible story
pressures rather than universally identical laws.

## Faith, magicks and cultural variation

[Foundational Lore](../saga/FOUNDATIONAL_LORE.md) owns the planetary cycle,
elemental Worldveins, natural and artificial imbalance, and magick constraints. The
[Revelation Plan](../saga/REVELATION_PLAN.md) distinguishes public understanding
from author truth. [Serene's local faith](ELDEN_VALE_FAITH.md) owns Elden Vale's
spiritual practices and the Sanctuary's service to the dead; the
[covenant](SANCTUARY_COVENANT.md) owns its public custodial terms.

Greater regions across Auratia have their own sanctuaries at natural aetherial
confluences. Their surroundings, architecture, customs and custodial arrangements
can vary with the people and landscape around them. Elden Vale Sanctuary is the
first regional example; Serene's home in Eldenmere is a household, not another
site in this category. This foundation does not assign a sanctuary to every
named atlas region or settle how all confluences behave.

Exploring these sanctuaries and studying their confluences may become a recurring
thread of the main plot. This is a narrative direction for future development,
not a completed itinerary or an explanation of a particular elemental event. A sanctuary's
natural confluence does not establish that it contains a machine controlling
the Worldveins.

Remembrance and care for living neighbours remain part of Elden Vale's
spiritual foundations. The Festival of Light and its preparation story are
retired; they do not remain as off-screen annual canon or a required departure
event. Other places may express their beliefs through gatherings, household
practices or public observances. These practices can
share historical fragments without establishing one identical religion
across the world. The names, hierarchy and relationships of major traditions
remain to be chosen.

The continental documents describe such practices as communal meals, repaired
lamps, remembrance records or household acknowledgements. Their theological
claims remain beliefs. They do not settle souls, reincarnation or personal
consciousness after death. The world's calendar remains open.

Magicks obey the same established conservation limits everywhere. Regional
differences can arise through teaching, tools, occupations, access to training
and institutions. Geography and ancestry do not automatically determine a
person's magical strength. Each region can contain healers, offensive casters,
craft workers, people with modest abilities and people whose work uses little
magicks.

Occasional brief natural imbalances in the Worldveins cause natural disasters;
their damage can outlast the disturbance. Carcovia's **Veinholds**
artificially disrupt that circulation while harvesting aether. Some opponents
call them **"clots"** with disdain. The foundational owner records their
connection to the cannon and Ice Dome; this atlas does not assign them to
particular settlements or make every unusual weather event evidence of a
Veinhold. The revised opening bridge attack has an approved Fire Veinhold
cause; it is not an unresolved lightning incident. The complete military
sequence and its remaining decisions belong to Chapter 1 and the foundational
owner.

## Turning the atlas into scenes

For a region's first substantial appearance, develop a household, a place of
work and a public institution before writing a lore lecture. Give residents
different concerns: a ferry owner and a reed grower can disagree about the
same embankment while both care about their home. Let a traveller's mistaken
expectation meet a specific person rather than a whole population confirming
the stereotype.

The approved [companion foundations](../game_01/CHARACTERS.md) place
Lucan Rooke in the Redstone March, Rhea Sorel on the wooded western Greyfold
slopes and Edran Hale in the Lower Leth. Specific home settlements remain open.
Use the atlas to develop future backgrounds as the characters' needs become
clear. Existing identities and relationships retain their approved facts.

The world name, continental arrangement, Eldenmere's placement and regional
foundations are approved. More precise borders, languages, political systems,
religious institutions, distances and calendar details remain open and can
grow from the stories that need them.
