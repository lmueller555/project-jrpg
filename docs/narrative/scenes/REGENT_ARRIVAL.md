# Retired regent arrival scene

**Status: Removed from current runtime.**

The old relief-caravan arrival cinematic, landscape, supporting cast and controller have been removed. Reusable Iselin and Lucan character art is retained in their character libraries; it does not replay this retired scene. A new royal arrival belongs to the approved future Fire Veinhold opening and is not implemented yet.

The [approved replacement opening](../game_01/CHAPTER_01.md) remains future story direction.
This retirement adds no Fire Veinhold prologue, invasion, new quest or initially
intact bridge. MQ01 and MQ02 remain playable with ordinary household and mill
framing; finishing MQ02 returns to exploration.

Historical review evidence remains in [the retained captures](../../reviews/regent_arrival/README.md).
