## Setting

A huge ice dome covers a ruined, frozen kingdom. The Ice Reaper created it to protect the kingdom against a hostile army. The relationship between the destruction, hostile army and dome's creation remains unspecified. This dome is a regional ice construction, not the Aethersphere.

The Ice Reaper is a woman who has merged with a great amount of ice-aspected aether. She has glassy blue wings, ice-covered parts of her body, and blue, white and silver accents. The approved art's silver-white hair and dark clothing do not establish a royal identity, biography or transformation mechanism.

Caelan, Lucan and Mira rescue Rhea from an ice prison. Rhea then takes control of the confrontation while moving her companions out of its path. Her wind blast and fireballs express her limit-breaking magic without establishing a named player ability or a combat damage formula.

## Continuity and presentation

The scene uses one world-space cast and one periodic environment panorama. Fixed landmarks, actor headings and camera projection determine the scenery and directional sprite views. Camera motion targets 60 FPS; authored character actions contain roughly four to ten drawings each. No limbs or bodies are procedurally stretched to produce a pose.

The original rescue is preserved: the first shard is aimed at Rhea, and Lucan's shield intersects its path. Lucan remains kneeling toward the Reaper with his shield raised after impact. Caelan lands beside Rhea, and Mira remains at the original party position until the wind sequence. The gust moves Caelan and Lucan on separate arcs to distinct landing positions beside Mira.

There is one Rhea sprite throughout. Her awakening uses the original 128×128 full-body scene sprite and original prison, with only a tiny facial mask changing. The rejected separate portrait and second prison are removed. During later camera moves, directional bodies and weapons follow world headings rather than mirroring equipment or swapping views on a timer.

The Reaper's retained approved design remains recognizable from all camera angles. The ice sword grows through authored conjuring poses, and she winds up before accelerating toward the final camera. Near-lens framing must keep her body visible until the fade, avoiding a near-plane disappearance.

The red rune has a fixed 8.2-world-unit footprint. Its six branches, rings and glyphs construct from 17.30–18.60 seconds. Six fireball anchors follow the actual pointed patterns in the authored rune metadata; the orbs rise from the ground instead of appearing at arbitrary screen positions. The 360-degree shot circles the same rune, actors and scenery and returns to its starting camera position.

Reduced Motion uses static compositions for the fast camera moves and orbit, reduces particle intensity, and suppresses flashes and shake. It retains the actions, dialogue, spell locations and final fade.
