# Liam's family — approved directional idles

`front_idles.png` is a native transparent 72×36 sheet: mother, Liam, father.
`family.json` links individual sprites, palettes, source layers and metadata.

All frames are 24×36 with the same `[12,34]` foot anchor. Liam is **8**, with
26 visible pixels of height; both parents are **mid-30s**, with 34 visible pixels.
Mother and Liam share chestnut hair and green eyes. Father has darker brown
hair, a short beard and hazel eyes. All have empty hands and front-facing idles.

Individual assets live in `../liam/`, `../liam_mother/` and `../liam_father/`.
All front, left, right and back idle designs are approved and available in each
member's folder. `idle_directions.png` is a 96×108
sheet with mother/Liam/father rows and front/left/right/back columns;
`idle_directions.json` records approval for all views.

inside its home; portraits, dialogue and routines remain future work.

## Family home

Liam, his mother, his father and Daisy live in the new wide cottage immediately
south of the sanctuary in western Eldenmere. The 16×10 interior has a shared
parents' bed, Liam's bed, four dining seats and clear floor space for Daisy.
The house is distinct from the eastern Venn family cottage. Liam and his parents
remain stationary without dialogue. Daisy rests on her rug, wanders, eats and
drinks; family refill routines remain future work. See the [home layout](../../maps/eldenmere/liam_house/README.md).
