# Floppy cap boy · right walk

Eight complete native pixel drawings, 70 ms each, for two steps in 560 ms.
The anatomical right arm opposes the right leg, and the left arm opposes the
left leg. The far ankle is partly occluded in passing. The leading foot
touches down as the trailing toe releases; recoil/passing/recovery are drawn
between the contacts. Some longer forward swing is retained between passing
and recovery, without a second contact or a frozen support-foot extreme.

The approved face/cap is preserved; coat, arms, folds and legs were drawn
for the pose and same character palette. There is no mirrored second view,
idle limb reuse, joint interpolation or fitting by lowest occupied pixel.
The 48×72 canvas, 2× density, and fixed draw/ground references match the
accepted idle asset. The foot ticks document authored artwork positions,
not inferred runtime contacts or a claim of world-space locking.
