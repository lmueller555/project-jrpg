# Caelen V2 — Approved left-facing art direction

**Approval:** left-facing idle, Contact A, and Contact B visual designs approved by the project owner on 2026-10-19.

This is a **source-art approval record**, not a runtime sprite. The approved reference is a larger rendered illustration, not three native 32×48 RGBA frames. Do not crop/downscale it into production or overwrite `assets/atlases/characters/caelen.png`.

## Design contract

- Facing: **left**, one visible blue eye, protruding nose and chin, readable profile
- Hair: tousled chestnut-brown layers with distinct locks and a small top cowlick
- Clothing: cobalt-blue sanctuary tunic with cream/gold trim, white sleeves, brown belt and buckle, dark trousers
- Boots: two distinct, complete brown boots, readable shaft, heel and toe
- **Idle:** balanced stationary stance, both feet visibly planted
- **Contact A:** leading left-facing foot forward, rear leg extended; nearer arm swings back while far arm comes forward
- **Contact B:** opposing contact pose with leg roles reversed and counter-swinging arms
- Authentic target: independent transparent 32×48 pixel cells with consistent baseline and anatomy
- Only after native inspection: complete two passing poses and assemble the five-frame 160×48 strip

The user-approved large illustrations are reference artwork, not validated native-size graphics. The earlier rejected `caelen_v2_left.png` should not be used.
