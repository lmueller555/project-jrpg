# Sanctuary interior design rationale — proposed for review

The source is the approved opening quest, especially S01–S03 and S07. The player
should see a home before receiving work. Family affection, curiosity, modest
comfort and recurring practical care supply the meaning of these rooms.

## Caelan's bedroom — 10×10

The small family drawing gives visual presence to the approved line about
Tomas drawing Caelan with enormous ears. It is a child's depiction, not a new
character reference sheet. The open notebook, copied loose pages and ink pot
support Caelan's work on local inscriptions and his habit of adding guesses.
Existing shelf books support his unfinished reading. The wardrobe retains the
approved clean-robes inspection. Patched clothes, a low chest, a mending basket
and a repaired rug suggest that someone keeps caring for these ordinary things.
The basin, boots and plant give the room daily habits and a personal scale.

All six existing inspect lines are retained verbatim as object metadata.
The review shows the room after waking, with Caelan standing. The scripted
asleep pose and the knock/bell audio are separate future integration work.

## Shared hall — 12×10

Two benches, a modest reading stand and an accessible bookcase make a place
where villagers can pause. Community notices are ordinary papers, with no
invented requests written on them. Mending materials and the basin connect
reflection to the tangible work of looking after people. The records cupboard
is closed everyday storage; no secret document, ominous marking or revelation
is visible. Herb boxes and worn work boots connect the sanctuary to the gardens
and errands outside. Furnishings frame the room rather than occupying its main
door routes.

The north doors lead to the bedroom and communal kitchen/dining room. A 32px
south threshold corresponds to the exterior paired doors, with its horizontal
position aligned to their proportion along the approved facade.

## Communal kitchen and dining — 16×10

Three breakfast settings make the S02 family relationship visible before any
delivery objective. The three chairs face the table and leave a south approach
for staging. The standing Caelan is a scale reference; Serene and Tomas have
not been assigned generic substitute sprites.

Kitchen storage, a sideboard, bread, washbasin and a quiet steaming kettle suggest
an early start to ordinary work. The reused animated hearth sits at the right,
consistent with the sanctuary exterior's kitchen chimney. Separate preparation
tables hold medicine for Halden, food and oil for Elira, and produce/linens for
the Venn household. These are readable visual proposals for the approved supply
abstraction, not new inventory quantities or mandatory pickup mechanics. They
preserve Halden as the first visit and the subsequent flexible household order.

The maps are separate expanded interiors, following the existing approach to
cottage rooms. The comparison board aligns them at one scale; its three Caelan
sprites belong to alternative room snapshots. Door links include reachable
approach and landing points. The hall's courtyard link references the approved
exterior entrance at foot (386,304).

A 24×12 planning probe reaches every furnishing approach, story object and
portal. Tabletop props inherit their supporting furniture's footprint rather
than blocking the floor separately. Rugs remain walkable. Source assets retain
their native scale. Soft changes in window light, restrained fire and small

The opening remains warm and peaceful. No new religious symbol, divine claim,

introduced. Story files remain unchanged; room dressing and new props await
